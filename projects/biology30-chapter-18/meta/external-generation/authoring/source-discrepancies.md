# Chapter 18: scientific and source reconciliation

These are developer records, not student-facing lesson commentary.

Griffith demonstrated transformation, not the chemical identity of DNA. Hershey–Chase evidence is described with the relevant DNA/protein labels. Both new strands grow 5′ to 3′. RNA polymerase reads the template strand; the coding strand matches the RNA sequence apart from T/U. Every authored sequence calculation supplies identity and direction. Introns are removed from RNA, not deleted from genomic DNA. CAG and CAA code for glutamine, not glutamate; stop codons add no amino acid. Not every transcription/translation error is a heritable DNA mutation. Bacterial expression of a human protein requires a compatible coding sequence rather than assuming ordinary bacteria splice human introns. Gel profile matches are limited evidence, not automatic proof of identity or guilt. External smoking research and an unperformed cloning simulation are not assigned as original data tasks.
