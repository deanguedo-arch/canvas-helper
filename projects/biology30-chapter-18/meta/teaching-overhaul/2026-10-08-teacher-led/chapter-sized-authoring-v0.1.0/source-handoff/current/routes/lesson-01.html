Evidence that DNA carries genetic information

What made DNA a stronger explanation of heredity than protein?

Before you begin

Genes are inherited units. Identifying the molecule that carries them requires evidence, not just finding a molecule inside a cell.

Griffith established a transferable change

Griffith studied bacteria with different disease-causing properties. Live non-virulent bacteria could acquire a heritable virulent trait when mixed with heat-killed virulent bacteria. Material from the killed cells had changed the living bacterial lineage.

This result supported . It did not identify the transforming substance. DNA and protein were among the candidates, so the next question was which component was necessary for the change.

Remove a candidate and test the effect

Avery, MacLeod and McCarty studied transforming preparations and used treatments aimed at different components. When DNA was destroyed, transforming activity was lost. Comparable treatments directed at other components did not remove activity in the same way.

This was stronger evidence for DNA because the experiment connected an intervention with the loss of a measured function. The useful comparison is not simply that DNA was present. It is that destroying DNA disrupted transformation under the experimental conditions.

Trace the material entering infected cells

Hershey and Chase used bacteriophages, viruses that infect bacteria. In separate preparations, phosphorus-32 labelled viral DNA and sulfur-35 labelled viral protein. After infection, they separated much of the external phage material from the bacterial cells.

The DNA label was associated much more strongly with the infected cells than the external protein label. This supported DNA as the material entering the cells and directing viral production in that system.

Together the experiments built a case for DNA as . They did not by themselves explain its double-helical structure or every step of gene expression. Match each scientist with the specific question their evidence addressed.

Worked example

Separate observation from conclusion

A transforming preparation stops transforming bacteria after a DNA-destroying enzyme is added; other enzyme treatments retain activity.

  1. Identify the measured outcome: transformation of the bacterial lineage.
  2. Identify the intervention: destroying DNA in the preparation.
  3. Compare with other treatments so that enzyme addition alone is not the explanation.
  4. Conclude that intact DNA is necessary for transforming activity in this assay.
  5. Do not claim that this experiment determined the sequence of the entire bacterial genome.
