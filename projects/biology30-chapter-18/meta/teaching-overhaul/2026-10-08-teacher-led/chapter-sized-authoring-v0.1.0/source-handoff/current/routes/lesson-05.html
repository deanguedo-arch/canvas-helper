Read the template, not the label you remember

binds at a gene’s regulatory starting region and opens a short DNA region. It reads the in the 3′ to 5′ direction while building complementary RNA 5′ to 3′. Only one DNA strand serves as the template for a particular transcript.

A on the DNA template pairs with U in RNA; T pairs with A; C pairs with G; G pairs with C. The other DNA strand is called the because its 5′ to 3′ sequence matches the RNA sequence except that DNA has T and RNA has U.

For example, template 3′-TAC GGA-5′ produces 5′-AUG CCU-3′. Its coding DNA partner is 5′-ATG CCT-3′. Identify which strand you have before deciding whether to complement or simply replace T with U.

An RNA message is not a new DNA helix

Transcription copies the information of a selected DNA region into RNA. The DNA strands close behind the enzyme and the RNA separates. It does not copy the entire genome in the same way as S-phase replication.

Because one continuous RNA molecule is synthesized from one template, transcription does not require a leading strand, lagging strand or Okazaki fragments. Those terms belong to DNA replication at a fork.

Process the eukaryotic transcript

A newly made eukaryotic pre-mRNA is commonly processed before export. Introns are removed, and exons are joined. A cap and a poly-A tail help protect the message and support its use by the cell.

Introns are removed from the RNA, not normally cut out of the genomic DNA during this processing step. Exons are the portions retained in the mature RNA; some retained sequence is not translated.

Once processing is complete, the mature message can leave the nucleus. Keep transcription, RNA processing and translation as distinct steps even though they contribute to one overall gene-expression pathway.

Worked example

Choose the correct conversion

You are given coding DNA 5′-ATG TTT TAA-3′. Find the corresponding mRNA.

  1. Identify the supplied strand as coding DNA, already written 5′ to 3′.
  2. Keep its base order and replace T with U.
  3. The mRNA is 5′-AUG UUU UAA-3′.
  4. Check against the template: 3′-TAC AAA ATT-5′ would give the same mRNA by complementary pairing.
