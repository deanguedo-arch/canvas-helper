# Chapter 19: scientific and source reconciliation

These are developer records, not student-facing lesson commentary.

Direct allele counts use 2N at a diploid autosomal locus and do not require equilibrium. Inferring allele frequencies from recessive phenotype proportions does require stated assumptions. Non-random mating can change genotype proportions without necessarily changing allele frequencies. Unchanged sampled frequencies do not prove that all equilibrium assumptions hold. Drift is sampling, whereas selection involves reproductive differences associated with heritable variation. Gene flow is weighted by contributions. Ancestry is not used to define Indigenous identity. The class phenotype survey on p. 684 is excluded, as are duplicate full-page “tasks” that were ordinary reading.
