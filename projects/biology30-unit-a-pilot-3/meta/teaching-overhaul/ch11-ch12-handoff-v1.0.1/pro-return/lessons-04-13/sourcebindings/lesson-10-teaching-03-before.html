The cerebrum and cerebral cortex

The cerebrum is the largest part of the brain. It has left and right cerebral hemispheres. The outer cerebral cortex is grey matter. Its folds increase the surface area that fits inside the skull.

The cerebrum is part of the forebrain. The thalamus and are also part of the forebrain.

The frontal lobe is involved in thought, decisions, memory, personality and voluntary movement.

The parietal lobe processes touch and body-position information.

The temporal lobe processes hearing and contributes to language and memory.

The occipital lobe processes vision.
