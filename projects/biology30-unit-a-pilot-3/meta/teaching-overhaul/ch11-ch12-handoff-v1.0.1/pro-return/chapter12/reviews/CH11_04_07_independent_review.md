## Lesson 4

The two-electrode inside-minus-outside reference is explicit. The −70 to−60 worked comparison correctly distinguishes depolarization from becoming positive. Concentration, ion movement and voltage are separated; higher K⁺ inside and Na⁺ outside, relative leak permeability, electrical attraction and retained large anions form a causal explanation rather than a list. The pump's ongoing ATP-dependent maintenance is distinct from channels and an immediate voltage reset.

The guided −65 to−80 task changes the direction of the voltage shift and asks for an evidence limit. Its −15mV calculation is correct. The stopped-pump transfer changes the mechanism and time scale, retains initial gradients/open channels and declines to invent an exact final voltage or timing. Both tasks supply complete inputs and explanatory recovery. The pump-arrow sentence is the only verified image-reading repair.

## Lesson 5

The lesson first follows one membrane region in time, then explains propagation in space. Threshold at−55mV is not mistaken for the positive peak. Figure7's actual approximately+30mV peak is used rather than forcing a different source's number. The old required “when an action potential begins” polarity ambiguity remains a separately recorded assessment decision.

## Lesson 6

The arrival→Ca²⁺ entry→vesicle fusion→release→diffusion→binding→postsynaptic effect sequence is complete. The release-side and receiving-side cases locate different failures with evidence. Calcium is not confused with the cleft messenger. ACh and cholinesterase have distinct transmitter/termination roles, and effects are qualified by target/receptor.

The lesson handles the current Figure10's unequal local baselines and dashed-reference ambiguity explicitly before using its own numerical model. Summation inputs are defined as effective contributions at the initiating region at one moment; direct arithmetic is clearly a simplified model. Spatial versus temporal summation differs by location versus overlap, not by changed names alone.

All arithmetic is correct: −70+15=−55; with inhibition−70+12=−58. Temporal overlap yields−54 compared with isolated−62. The changed-threshold transfer yields−56 with inhibition and−52 without it against−53 threshold, correctly illustrating removal of opposition to firing. The models do not change threshold when they change input voltage.
