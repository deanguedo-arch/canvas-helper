# Chapter 20: scientific and source reconciliation

These are developer records, not student-facing lesson commentary.

Births and immigration are gains; deaths and emigration are losses. Net change is distinct from final population size. Growth rate divides by elapsed time; interval per-capita change divides by initial N. The beaver example consistently gives final N = 253, not 251. Producers transform rather than create energy. Carrying capacity depends on conditions, r/K traits are a continuum, and survivorship is not population growth through time. Predator–prey correlation does not uniquely prove causation. Succession is not an inevitable march to one permanently fixed endpoint. Original population tables retain their historical years; no old count is presented as current.
