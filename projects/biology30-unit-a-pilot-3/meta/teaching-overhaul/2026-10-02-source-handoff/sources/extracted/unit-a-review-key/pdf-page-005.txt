Measuring the Membrane Potential of a Spinal Neuron 
A microelectrode can be inserted into the axon of a neuron in order to measure the  
differences in charge between the outside and inside of the cell. A specialized, sensitive  
voltmeter is used to measure this difference. Electrode 1 is placed on the outside of the cell 
membrane and Electrode 2 is placed on the inside of the cell membrane.
