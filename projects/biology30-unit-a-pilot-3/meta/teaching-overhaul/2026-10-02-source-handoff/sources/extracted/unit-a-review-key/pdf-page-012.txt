Bovine somatotropin (BST) is a growth hormone that has been produced using biotechnology  
since 1970. BST increases milk production by 10% to 20% when injected into milkproducing  
cows. BST increases nutrient absorption from the bloodstream into the cow’s  
mammary gland.
