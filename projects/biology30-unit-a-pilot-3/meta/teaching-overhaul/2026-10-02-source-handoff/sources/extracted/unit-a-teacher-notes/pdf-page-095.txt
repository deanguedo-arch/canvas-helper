Extra: Hearing Loss
Hearing loss may be caused by a number of factors, including:
 ➢ Birth defects 
Improper development of any of the organs required for 
hearing can lead to deafness; often hereditary, but can 
sometimes be treated with cochlear implants
➢ Ear infections
Swelling of the middle ear can prevent sound waves from 
reaching the cochlea; in some cases, structures of the 
middle ear can become permanently damaged
➢ Noise
Loud noises can damage hair cells within the cochlea or 
even cause the tympanic membrane to rupture
