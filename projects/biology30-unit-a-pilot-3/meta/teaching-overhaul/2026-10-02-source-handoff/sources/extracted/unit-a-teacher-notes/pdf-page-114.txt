Anterior Pituitary Hormones
Prolactin (PRO)
○ Prolactin stimulates the production of milk (lactation) in the mammary glands
Follicle Stimulating Hormone (FSH)
○ Stimulates the production of sperm in the testes and egg in the ovaries (more on this in Unit B)
Luteinizing Hormone (LH)
○ Stimulates sex hormone production in the ovaries and testes
○ Stimulates ovulation (more on this in Unit B)
