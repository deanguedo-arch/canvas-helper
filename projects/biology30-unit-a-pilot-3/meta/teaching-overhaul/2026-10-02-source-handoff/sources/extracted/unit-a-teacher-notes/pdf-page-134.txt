Adrenal Cortex & Adrenal Medulla
The adrenal gland is located on the top of the kidneys, and is divided 
into two regions: the outer cortex and the middle medulla. 
o Adrenal medulla produces epinephrine and norepinephrine, involved in the 
short-term stress response
o Adrenal cortex produces glucocorticoids (e.g. cortisol) and mineralocorticoids 
(e.g. aldosterone), involved in the long-term stress response
