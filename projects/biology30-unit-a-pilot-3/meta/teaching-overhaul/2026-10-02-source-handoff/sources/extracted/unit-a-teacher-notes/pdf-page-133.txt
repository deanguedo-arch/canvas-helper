TYPE 1 DIABETES MELLITUS
o Diagnosed in childhood
o Immune system produces antibodies that attack 
and destroy the beta cells of the pancreas
o Leads to decreased insulin production
o Treated with daily insulin injections
TYPE 2 DIABETES MELLITUS
o Develops over time
o Cell receptors stop responding well to insulin
o Poor diet and lack of exercise can be contributing 
factors
o Treated with either insulin injections before meals 
OR diet, exercise and sulfonamides
