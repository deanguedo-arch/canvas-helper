Anatomy of the Outer/Middle Ear
➢ Sound waves are directed by pinna (auricle) 
to the auditory canal, where they strike the 
tympanum (eardrum), generating vibrations 
within the middle ear.
➢ When sound waves push against the 
tympanum, its vibrations are passed on and 
ampliﬁed by a set of three small connected 
bones, called the ossicles 
➢ Vibrations are concentrated on the oval 
window, causing changes in pressure within 
the organs of the inner ear
