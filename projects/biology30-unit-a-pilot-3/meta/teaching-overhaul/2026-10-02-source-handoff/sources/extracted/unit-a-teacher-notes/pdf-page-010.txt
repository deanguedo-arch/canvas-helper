➢ Schwann Cells, a type of glial cell, are 
responsible for producing the myelin 
that surrounds each axon.
➢ In myelinated axons, gaps between 
Schwann cells are referred to as the 
nodes of Ranvier. Electrical impulses 
“jump” from node to node, which is 
referred to as saltatory conduction. 
This speeds up transmission, avoiding 
having to travel down the entire 
length of the axon.
➢ Once the electrical signal reaches the 
axon terminal, it is then passed on to 
the dendrites of an adjoining neuron.
○ Synapse: the space/junction 
between two adjoining neurons
