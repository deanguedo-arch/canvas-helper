Extra: Levels of Paralysis
➢ Paraplegia: If the injury occurs below the ﬁrst thoracic 
vertebrae in the upper back region, the paralysis is 
likely to be conﬁned to the legs. A paraplegic patient 
will retain full use of their arms and hands.
➢ Quadriplegia: If the injury occurs above the ﬁrst 
thoracic vertebrae in the cervical vertebrae or neck 
region of the body, the paralysis generally results in 
loss of movement in all limbs. Abdominal and chest 
muscles may also be aﬀected, resulting in weakened 
breathing and the inability to properly cough to clear 
the chest. 
A spinal cord injury usually results in the area of 
the body below the injury site being partially or 
completely unable to communicate with the brain. 
Paraplegic patients are 
not necessarily 
conﬁned to a 
wheelchair; modern 
prosthetics and stem 
cell treatment can 
provide an increased 
level of mobility
