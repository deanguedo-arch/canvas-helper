1) Resting State 
The diﬀerence in charge across the membrane of a resting neuron is 
called resting membrane potential. 
 ➢ The inside of a neuron has a slight 
negative charge at rest, whereas the 
outside has a slight positive charge.
➢ This results in a resting potential of 
-70 mV in most neurons 
○ Note: we often use speciﬁc numbers for 
voltages in our examples, but these can 
change from one neuron to another
