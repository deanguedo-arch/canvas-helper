Sensory Adaptation
➢ Do you constantly feel your clothes against your skin? Sometimes the brain can 
actually ﬁlter out redundant sensory information so as to prevent 
over-stimulation. This is known as sensory adaptation.
 
➢ Another example is when you walk into a coﬀee shop and smell the fresh smell 
of coﬀee, but after a while you are not aware of it. Or when you no longer 
hear/notice a background noise. 
➢ When your sensory receptors detect a 
signiﬁcant change in external or internal 
conditions, the body readjusts.
