Hormones of the Thyroid and 
Parathyroid Glands
     Calcitonin
o Released by the thyroid gland as a response 
to high blood calcium (Ca2+) levels
o To reduce blood calcium levels, calcitonin 
○ stimulates uptake of Ca2+  into bones
○ reduces reabsorption of Ca2+ in kidneys 
     Parathyroid Hormone (PTH)
o Released by the parathyroid gland as a 
response to low blood calcium (Ca2+) 
levels
o To increase blood Ca2+ levels,  PTH
○ stimulates release of Ca2+  from bones
○ increases reabsorption of Ca2+ in 
kidneys
