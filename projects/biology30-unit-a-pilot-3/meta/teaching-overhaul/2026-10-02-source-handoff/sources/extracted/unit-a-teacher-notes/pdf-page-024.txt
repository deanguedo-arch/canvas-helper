2) Depolarization 
Action potential occurs when a neuron is stimulated by an electrical impulse.
1. If a strong enough impulse is received and the threshold 
potential is reached (approx. -55 mV), sodium gates will 
open, thus allowing Na+ to diﬀuse freely across the 
membrane.
2. Na+ rushes into the cell (down their concentration 
gradient → facilitated diﬀusion), leading to a slight 
positive charge on the inside relative to the outside.
3. This reverses the membrane potential from -70 mV to 
+40 mV; the membrane is now said to be depolarized. 
4. Once Na+ reaches equilibrium, the membrane once 
again becomes impermeable to them, and the gates 
close.
