Olfaction/Smell (Chemoreception)
Humans can distinguish between over 10,000 diﬀerent odours. Each of these odour 
particles is thought to ﬁt like a lock & key into speciﬁc chemoreceptors which line the 
nasal cavity, known as olfactory cells. 
➢ When stimulated, olfactory cells transmit signals 
to the olfactory bulb in the cerebrum of  the 
brain, leading to the sensation of smell
➢ The olfactory bulb of dogs is more than twice the 
size of humans, explaining their heightened sense 
of smell
