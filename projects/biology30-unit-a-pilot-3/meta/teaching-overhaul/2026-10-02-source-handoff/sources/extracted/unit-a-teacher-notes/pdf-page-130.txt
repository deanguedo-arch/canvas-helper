Hormones of 
the Pancreas
     Insulin
o Released by the beta cells of the pancreas
o Released after a meal when blood sugar 
levels are high
o Makes cells more permeable to glucose, and 
therefore causes cells to take in glucose → 
this in turn reduces blood sugar levels to 
the normal range
o Promotes storage of glucose as glycogen 
in the liver
     Glucagon
o Released by the alpha cells of the 
pancreas
o Released when blood sugar levels are low
o Promotes the breakdown of glycogen in 
the liver to re-release glucose into the 
bloodstream → this in turn increases 
blood sugar levels to the normal range
Another antagonistic hormone pair!
