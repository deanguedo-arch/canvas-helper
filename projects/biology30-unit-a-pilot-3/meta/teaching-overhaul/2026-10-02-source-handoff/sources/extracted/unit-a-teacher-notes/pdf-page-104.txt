Feedback Loop Examples
Negative Feedback:
● Blood sugar: After eating, blood sugar levels rise, triggering the release of 
insulin from the pancreas. Insulin stimulates cells to become permeable to 
sugar, causing blood sugar levels to fall, and insulin production to cease.) 
Positive Feedback:
● Childbirth: As the baby moves into position and presses on the cervix, it 
sends a signal to increase the rate of contractions, which further pushes 
the baby against the cervix which in turn increases contractions even 
further.
