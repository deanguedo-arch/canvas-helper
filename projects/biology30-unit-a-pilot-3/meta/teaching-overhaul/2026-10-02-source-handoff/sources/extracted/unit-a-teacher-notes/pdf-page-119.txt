Posterior Pituitary Hormones
     Oxytocin (OCT)
○ Stimulates uterine contractions during childbirth in a positive feedback loop 
○ Releases milk from the mammary glands 
○ Note: Prolactin allows mammals to produce milk, but oxytocin is required to release to milk
○ In other words, oxytocin is the hormone that helps ‘squeeze things out of the body’
