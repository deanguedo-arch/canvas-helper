Neurotransmitter Release 
1. An action potential reaches the axon terminal
2. Calcium channel “gates” within the axon terminal 
open, causing calcium ions to ﬂow into the cell 
3. Calcium ions trigger the movement and fusing of 
the vesicles with the presynaptic membrane 
(exocytosis). In turn, neurotransmitters are 
released into the synapse.
4. Neurotransmitters diﬀuse across the synaptic cleft 
to the post-synaptic membrane
Neurotransmitters are required to help transmit the electrical impulse from one 
neuron to another. These are stored in vesicles in the axon bulb.
