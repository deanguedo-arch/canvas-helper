Antidiuretic hormone (ADH)
o also known as vasopressin
o Released when dehydrated and 
regulates water reabsorption by 
kidneys 
o Making the collecting ducts of the 
nephrons more permeable to water 
and thus increases the reabsorption of 
water into the blood → this in turn 
makes less, more concentrated urine
