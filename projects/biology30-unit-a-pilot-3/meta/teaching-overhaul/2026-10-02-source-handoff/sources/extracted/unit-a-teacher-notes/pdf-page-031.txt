Signal Transduction across a Synapse (Chemical 
Event)
➢ Once the impulse has traveled down the length 
of the axon, it reaches the axon terminal.
➢ The axon terminal is in close contact with the 
dendrites of another neuron.
➢ For the signal to move to the next neuron, it 
must cross the space between the axon 
terminal and the dendrites of the subsequent 
cell; this space is referred to as the synapse.
