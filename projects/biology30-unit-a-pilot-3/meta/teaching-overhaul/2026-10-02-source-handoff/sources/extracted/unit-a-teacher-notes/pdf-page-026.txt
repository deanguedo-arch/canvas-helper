3) Repolarization 
Once the action potential has peaked, Na+ gates close, and K+ gates open.
➢ This restores the positive charge on the outside of the 
membrane; however, Na+ and K+ concentrations are 
brieﬂy reversed compared to at the resting state (Na+ is 
higher inside the membrane)
➢ The K+ channels are slow to close, which results in the 
hyperpolarization of the neuron. This means that the 
inside becomes slightly more negative than the resting 
potential.
