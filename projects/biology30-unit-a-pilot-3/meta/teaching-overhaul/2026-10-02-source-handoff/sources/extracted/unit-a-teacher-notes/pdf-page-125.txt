Consider the following situations and make predictions:
1. A patient complains of persistent fatigue, feeling cold and weight gain. Their 
doctors conﬁrms via blood test that they have hypothyroidism. What would their 
thyroxine and TSH levels be on a blood test?
Low thyroxine, but high TSH. Patient is not at homeostasis, and since their 
thyroxine levels are low, there is a constant release of TSH trying to stimulate the 
thyroid gland to produce more thyroxine.
