Two types of hormones, classified by the way 
they interact with target cells:
○ Water soluble hormones cannot cross the lipid 
membrane, and thus interact with cells by 
binding to external receptor sites (e.g. hGH, 
epinephrine)
○ Fat soluble hormones are able to diﬀuse 
across the lipid membrane, and thus interact 
with cells by binding to receptor sites within 
the cytoplasm (e.g. sex hormones)
