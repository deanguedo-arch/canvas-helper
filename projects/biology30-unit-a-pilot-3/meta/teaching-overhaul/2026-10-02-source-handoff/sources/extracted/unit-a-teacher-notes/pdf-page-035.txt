2. Inhibitory (e.g. GABA)
● Trigger potassium channels to open, causing potassium ions 
to ﬂow out and thus lowering the membrane potential.
● This leads to hyperpolarization, making it more diﬃcult to 
generate an action potential (because the threshold level has 
been increased)
● Problems with GABA are often associated with epilepsy and 
Huntington’s disease
