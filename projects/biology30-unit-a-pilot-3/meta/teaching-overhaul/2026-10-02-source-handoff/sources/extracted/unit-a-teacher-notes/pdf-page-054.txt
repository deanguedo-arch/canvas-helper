The Brain
The brain is the center for intelligence, consciousness, and emotion. It also 
regulates bodily functions without conscious thought (e.g. breathing). 
● Enclosed within the skull, the brain is 
surrounded by a tough three-layer membrane 
known as the meninges.
● Cerebrospinal ﬂuid circulates between the 
meninges and the spinal cord, acting as a shock 
absorber and a transport medium, carrying 
nutrients & waste to and from brain cells.
