Anatomy of the Eye & Vision
➢ Light enters the eye through the cornea, which is the clear 
part of the sclera in front of the eye. 
➢ The iris, is the colored part of the eye and is a muscle that 
contracts and relaxes to control how much light actually 
enters the eye through the pupil, the hole in the iris. 
➢ Ciliary muscles hold the lens in place and control its 
shape, allowing it to focus on objects closer or further 
away and focus the incoming light onto the retina. (this 
process is called accommodation)
➢ The focused light strikes the retina, which is the inner 
layer of the back of the eye. The retina is composed of 
rods (sensitive to light intensity) and cones (sensitive to 
wavelengths of colour)
➢ Photoreceptors (rods and cones) relay sensory impulses to 
the occipital lobe of the brain via the optic nerve
