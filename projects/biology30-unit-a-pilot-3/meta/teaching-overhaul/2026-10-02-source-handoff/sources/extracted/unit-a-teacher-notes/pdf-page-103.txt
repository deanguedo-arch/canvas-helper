Feedback Loops
Negative Feedback:
● returns a system to homeostasis
● inhibits or counteracts the stimulus 
that sets off the response
Positive Feedback:
● takes the body further from 
homeostasis
● exaggerates or increases the 
stimulus that sets off the response
The endocrine system responds to changes in the internal 
environment through the use of positive or negative feedback.
