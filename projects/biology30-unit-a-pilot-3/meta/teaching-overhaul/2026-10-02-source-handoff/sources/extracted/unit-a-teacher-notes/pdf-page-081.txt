Extra: Conditions Aﬀecting the Shape of the 
Eye
➢ Myopia (nearsightedness)
● the inability to see objects at a distance
● eyeball is slightly elongated, so focused 
light falls in front of the retina
● nearsighted people can wear concave 
lenses to help focus light directly on the 
retina
➢ Hyperopia (farsightedness)
● the inability to see objects at close range
● eyeball is slightly shortened, so focused 
light falls behind the retina
● farsighted people can wear convex lenses 
to help focus light directly on the retina
