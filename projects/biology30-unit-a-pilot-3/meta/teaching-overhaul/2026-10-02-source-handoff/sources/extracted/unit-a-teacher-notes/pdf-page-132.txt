Diabetes Mellitus
…
 The inability to produce enough insulin or respond appropriately to 
insulin
○ Causes blood glucose levels to rise sharply after 
meals (hyperglycemia)
○ Cells cannot properly obtain the glucose they 
require for metabolism; individuals become fatigued 
○ Body compensates by metabolising fats and proteins 
instead of glucose. These are more diﬃcult to break 
down, and release ketones, a toxic by-product.
○ Excess glucose in the bloodstream is excreted 
through urine; high amounts of urine are produced 
due to changes in osmotic gradient (can lead to 
frequent thirst)
