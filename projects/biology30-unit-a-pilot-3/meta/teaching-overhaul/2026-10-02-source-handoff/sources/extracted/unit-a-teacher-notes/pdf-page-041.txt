Action of Drugs at 
the Synapse
Mechanisms of stimulants
1) Drug mimics the neurotransmitter & 
stimulates the receptor.
2) Drug decreases the rate of 
neurotransmitter breakdown or 
re-uptake, leading to continued 
stimulation of the receptor.
3) Drug increases the rate of 
neurotransmitter release from the 
presynaptic neuron.
