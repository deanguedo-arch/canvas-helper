Diabetes Insipidus
…
The inability to produce, store, or properly respond to ADH 
○ Affected individuals produce 
large volumes of urine, 
resulting in the loss of ions 
from the blood
○ Continuous risk of 
dehydration
○ Treated by administering ADH
*Note: Diabetes Insipidus has nothing to do with Diabetes Mellitus (more later). They simply share the 
name due to similarities in symptoms.
