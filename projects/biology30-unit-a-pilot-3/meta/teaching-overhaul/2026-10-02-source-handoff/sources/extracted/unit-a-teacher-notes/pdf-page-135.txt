Hormones…
Cortisol: reduces pain, increases 
inflammation, increases energy availability 
by decreasing glucose storage, and by 
breaking down proteins
Aldosterone: Increases reabsorption of 
Na+ in the kidneys, which in turn increases 
water reabsorption leading to increased 
blood volume and pressure
Epinephrine and 
norepinephrine: initiate the 
fight-or-flight response (increase 
HR, increase breathing rate, etc.)
