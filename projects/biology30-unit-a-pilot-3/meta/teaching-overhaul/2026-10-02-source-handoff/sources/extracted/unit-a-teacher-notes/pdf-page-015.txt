T ypes of Neurons
Three main types of neurons work together to signal eﬀectors (e.g. muscle 
cells) to respond appropriately to diﬀerent stimuli:
 1. Sensory Neurons (aﬀerent)
● Gather information from sensory receptors (i.e. touch/sight/sound/taste receptors) 
and transmit these impulses to the brain & spinal cord
2. Interneurons
● Found in the Central Nervous System (brain and spinal cord)
● Process and integrate incoming sensory info from sensory neurons and relay outgoing 
information to motor neurons
3. Motor Neurons (eﬀerent)
● Transmit information to eﬀectors, such as muscles and glands
