Neurotransmitter Binding
5. Once neurotransmitters have reached the 
post-synaptic membrane, they bind with 
receptors embedded in the membrane 
6. If the neurotransmitter is excitatory, binding 
induces an action potential in the postsynaptic 
neuron
7. Neurotransmitters are then released by the 
receptors and either return to the presynaptic 
neuron or are broken down by enzymes such as 
cholinesterase on the post-synaptic membrane
