Extra: Balance Disorders
Balance disorders commonly result from damage to the semicircular canals, including...
➢ leakage of inner ear ﬂuid after head 
injury or dramatic changes in air 
pressure, causing dizziness and nausea
➢ failure of canals to ﬂex properly in 
response to a change in head position, 
causing dizziness (vertigo) 
➢ an increase in ﬂuid volume, leading to 
a ringing or buzzing and a feeling of 
fullness in the ear
