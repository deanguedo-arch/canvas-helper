Sensation
Sensation occurs when neural impulses generated by the stimulation of 
sensory receptors arrive at the cerebral cortex. Perception occurs in the 
brain as it makes sense of the received input.  
Example:
When a ray of sunlight hits your skin, 
sensory receptors send a message to the 
brain, which receives and processes this 
information, causing you to feel warmth.
* each person’s unique perception of sensation 
  depends on how their cerebral cortex 
interprets 
  this sensory information
