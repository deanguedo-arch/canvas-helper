Anatomy of the Eye & Vision
➢ The aqueous humour supplies the cornea with 
nutrients, while the vitreous humour maintains 
the shape of the eye and permits light 
transmission to the retina.
➢ The sclera protects the eye and helps to maintain 
its shape. 
➢ The choroid layer contains blood vessels which 
nourish the retina.
➢ The fovea centralis is a location in the retina that 
contains a high concentration of cones, while the 
blind spot (the point at which the optic nerve 
connects to the retina) contains neither rods nor 
cones. This is why when light falls on the blind 
spot, no image is generated.
