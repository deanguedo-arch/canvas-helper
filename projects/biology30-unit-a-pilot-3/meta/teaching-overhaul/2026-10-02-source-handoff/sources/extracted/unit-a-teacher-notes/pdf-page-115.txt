Anterior Pituitary Hormones
These last two anterior pituitary hormones are tropic hormones, which means they act on another endocrine gland. We will look at these in more detail as we look at their target glands. 
Thyroid Stimulating Hormone (TSH)
○ Stimulates the thyroid gland to release thyroxine when thyroxine levels are low
Adrenocorticotropic Hormone (ACTH)
○ Stimulates the adrenal cortex to release cortisol and aldosterone
