Na+/K+ exchange pump helps reset the correct 
distribution of ions
➢ Even though during repolarization the 
inside becomes more negative again 
compared to the outside, we do not have 
the correct distribution of Na+ and K+ 
across the membrane. The 
sodium-potassium exchange pump helps 
reset the original distribution by pumping 3 
Na+ out for every 2 K+ it pumps in.
