Schwann cells and Myelin Sheath
 Schwann cells (white 
matter) 
 Surround the axon
 Function – speed up nerve 
impulse
 Group of Schwann cells 
makes up myelin sheath 
around the axon
 Gaps between Schwann cells 
are called nodes of Ranvier
 Nerve impulse jumps 
between nodes – called 
saltatorial conduction
 Myelination and increased 
diameter speed up nerve 
impulse
