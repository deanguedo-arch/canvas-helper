Pancreas
 hormones of the pancreas act antagonistically to 
maintain blood glucose:
 What is the function of insulin?
 Decrease blood glucose levels by:
 Increasing permeability of cells to glucose → increased uptake of 
glucose into cells from blood
 What is the function of glucagon?
 Increase blood glucose levels by:
 Stimulating liver to break down glycogen to release glucose into 
blood
 Activating gluconeogenesis to convert amino acids into glucose
 Breaking down fats into fatty acids to be used as fuel
