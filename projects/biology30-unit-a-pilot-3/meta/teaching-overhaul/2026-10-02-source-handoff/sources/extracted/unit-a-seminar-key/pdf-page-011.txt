Action Potentials
 Definition: the change in electrical potential associated with 
the passage of an impulse along the membrane of a muscle 
cell or nerve cell
 Steps:
 Stimulus received (must pass threshold potential)
 Depolarization: sodium  gates open, sodium flows into neuron 
(makes inside positive)
 Sodium gates close and potassium open
 Repolarization: potassium flows out of neuron, makes it 
negative again
 Hyperpolarization: repolarization step overshoots resting 
potential, important to make sure impulse moves in one 
direction
 Rest potential brought back by sodium-potassium pump (uses 
ATP)
