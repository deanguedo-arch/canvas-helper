Thyroid
 Roles: 
 Metabolism
 Stimulated by TSH (from ant. pit.)
 Releases T3, T4 (thyroxine), both increase 
metabolism
 Use up blood sugar, burn fat, increase temp
 Calcium levels
 Releases calcitonin (when blood Ca2+ levels 
high)
 Parathyroid releases PTH when blood 
calcium levels are low
