Synapses
 How do neurons send signals to each other, since there 
is a gap (synapse) between? 
 Action potential reaches axon terminal
 Causes calcium gates to open and Ca2+ flows in
 Calcium fuses with vesicles full of neurotransmitters, 
these fuse to the membrane
 Neurotransmitters released into synapses, diffuse across
 Attach to post synaptic membrane and cause an effect
 Excitatory – cause action potential
 Inhibitory – raise threshold potential
 Release from receptors and are brought back into 
presynaptic neurons or are broken down by enzymes
