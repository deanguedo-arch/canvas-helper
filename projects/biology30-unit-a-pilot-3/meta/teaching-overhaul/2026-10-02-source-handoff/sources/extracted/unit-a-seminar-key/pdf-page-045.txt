Eyes
 Cornea – refracts light towards lens
 Aqueous humor – fluid between cornea
 Pupil – opening in iris that allows light to pass in
 Iris – controls size of pupil
 Lens – focuses light onto the retina
 Vitreous humor – fluid in eye, gives it shape
 Sclera – outer part of eye, provides structure support and 
protection
 Retina – senses light and sends signal through optic nerve to brain
 Contains rods that detect dark/black and white
 Contains cones that detect colour , detail, lots of cones near the fovea 
centralis
 Choroid – nourish the eye, absorb light
