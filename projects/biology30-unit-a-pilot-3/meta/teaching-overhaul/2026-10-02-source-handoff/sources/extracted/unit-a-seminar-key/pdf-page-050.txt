Structures
 Pinna – outer part of the ear , some mammals can control
 Auditory canal – tube that carries sound to ear drum
 Tympanic membrane – ear drum, vibrates in response to 
sound waves
 Ossicles (3) – smallest bones in body, transmit and amplify 
vibration from ear drum to fluid filled cochlea through 
oval window
 Organ of Corti – inside the cochlea, has hair cells in fluid 
that move in response to vibrations from ossicles, send 
nerve impulse
 Semicircular canals – fluid filled canals that send signals 
concerning balance and head position
 Eustachian tube – leads to throat, controls pressure inside 
the ear (ear popping)
