that transmit 
information (A nerve is 
a bundle of neurons)
 Cell Body – contain the 
nucleus and two extensions
 Dendrites – shorter (look 
like tentacles) receive 
information
 Axons – single long ‘fiber’ 
which conducts nerve 
impulse, carries 
information

myelin sheathe, speed up 
impulse by allowing action 
potential to jump to each
