Posterior pituitary gland
 Antidiuretic hormone (ADH, 
vasopressin)**
 Acts on the collecting duct 
of the nephron in the 
kidney, causes you to retain 
water
 Released when dehydrated
 Also increases blood 
pressure (more water in 
blood)
 Lack of ADH causes diabetes 
insipidus (can’t retain 
water)
