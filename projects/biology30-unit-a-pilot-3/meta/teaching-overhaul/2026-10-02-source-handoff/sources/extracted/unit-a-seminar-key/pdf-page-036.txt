Brain structures
 Cerebellum – balance and movement
 Thalamus – relay center for senses
 Hypothalamus – regulates homeostasis, links nervous 
and endocrine systems, sends signal to pit gland
 Pons – sleep, motor control
 Midbrain – senses, alertness, regulates temperature
 Medulla oblongata – vital body functions; breathing, 
heart rate, blood pressure
