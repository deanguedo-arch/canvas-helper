A mutation in the connexin 26 gene involves the deletion of two bases and their 
replacement by two new bases. The deletion is shown below.
The two deleted bases are replaced by two adenine bases.
—based on Human Gene Mutation Database, 2010
  The transcription of the mutated connexin 26 gene described above results in the 
replacement of a
 A. stop codon with a lysine codon
 B. methionine codon with a lysine codon
 C. stop codon with a phenylalanine codon
 D. methionine codon with a phenylalanine codon
Answer: C 
Outcomes: C3.6k, C3.3k, and C3.2s 
Cognitive level: A
