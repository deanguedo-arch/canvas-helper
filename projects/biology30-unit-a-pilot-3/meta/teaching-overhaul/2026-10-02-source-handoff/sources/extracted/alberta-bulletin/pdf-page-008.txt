Diploma Examinations: Multiple Forms
Some subjects may have two distinct forms (versions) of diploma examinations during major administrations (January and June). 
Like all other diploma examinations, the two forms are equated to the baseline examination to ensure that the same standard 
applies to both forms. Both forms adhere to the established blueprint specifications and are reviewed by a technical review 
committee.
To facilitate the analysis of school-level results, each school receives only one examination form per subject. In subjects offering 
a translated French-language examination, both forms are administered in English and in French.
