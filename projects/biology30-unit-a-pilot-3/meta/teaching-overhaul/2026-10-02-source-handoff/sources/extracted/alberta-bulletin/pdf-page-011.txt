How can teachers schedule field tests?
Field tests are offered digitally on the digital assessment platform. To schedule a field test, teachers must have a digital 
assessment platform teacher account.
For information about how to schedule and administer a field test, please refer to the Field Testing Guide 2025–2026  on the 
Teacher participation in provincial assessments web page or contact Field.Test@gov.ab.ca .
Detailed instructions for how to schedule a field test can also be found on the digital assessment platform Help  web page.
