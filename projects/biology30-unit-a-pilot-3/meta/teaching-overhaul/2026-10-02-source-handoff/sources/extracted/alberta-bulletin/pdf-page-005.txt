Introduction
The purpose of this bulletin is to provide teachers of Biology 30 with information about the diploma examinations scheduled in 
the 2025–2026 school year. This bulletin should be used in conjunction with the current Biology 30 Program of Studies . 
This bulletin includes information about the Biology 30 Diploma Examinations  that will be administered in November 2025 and 
January, April, June, and August 2026; clarifications of some aspects of the examinations; and other subject-specific information.
Diploma exams will be weighted at 30%, and the school-awarded mark will constitute 70% of a student’s final mark. 
Teachers are encouraged to share the contents of this bulletin with students.
For further information about program implementation, refer to the Alberta Education and Childcare website.
