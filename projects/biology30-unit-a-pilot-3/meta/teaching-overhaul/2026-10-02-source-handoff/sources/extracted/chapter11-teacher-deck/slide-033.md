Neurotransmitter Binding

Once neurotransmitters have reached the post-synaptic membrane, they bind with receptors embedded in the membrane 

If the neurotransmitter is excitatory, binding induces an action potential in the postsynaptic neuron

Neurotransmitters are then released by the receptors and either return to the presynaptic neuron or are broken down by enzymes such as cholinesterase on the post-synaptic membrane
