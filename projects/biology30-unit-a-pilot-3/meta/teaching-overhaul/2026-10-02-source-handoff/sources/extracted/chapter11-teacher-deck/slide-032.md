Neurotransmitter Release 

An action potential reaches the axon terminal

Calcium channel “gates” within the axon terminal open, causing calcium ions to flow into the cell 

Calcium ions trigger the movement and fusing of the vesicles with the presynaptic membrane (exocytosis). In turn, neurotransmitters are released into the synapse.

Neurotransmitters diffuse across the synaptic cleft to the post-synaptic membrane

Neurotransmitters are required to help transmit the electrical impulse from one neuron to another. These are stored in vesicles in the axon bulb.
