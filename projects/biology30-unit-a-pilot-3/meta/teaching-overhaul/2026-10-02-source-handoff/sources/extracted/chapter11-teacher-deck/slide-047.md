Sympathetic response (stress response)

“fight or flight” response 

activated during stressful situations

the neurotransmitters acetylcholine &  norepinephrine are released to excite muscles

Pupils dilate, blood pressure increases & heart beats faster to prepare your body

digestion slows and the bladder is constricted

Parasympathetic response

“rest and digest” response

activated when the body is calm and at rest

associated with the neurotransmitter acetylcholine

Pupils constrict, slows heart rate, reduces blood pressure, promotes digestion

Subdivisions of the autonomic system:

many of the physiological changes in response to stress are detectable by polygraphs; heart rate, blood pressure, and breathing rate all tend to increase when someone is telling a lie
