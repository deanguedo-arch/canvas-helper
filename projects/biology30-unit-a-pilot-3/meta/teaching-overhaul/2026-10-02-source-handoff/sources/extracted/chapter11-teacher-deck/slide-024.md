2) Depolarization 

Action potential occurs when a neuron is stimulated by an electrical impulse.

If a strong enough impulse is received and the threshold potential is reached (approx. -55 mV), sodium gates will open, thus allowing Na+ to diffuse freely across the membrane.

Na+ rushes into the cell (down their concentration gradient → facilitated diffusion), leading to a slight positive charge on the inside relative to the outside.

This reverses the membrane potential from -70 mV to +40 mV; the membrane is now said to be depolarized. 

Once Na+ reaches equilibrium, the membrane once again becomes impermeable to them, and the gates close.
