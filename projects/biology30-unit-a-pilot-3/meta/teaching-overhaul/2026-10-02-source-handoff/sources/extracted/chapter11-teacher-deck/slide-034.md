Neurotransmitter Types 

Excitatory (e.g. glutamate, acetylcholine, epinephrine)

cause the sodium channels of the post-synaptic membrane to open and sodium to flow in, resulting in slight depolarization of the membrane and lowering the threshold potential of the post-synaptic neuron 

e.g. acetylcholine is a neurotransmitter found in muscle cells; it excites the muscle membrane, causing depolarization and contraction of the muscle fibre

Cholinesterase (an enzyme) is required to break down acetylcholine after the action potential has occurred

Often when more than one neuron fires at the same time, a sufficient amount of neurotransmitter is released to cause depolarization of the postsynaptic membrane. The production of an action potential as a result is referred to as summation
