Neurotransmitter Types Cont’d 

Inhibitory (e.g. GABA)

Trigger potassium channels to open, causing potassium ions to flow out and thus lowering the membrane potential.

This leads to hyperpolarization, making it more difficult to generate an action potential (because the threshold level has been increased)

Problems with GABA are often associated with epilepsy and Huntington’s disease
