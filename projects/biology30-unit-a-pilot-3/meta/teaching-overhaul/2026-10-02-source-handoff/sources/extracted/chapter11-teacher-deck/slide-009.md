Neuron Structure

Dendrites are short thread-like branches which act to accept nerve impulses from other neurons and carry them towards the cell body, which is the main part of the cell, containing the nucleus and other organelles 

Axons are longer branches which carry nerve impulses away from the cell body 

A myelin sheath surrounds many axons, insulating the neuron and speeding up the rate of impulse transmission.
