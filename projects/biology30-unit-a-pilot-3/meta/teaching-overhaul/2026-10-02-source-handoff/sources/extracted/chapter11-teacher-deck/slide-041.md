Action of Drugs at the Synapse

Mechanisms of stimulants

Drug mimics the neurotransmitter & stimulates the receptor.

Drug decreases the rate of neurotransmitter breakdown or re-uptake, leading to continued stimulation of the receptor.

Drug increases the rate of neurotransmitter release from the presynaptic neuron.
