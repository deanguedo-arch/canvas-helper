Action of Drugs at the Synapse

Mechanisms of depressants:

Drug blocks the receptor site so that the neurotransmitter cannot interact with the receptor.

Drug decreases synthesis & storage of a neurotransmitter at the presynaptic neuron.

Drug increases rate of breakdown of a neurotransmitter in the synapse.
