Types of Neurons

Three main types of neurons work together to signal effectors (e.g. muscle cells) to respond appropriately to different stimuli:

Sensory Neurons (afferent)

Gather information from sensory receptors (i.e. touch/sight/sound/taste receptors) and transmit these impulses to the brain & spinal cord

Interneurons

Found in the Central Nervous System (brain and spinal cord)

Process and integrate incoming sensory info from sensory neurons and relay outgoing information to motor neurons

Motor Neurons (efferent)

Transmit information to effectors, such as muscles and glands
