Multiple Sclerosis

MS is a genetic disorder which involves damage to the myelin sheath

Symptoms include numbness and tingling of the limbs, muscle spasms, and a loss of balance and coordination.

The more scar tissue that builds up along the nerve, the worse the symptoms become. As a result, MS gradually worsens with age.
