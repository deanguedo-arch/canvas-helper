The Forebrain: Cerebrum

The forebrain or cerebrum is the largest part of the brain. It is divided into left and right hemispheres, and can be further categorized into four main lobes, each of which is responsible for a series of specific functions. 

The surface of the cerebrum is referred to as the cerebral cortex. Composed of grey matter, the cortex has many folds which act to increase surface area.
