b. Define each structure: pinna, auditory canal, tympanum, ossicles, cochlea, organ of 
Corti, auditory nerve, semicircular canals and Eustachian tube 
i. Pinna – outer part of the ear, some mammals can control 
ii. Auditory canal – tube that carries sound to ear drum 
iii. Tympanic membrane – ear drum, vibrates in response to sound waves 
iv. Ossicles (3) – smallest bones in body, transmit and amplify vibration from ear 
drum to fluid filled cochlea through oval window 
v. Organ of Corti – inside the cochlea, has hair cells in fluid that move in response 
to vibrations from ossicles, send nerve impulse 
vi. Semicircular canals – fluid filled canals that send signals concerning balance and 
head position 
vii. Eustachian tube – leads to throat, controls pressure inside the ear (ear popping) 
 
 
c. The inner ear contains mechanoreceptors for hearing and balance
