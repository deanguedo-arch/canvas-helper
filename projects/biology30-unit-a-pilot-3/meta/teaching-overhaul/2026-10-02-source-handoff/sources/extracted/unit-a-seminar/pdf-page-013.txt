 Structures: cerebral cortex, hemispheres (left and right) and lobes (4); cerebellum, pons, 
medulla oblongata, hypothalamus 
 Cerebrum: thinking part of brain, cerebral cortex is outer wrinkly part (increase surface 
area) 
 Left hemisphere – controls right side, math, logic, problem solving 
 Right hemisphere – controls left side, visual, musical, mental imaging. 
 4 lobes 
 Frontal: fine motor skills, reasoning, higher thinking, speech, planning 
 Parital: recognition of stimuli, sensory input 
 Occipital lobe: visual information 
 Temporal: auditory information, memory 
 Brainstem: medulla, pons, midbrain (involuntary) 
 Cerebellum – balance and movement
