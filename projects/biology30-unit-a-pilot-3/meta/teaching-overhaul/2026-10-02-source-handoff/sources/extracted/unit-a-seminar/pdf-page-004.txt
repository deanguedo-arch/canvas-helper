d. Schwann cells and Myelin Sheath 
i. Schwann cells (white matter)  
1. Surround the axon 
2. Function – speed up nerve impulse 
3. Group of Schwann cells makes up myelin sheath around the axon 
4. Gaps between Schwann cells are called nodes of Ranvier 
5. Nerve impulse jumps between nodes – called saltatorial conduction 
6. Myelination and increased diameter speed up nerve impulse
