 
i. Posterior Pituitary Gland (2 major hormones) 
i. Antidiuretic hormone (ADH, vasopressin) 
1. Acts on the collecting duct of the nephron in the kidney, causes you to 
retain water 
2. Released when dehydrated 
3. Also increases blood pressure (more water in blood) 
4. Lack of ADH causes diabetes insipidus (can’t retain water)
