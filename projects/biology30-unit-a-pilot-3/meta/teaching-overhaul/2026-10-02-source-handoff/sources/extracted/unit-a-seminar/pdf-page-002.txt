Your Exam 
 What to expect? 
 45 MC  7 NR 15 marks of WR 
 Diploma Style/Context questions 
 Most questions are context-based, meaning they require reading. 
 Often times totally new biological contexts will be covered, however the material 
learned in Bio 30 will allow you to answer these questions 
 Ex: A question refers to ALS (neurodegenerative diseases). You won't need to 
know anything about ALS it will be explained in the question. The question itself 
will be from the Biology 30 curriculum 
Studying Tips 
 
1. Summarize  
a. Imagine you get to bring 1 sheet of paper into the exam with anything you want 
b. Condense each unit into as small as possible (Bio 30 on 4 pgs) 
2. Out loud 
a. Studies show reading out loud helps retention 
3. Practice questions 
a. Self-assessment, Exam Bank, Quest A+  
4. Flash cards/Quizlet → Spaced Repetition 
5. Visualize and connect 
a. Notes in your own words, making connections, webs and drawings
