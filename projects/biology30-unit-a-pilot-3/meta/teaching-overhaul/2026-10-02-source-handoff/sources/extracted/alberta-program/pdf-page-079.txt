Specific Outcomes for Science, Technology and Society (STS) (Nature of Science Emphasis)

30–D3.1sts explain how concepts, models and theories are often used in interpreting and explaining 
observations and in predicting future observations (NS6a) 
• develop appropriate investigative strategies, such as a risk-benefit analysis or 
cost-benefit analysis, for analyzing biological issues
