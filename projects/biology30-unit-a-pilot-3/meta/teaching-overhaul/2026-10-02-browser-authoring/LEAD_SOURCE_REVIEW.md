## Consequential review questions

1. The broad systems-table prompt cannot be satisfied solely by naming CNS/PNS. Teach a concise, accurate hierarchy first: CNS/PNS; sensory versus motor routes; somatic versus autonomic motor control; sympathetic/parasympathetic. Preserve more detailed physiology for lesson-08. Avoid teaching that all somatic activity is consciously chosen: skeletal-muscle withdrawal is a somatic reflex.
2. Distinguish stimulus (a change), receptor (detecting structure), incoming information, integration, motor instruction, effector and response. Homeostasis is regulation within workable ranges, not every response being homeostatic or an absolutely constant internal value. Teach a complete causal homeostasis example, not just the term.
3. A neuron is one cell, a nerve is a bundle of nerve fibres/axons; not a bundle of whole dendrite-to-terminal drawings. Neuron-to-neuron chemical signalling should be previewed without assuming that the same impulse physically leaps a synaptic gap or that all synapses connect to dendrites only.
4. Neuron structure is a typical multipolar example, not a universal sensory-neuron shape. Branching supports receiving area; the soma carries out metabolic activity; the axon conducts away from the cell body; terminals signal other cells. Do not depend on untaught advanced synapse or ion-channel mechanisms to complete first-batch practice.
5. Existing myelin arrows use external jumping shorthand. Accompany them with explicit continuous-axon guidance and nodal regeneration. State Schwann cells/PNS and oligodendrocytes/CNS. Do not equate myelinated/unmyelinated with guaranteed regeneration/no regeneration, or white/grey matter with two exclusive neuron classes. Avoid unnecessary disease-detail expansion.
6. Withdrawal may begin before conscious awareness, but sensory information still reaches the brain. Spinal withdrawal is an example, not the wiring of every reflex. The direct sensory-to-motor connection in the simplest stretch-reflex component must not be generalized to all arcs. No dangerous hot/sharp-object experiment should be assigned.
7. The required drawing has no new photo-upload control. Explain a paper drawing with a typed label/function account compatible with the actual written box; do not imply the drawing itself is stored in browser work.
8. Current guides contain a generic MC sentence that is inappropriate for these three written-only checks. Manuscript instructions should accurately describe these checks while retaining all underlying controls and semantics. This is proposed copy, not permission to modify HTML now.
9. The teacher-only comprehension key's physical PDF page 2, Q6, labels dodging a seen tennis ball as the same spinal withdrawal reflex. This conflicts with the current explicitly compared voluntary-response example and is not an acceptable authority for a direct eye → spinal interneuron shortcut. Frame the worked voluntary example as seeing and choosing a movement; distinguish visual/brain processing from the spinal withdrawal example. Rapid movement alone is not enough evidence to identify a reflex. Preserve the original required prompt, record the historical-key conflict outside learner copy, and do not turn the old key into new automatic feedback.
