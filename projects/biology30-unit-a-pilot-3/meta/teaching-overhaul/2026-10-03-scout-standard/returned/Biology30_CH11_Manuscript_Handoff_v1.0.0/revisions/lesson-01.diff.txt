 Nervous and endocrine communication help coordinate these adjustments. Neurons carry rapid electrical changes along their membranes and usually communicate chemically at junctions. Endocrine glands release **hormones** into blood; a hormone affects cells with suitable receptors. Nervous responses are generally rapid and targeted, while many hormonal effects develop more slowly and last longer. The two systems work together, and those timing descriptions are general patterns.
 
 Not every nervous response is a negative-feedback loop. Choosing to wave to someone is nervous communication, but it does not necessarily correct a disturbed internal variable. When you call something homeostatic, identify the condition being regulated and explain how the response helps regulate it.
+
+For warming, connect the response to heat loss: sweat glands release sweat onto the skin, and evaporation removes heat from the body.
