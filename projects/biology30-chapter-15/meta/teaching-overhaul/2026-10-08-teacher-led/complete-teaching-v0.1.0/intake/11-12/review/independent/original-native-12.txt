The independent set and textbook review are optional.Follow development at three levelsAt the cell level, fertilization restores a diploid chromosome complement and cleavage increases cell number. At the tissue level, the blastocyst has distinct regions and gastrulation establishes germ layers. At the organism level, morphogenesis and organogenesis establish structures that continue growing and maturing.Alongside these changes, supporting tissues provide exchange, protection and hormones. Keep the embryo or fetus, its membranes, the placenta and maternal tissues distinct. They cooperate without becoming one indistinguishable structure.Explain a pathway rather than listing termsFor each mechanism identify a starting change, a signal or process, its target and the resulting effect. hCG maintains corpus-luteum activity early in pregnancy. Placental exchange moves substances across an interface. Cervical stretch and oxytocin reinforce labour. Prolactin and oxytocin support different lactation functions.When a condition changes, ask which step is affected. Normal hormone concentrations do not prove normal transport or receptor function. A visible organ does not prove mature function. A measured exposure does not by itself establish an exact developmental outcome.Connect the main ideasTopicWhat you should be able to explainFertilization and the zygoteExplain where fertilization
