# Chapter 15: scientific and source reconciliation

These are developer records, not student-facing lesson commentary.

Developmental ages are explicitly measured from fertilization unless gestational age is named. The fetal period continues from week 9 to birth, rather than ending at week 12. Gastrulation is placed in the third post-fertilization week. The human polyspermy explanation emphasizes the cortical/zona response, not a universal instantaneous membrane-depolarization rule. hCG supports the corpus luteum but is not described as a uniquely infallible pregnancy diagnosis. Prolactin production and oxytocin ejection are distinguished. Maternal and fetal circulations are separate. Clinical treatment doses, emergency-contraception deadlines, survival predictions and historical folic-acid recommendations were not copied as current medical guidance. The external-research task on p. 524 is excluded from the optional task selector; the original remains in the book.
