SLIDE18
Occurs between weeks 3 & 8, when germ layers begin to fold and early organ systems take shape… Week 3  =  neurulation  (formation of  neural tube  which will later develop into the brain and spinal  cord ) , primitive heart starts beating Week 4  = Formation of blood cells.  Lungs and kidneys take shape, and small buds (which will develop into arms and legs) appear. A distinct head is visible. Week 5  = Eyes open, cells of the brain undergo rapid differentiation. Week 6  = Continued development of the brain, lengthening of limbs.  Week 7  = Organs and skeleton almost completely formed, external genitalia begin to differentiate. Nervous system begins to coordinate body activity Week 8  = Embryo is the size of a paperclip. 90% of organs are established.  Organ Formation (Organogenesis )

FUNCTIONS OF THE PLACENTA: Nutritional functions Transports nutrients from mother to fetus  Stores nutrients Excretory functions Transports wastes from fetal blood to the mothers blood Respiratory functions Transports  oxygen  from the mother to the fetus and CO2 from the fetus to the mother Endocrine functions Secretes hormones Allows hormones from the fetus to diffuse into the mothers blood (and vice versa) Immune functions Transports antibodies from the mother to the fetus
