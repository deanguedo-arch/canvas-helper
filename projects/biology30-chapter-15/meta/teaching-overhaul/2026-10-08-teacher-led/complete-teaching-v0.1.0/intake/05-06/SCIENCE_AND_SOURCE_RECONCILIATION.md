## Human yolk sac: storage is not the same as support
Textbook516's no-nutritive-function statement is too absolute. Goh et al., *Yolk sac cell atlas reveals multiorgan functions during human early development*, Science381:eadd7564(2023), DOI10.1126/science.add7564, primary abstract/results summary inspected at https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37590359/ and indexed primary manuscript https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7614978/ . Human yolk-sac data support early blood-forming, metabolic and nutritional functions with changing roles as other organs develop. The candidate uses only that bounded distinction, not experimental methods or a quantitative claim about how much nutrition the sac supplies.

A large food-yolk reserve in a bird egg is different from early processing/transfer support in humans. Required05 selections/model already make the limited correct claim: early roles, not a food bag until birth. Both remain unchanged. The new text also avoids a direct yolk-sac-to-bone-marrow-only handover by mentioning developing liver as an intermediate major blood-forming site. No new clinical advice is introduced.
