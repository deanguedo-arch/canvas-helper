## Science checks and source disagreements

1. The cervical-stretch model correctly separates physical stimulus, neural input, hypothalamic control/synthesis, posterior-pituitary oxytocin release, blood transport, uterine target and the return to increased stretch. It also includes local prostaglandin action and makes clear that reinforcement is not a full theory of labour initiation. Delivery limits this particular loop while uterine contraction can continue for placental delivery and afterward.
2. The cervix was initially called an opening. The final candidate says it is the lower, narrow part of the uterus that opens into the vagina. This restores the structure/location distinction without changing the model or assessment meaning. The adjacent sensory-pathway paragraph and all affected first answers remain valid.
3. Dilation, expulsion and placental delivery are distinguished by events. The stages are not confused with fertilization or a fixed duration. The baby and placenta are not treated as necessarily delivered simultaneously.
4. Prolactin from the anterior pituitary supports secretory-cell production. Oxytocin made in the hypothalamus and released by the posterior pituitary supports myoepithelial contraction and ejection. Neural impulses, circulating hormones and milk in ducts have distinct routes. The worked example correctly identifies impaired ejection without diagnosing absent prolactin or uniquely locating the cause.
5. The candidate correctly preserves prenatal prolactin and explicitly corrects the contrary textbook526 and teacher28 statements. A directly checked primary study measured maternal serum prolactin during pregnancy and attributes serum prolactin to the pituitary: Kletzky et al., 1985, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3985054/ . It found increasing levels between early pregnancy and later gestation, directly contradicting a first appearance only after birth.
6. Post-placental hormone changes are appropriately linked to greater prolactin-supported secretion rather than hormone first appearance. Prenatal small amounts of colostrum and the limits of a simple quantity/time prediction are appropriately stated. The model does not claim all lactation control resides in one hormone or all production ceases immediately when a feed ends.
7. The optional textbook's vitamin C premise cannot receive an unqualified current-science pass. The directly checked 2024 OTIS fact sheet describes limited birth-defect evidence and no known increase at the cited lower/higher intake ranges; it does not establish categorical teratogenicity: https://mothertobaby.org/fact-sheets/vitamin-c/ . This is a source-validity limitation, not a newly authorized replacement key or dietary recommendation.
8. The directly checked NAHO2004 Appendix2 passage confirms the food pattern cited in optional Q8 but does not give its reasons. Therefore reasons remain hypotheses requiring additional evidence; the hormone lessons cannot establish cultural rationale. Source: https://www.ontariomidwives.ca/sites/default/files/2019-08/NAHO%20Midwifery%20and%20Aboriginal%20Midwifery%20in%20Canada%202004.pdf , printed24, PDF page index23.
