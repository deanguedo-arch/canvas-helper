Hormonal control of lactation: During pregnancy, high levels of estrogen and progesterone suppress the production of milk  After birth, the pituitary begins to secrete  pro lactin , which then stimulates milk  pro duction The actual  release of milk from the breasts (lactation)  is mediated by  oxytocin , which causes contractions within the mammary lobules; suckling motions send signals through nerve endings to the hypothalamus, which then stimulates the pituitary to release oxytocin.  For some women, the sound of an infant crying can also stimulate the production of oxytocin.
