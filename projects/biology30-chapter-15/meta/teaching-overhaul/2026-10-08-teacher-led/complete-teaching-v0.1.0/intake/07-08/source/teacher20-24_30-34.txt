The placenta does NOT filter out harmful substances… Drugs, alcohol, etc. circulating in the mothers blood will pass through the placenta to the fetus’s blood Can have severe consequences (such as FASD, or Fetal Alcohol Spectrum Disorder) Such substances are referred to as  teratogens Teratogens are most harmful during the first trimester during when the neural tube and most organs develop Why would the developing blastocyst not be very  sensitive  to teratogens during the first 10-14 days after fertilization? The cells in the blastocyst have not begun to differentiate yet so if one cell is damaged, a different one can easily take over You should be able to describe some examples teratogens and their impact on embryonic or fetal development.  ! SLIDE34
However,  anatomic sex  is not determined until about the seventh week of development: At week seven, genes contained within the short arm of the  Y chromosome encode the secretion of testosterone , which then causes the embryo to begin differentiating. The absence of this gene means that no testosterone is produced. As a result, the embryo will continue developing as a female.
