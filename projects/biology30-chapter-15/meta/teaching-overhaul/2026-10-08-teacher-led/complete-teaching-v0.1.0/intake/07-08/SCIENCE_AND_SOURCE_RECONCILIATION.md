## Placental growth and mass
Actual p516 incorrectly associates a fully developed600g placenta with ten weeks. Human evidence contradicts the combination:
- Institute of Medicine/National Research Council, Weight Gain During Pregnancy(2009), chapter3 p84, official PDF https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/n/nap12584/pdf/ and chapter https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK32815/ . The independent preflight directly located early placental measurements and continuing growth, while noting limitations of early specimens. Its cited1969 study table was not directly accessible; no direct primary-table read is claimed.
- Burkhardt et al.(2006), DOI10.1016/j.ejogrb.2005.10.032, PMID16377060, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16377060/ . Indexed primary abstract describes11,141 uncomplicated term pregnancies, with delivered placental means varying by delivery mode around545/621g. These are method/population-specific term observations, not a universal expected weight.
- Boyd(1984), DOI10.1016/0378-3782(84)90074-4, PMID6745150, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6745150/ . Indexed primary abstract reports placental/villus growth from10gestational weeks to term. Full article was not inspected.

Candidate07 explains continuing development and places the rough500–600g scale at term, not at ten weeks. No new exact early-weight target, growth curve or patient inference is introduced.

## Exchange and image abstraction
Original p516Table15.1, p517Figure15.11 and actual teacher20 support placental roles and separate circulations. The native SVG compresses cord vessels and capillary branching into one loop. C/D denote incoming arterial and outgoing venous routes, not literal whole umbilical arteries inside one villus. Crossing arrows indicate substance transfer; white arrows indicate fetal blood direction. These meanings are now taught.

Oxygen/carbon dioxide can cross by diffusion; nutrient transport is heterogeneous, including carriers and energy-dependent processes. Source caption shorthand must not become a universal simple-diffusion rule. Vein/artery names describe direction relative to the fetal heart. Relative oxygen content follows placental exchange, not vessel colour or a vein making oxygen.

## Dating and development
The source's p520 calculation266days≈40weeks after fertilization is inconsistent:266/7=38. The common forty-week count uses LMP-based gestational dating with an approximate two-week offset. The candidate defines the conventions, states the illustrative assumption and does not claim a precise personal fertilization date. Official corroboration: https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/002398.htm , dating definitions and the38-after-conception/40gestational distinction. No external text or images copied.

The fetal period continues until birth. Teacher22's9–12 range can describe the first-trimester portion but is not the whole fetal period; teacher7's whole-period9–12 definition is wrong. Source timelines/length tables are not clinical growth or viability standards. The month-nine50cm value under a crown-to-rump footnote, broad survival language and twins-usually-month-eight claims are not adopted.
