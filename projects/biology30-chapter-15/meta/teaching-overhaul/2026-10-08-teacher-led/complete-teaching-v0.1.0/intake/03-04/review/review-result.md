## Final-copy reattempts

### Lesson 03 selection 2 and measurement reasoning

Final answer remains **“That implantation is definitely in the normal uterine location.”** The new paragraph says the pregnancy-associated label describes hCG's normal role and why tests measure it, not proof of a normally located pregnancy. That is scientifically sound and helps the learner interpret the intended contrast. The literal ambiguity in “is being used,” described above, remains. It should not be counted as a missing-given failure of the surrounding lesson.

A defensible explanation from the final copy is: “The sample contains detectable hCG. hCG is a hormone associated with the normal early-pregnancy pathway, but that classification does not locate implantation or establish normal development in this tested individual. hCG can also occur outside pregnancy, and detecting a signal does not prove that its target responded.” Nothing in this answer requires an unprovided patient history, a diagnosis or an invented concentration.

The conceptus definition is suitable: the developing organism together with its associated membranes. It supplies the previously unexplained title term without adding a new assessment demand.

### Lesson 04 required writing 1

Reattempt: “Gastrulation rearranges cells of the early embryonic disc, rather than merely increasing their number. Cells move inward and spread. Some establish endoderm; others form mesoderm between the inner layer and the remaining surface cells, which become ectoderm. The result is a three-layer embryo, or gastrula. These primary germ layers contribute to different tissues as development continues.”

Every step is now directly taught. It fits the unchanged model's cell movement, organization and three lineages. The prose remains a suitable simplified human account without requiring terminology such as epiblast or primitive streak. It does not assert that the diagram itself depicts these movements.

### Lesson 04 worked skin sample and intestinal writing

The final trunk-skin condition usefully bounds the dermal example. Reattempt: epidermis and sensory nerve cells have ectodermal origin; trunk dermal connective tissue is mesodermal. Therefore adult surface location does not assign the entire sample one origin. This is consistent with the source's broad derivatives and avoids an unnecessary generalization about facial dermis.

The unchanged intestinal writing remains fully supported: muscle is mesodermal and much of intestinal epithelial lining is endodermal. Selective disruption can affect the two contributions differently. The stated case does not establish a specific diagnosis or that every tissue of one layer is damaged.

## Exact-source science and figures

### hCG pathway

- Actual PPTX slide 11 describes corpus-luteum progesterone/estrogen maintenance and then overstates no-pregnancy decline as no hormone being produced. The candidate correctly uses declining support rather than universal absence.
- Actual slide 12 describes trophoblast hCG, continued corpus-luteum output and later placental output. Its actual embedded image has **only one hCG curve**, a gestational-week x axis and no numerical y scale. It could not independently support a progesterone comparison. The candidate instead includes original textbook Figure 15.5 from printed p. 512.
- Actual slide 13 claims trophoblast is the only possible bodily hCG source. The candidate rejects that absolute while preserving the usual pathway. Stenman et al.'s primary nonpregnant-subject study directly supports the existence of low nonpregnancy hCG-like material: [PubMed PMID 3546353](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3546353/). It does not establish that every commercial urine test detects those levels, and the candidate makes no such claim.
- The first-trimester shift toward placental progesterone is supported by Tal and Taylor's publisher-hosted [Endocrinology of Pregnancy](https://www.endotext.org/wp-content/uploads/pdfs/endocrinology-of-pregnancy-2.pdf), updated February 6, 2024, pp. 9–10. Its approximate gestational-week dates should not be silently relabelled as weeks after fertilization; the candidate avoids that error. Its placental-estrogen account supports the broader source-change wording as pregnancy continues.
- The measurement-versus-location distinction accords with [ACOG's ectopic-pregnancy diagnostic explanation](https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/ectopic-pregnancy): hormone testing and ultrasound location evidence serve different purposes. This supports the curricular evidence limit, not an individual medical interpretation.

### Germ layers and differentiation

Actual PPTX slide 14's week-two heading and textbook pp. 511–513 blend the earlier two-layer disc with the definitive three-layer sequence. The candidate distinguishes them. The third week after fertilization is directly corroborated by the primary study [Tyser et al., 2021, PMID 34789876](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34789876/), which profiled a human embryo staged 16–19 days after fertilization. One embryo does not establish an exact universal onset day; the candidate does not claim one.

The final inward-movement sequence is consistent with [UNSW's authored human gastrulation practical](https://embryology.med.unsw.edu.au/embryology/index.php/BGDA_Practical_3_-_Gastrulation): cells migrate inward to definitive endoderm and intervening mesoderm, with remaining cells becoming ectoderm. The indexed content was available; direct UNSW retrieval returned 403, so this report does not claim full live-page access.

Actual slide 14's image depicts generic blastula invagination. Actual slide 16 is a simplified inward-migration illustration with whole-organ derivative shorthand. Neither was mistaken for an exact human anatomical drawing. The candidate's retained bands explicitly summarize the resulting layers, not the movements, real shape or cavities.

Textbook Figure 15.7 and actual PPTX slides 15–16 supply the representative germ-layer lists. The candidate properly narrows organ-level labels to named tissues and epithelial linings. The trunk qualifier is sound: primary lineage work distinguishes dorsal-trunk dermal precursors from neural-crest-derived facial precursors, [Jinno et al., PMID 20848654](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20848654/). This is corroborative animal lineage research, not a claim that the candidate teaches its experimental details. General different expression of shared inherited genetic information is appropriate; “generally” avoids an unwarranted universal genome identity claim.
