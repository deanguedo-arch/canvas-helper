Cleavage and Implantation
After fertilization, the zygote 
continues moving through the oviduct towards the uterus. During this time, several cell divisions occur, leading to the formation of the morula.
Initial cell division occurs rapidly, leaving little time for cell growth. 
    This process of 
cell division without enlargement
 is called 
cleavage
. 
Over the 3-5 day period required to reach the uterus, cleavage 
    occurs several times to form a sphere of 16 cells. This is the 
morula
. 
Trimester 1/Embryonic Stage

===== SLIDE 9 =====
Upon entering the uterus, the 
morula
 further develops into the 
blastocyst
, a fluid-filled structure consisting of two layers:
the 
trophoblast
 which will 
release hCG and later turn into the chorion and placenta
, and 
and the 
inner cell mass
, which will later 
develop into the embryo
Between days 5-7, the blastocyst will begin attaching itself to the endometrium of the uterus; a process known as 
implantation
. 
Implantation is complete by days 10-14. If successful, the woman is now said to be pregnant

Remember… in the menstrual cycle, the role of the 
corpus
 luteum is to release progesterone (and some estrogen) to maintain the endometrial lining should implantation occur. If no fertilization and implantation occur, the corpus luteum degenerate after about 14 days. In turn, there is no progesterone and estrogen produced and thus the endometrium will shed → menstruation. 
Human Chorionic 
Gonadotropin
 (hCG)
But what if fertilization and implantation do occur? 
We do not want the endometrium lining to shed as that would lead to a miscarriage.

===== SLIDE 12 =====
Once implantation occurs, the 
trophoblast
 begins secreting the hormone human chorionic gonadotropin (hCG)…
hCG maintains the corpus luteum so that it continues secreting estrogen and progesterone
This maintains the endometrium and prevents menstruation
hCG levels decline after the second month of pregnancy, when the placenta is able to secrete these hormones on its own
Human Chorionic Gonadotropin (hCG)

===== SLIDE 13 =====
Urine pregnancy tests look for the presence of hCG. Since the trophoblast is the only possible source of hCG in the body, if hCG is present then pregnancy is confirmed.
Since the trophoblast begins to produce hCG within 1-2 weeks of fertilization, which occurs soon after ovulation, hCG can be often detected up to one week before an expected menstrual cycle. Most accurate results, however, would be expected after a missed cycle.

During week 2, the blastocyst begins to change…
A space known as the 
amniotic cavity
 
forms between the inner cell mass and the
 
trophoblast. 
This cavity will contain 
amniotic fluid
 that will serve to cushion and protect the developing baby from physical trauma
The inner cell mass flattens and folds upon itself to create three distinct layers: the 
outer 
ectoderm
, the inner 
endoderm
, and the middle 
mesoderm
. 
This process is known as 
gastrulation
, after which the blastocyst is referred to as the 
gastrula
.
