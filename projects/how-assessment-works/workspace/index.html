How Assessment Works

Your grade is based on more than a finished assignment. You will show what you created, how your thinking developed, and what you understand.

No surprises: every major assessment tells you the learning goal, success criteria, evidence to submit, and the weight of each part.

Your assessment journey

Major assessments come after learning and practice. A readiness checkpoint helps you decide whether to move forward or complete one useful next step first.

Product, Process, and Defence

The three pillars answer different questions. Together, they give a clearer picture of what you know and can do.

Product

Your final response, performance, project, exam, or other result demonstrates the learning outcome.

Examples

  • Final written response or exam
  • Presentation, demonstration, or performance
  • Completed project or solution

After you submit: Defence

After you submit the requested Product and Process evidence, Defence is a normal opportunity to explain choices, demonstrate understanding, and apply learning. It is not an accusation.

Short conversation

Answer a few focused questions with your teacher.

Written response

Explain a decision or apply the learning in writing.

Recorded explanation

Use an approved audio or video response when directed.

Your assessment directions identify the expected format. Accommodations and alternate ways to respond remain available when they are part of your learning plan.

Needs more explanation

Shows understanding
