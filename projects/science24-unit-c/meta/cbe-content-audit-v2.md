Its fifteen Unit C HTML pages and nineteen images were inventoried; selected images were opened at their native size. No CBE page or figure is approved for direct learner publication in this candidate. Adapt useful *ideas* into new, source-mapped explanations only after accuracy review.

## Figure review

- **Potential source prompts:** `ecolibacteria.jpg` is a clear colourized bacterial micrograph; `epithelialcells.jpg` clearly shows cilia with a 5 μm scale. Their original publishers and image rights are not declared in the archive, so keep them reference-only. The class textbook and workbook provide the necessary learner-visible page images.
- **Replace with accessible new diagrams:** `AntibodyAndAntigen.png` is small and uses confusing “antibody marker” labeling; `ImmuneSystemAlert.png` compresses a complex response into an overcertain five-step story. `ImmunityChart.png` says inherited immunity lasts a lifetime and active immunity is usually permanent. `DNA_simple.png` lacks usable labels. The small pedigree examples are less complete than the 2023 workbook p. 31 diagram.
- **Historical data only:** `DiseaseTimeline1.png`, `DiseaseTimeline2.png`, `AirQualityIndex.png`, `MutagenTable.png` and `GeneticEngineeringTable.png` need dated/source-specific framing before any use.
- **No decorative duplication:** `BacteriaImage1.jpg`, `recessive.gif`, and the small pedigree symbol assets add little beyond the better class source tasks.
