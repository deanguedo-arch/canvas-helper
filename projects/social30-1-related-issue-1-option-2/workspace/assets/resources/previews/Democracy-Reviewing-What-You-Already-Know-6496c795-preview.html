Liberal Democracy—Reviewing What You Already Know


List as many characteristics and principles associated with liberal democracy that you can think of. For each characteristic or principle you list, provide a brief example or explanation. Take your time and do as thorough a job as possible. A sample has been provided to get you started.


Principle of Democracy

Example or explanation

right to a fair trial

In a democracy, anyone charged with a crime has the right to a trial by an impartial judge or jury. People are not thrown in prison just because the country’s ruler wants to get rid of them.
