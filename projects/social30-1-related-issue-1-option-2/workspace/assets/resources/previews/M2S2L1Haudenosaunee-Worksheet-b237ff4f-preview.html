By what other name are the Haudenosaunee known?


What were the names of the five tribes that made up the original confederacy?



Approximately how long have the Haudenosaunee lived under the principles of the Great Binding Law?  How have scholars determined this?


What is the Great Binding Law and by what other name(s) is it known?


Find a statement or statements within the Great Binding Law that suggests the Haudenosaunee, like other advocates of liberalism, believed that the protection of property rights was important.  Record the relevant passage(s) below.


Find a statement within Great Binding Law that is consistent with the liberal understanding of the need to protect freedom of religion.  Record the relevant passage below.


What evidence is exists in Benjamin Franklin’s writings to prove that Franklin was impressed by the Haudenosaunee’s approach to governing themselves.  Provide a quotation and explain its significance.


What role did women play in the government of the Haudenosaunee?


When did women in the United States obtain the power to participate fully in the government of the nation?


Imagine that you are a writer who has been asked to contribute to a section of a Social Studies textbook dealing with the topic of liberalism. You have been asked to write the summary for the section dealing with the Haudenosaunee. Write a four to six sentence paragraph that summarizes what you have learned and answers the following question: 


In what ways did the laws, governmental structures and practices of the Haudenosaunee reflect the principles of liberalism?


If possible, post your summary paragraph to a blog or discussion group and review the responses of others working on the same assignment.  Compare your impressions of the Haudenosaunee approach to liberalism with those of your peers.  Feel free to (politely) point out others’ misconceptions, admit your own misconceptions or argue why your summary is “more correct”.
