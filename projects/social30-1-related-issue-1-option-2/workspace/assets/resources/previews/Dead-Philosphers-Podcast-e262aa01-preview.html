Locke: The Social Contract, as I see it, is an agreement between the government and the people they govern.  You see everyone is born with what we call, natural rights... the right to life, liberty and property.   However, people may sometimes disagree on the interpretations of these rights.  For example, two business people might get into a dispute over some property, feeling that they both have a right to it.  Without rules and laws to help them sort out their differences, they might resort to violence.  Someone might even be killed!  The role of government, then, is to write laws and enforce laws that help people get along with each other.  By doing this they fulfill their role of protecting the right to life, liberty and property.
