# Biology 30 — Unit B: Reproduction and Development — Teacher Implementation Guide

Candidate build: 9f0b61ac8bdd17527ccf6169a88c85af662e26d544abd3cc8f0df9773729688e

The course is independent-first and asynchronous. Required learning totals 1200 minutes; optional learning totals 240 minutes and never blocks completion. Learners complete 14 lesson exits, 8 artifact checkpoints, and one submission of the 24-item formative final practice.

All investigations provide safe local models or supplied-data paths. Classroom activities must not be treated as clinical, genetic, or ecological diagnosis. Practice remains non-graded; the secure Unit B test remains a separate native Brightspace activity.
