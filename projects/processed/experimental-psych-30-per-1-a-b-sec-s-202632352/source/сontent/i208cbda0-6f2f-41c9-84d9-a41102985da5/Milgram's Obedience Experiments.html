Milgram's Obedience Experiments
I don't have to think for myself...
Stanley Milgram was interested in the justifications for acts of genocide offered by those accused at the World War II, Nuremberg War Criminal trials.  The defense of the accused was often based on obedience and the excuse that they were just following orders of their superiors. 

 

Getting You Thinking
Background:  During the experiment many participants administering the shocks asked the lead experimenter about who was responsible for any harm resulting from shocks the learner received.  Upon receiving the answer that the experimenter assumed full responsibility, participants seemed to accept the response and continue shocking, even though some were uncomfortable doing so.


Q:  Do you think researchers had the right to expose participants to emotional stress?


Q:  Does the search for knowledge always justify such costs to participants?
