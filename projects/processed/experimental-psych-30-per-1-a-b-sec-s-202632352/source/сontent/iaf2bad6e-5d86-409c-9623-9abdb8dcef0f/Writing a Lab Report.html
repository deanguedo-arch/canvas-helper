Writing a Lab Report

The next task is the writing of the lab report.  Preparing the report requires you to organize, into one large document, the information you have already collected.  If a researcher wants to publish his or her report in a magazine or journal, an abstract must be included at the start of the report.  An abstract is a short summary of the experiment and includes the purpose and/or hypothesis, procedure, and results.  A student who intends to enter an experiment in a science fair must include an abstract with the report.

Lab reports also include an acknowledgement section (if applicable) and a reference section (bibliography).  Each of these sections is on a separate page at the end of the report.   

For help creating a bibliography, you may use an online tool such as Easybib.com or you can follow the format used on the references page found under the Course Information section of this course.
