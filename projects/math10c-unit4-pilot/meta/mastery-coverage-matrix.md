## Evidence and supply rules

- Stages are 0, 25, 50, 75 and 100. A fresh unaided verification establishes 25. Two structured verification receipts with two explicit variation categories in the recent-three prefix establish 50. A distinct transfer and mapped reasoning pair establish 75. A distinct later-session task in another session at least 48 hours after its transfer origin establishes 100.
- A supported/corrected task keeps 100/75/50/25 practice credit separately. It cannot establish fresh mastery.
- Ten successive five-question lesson sets produce 50 distinct mathematical fingerprints per lesson. Even/odd chapter-review partitions cover 16 targets each and all 32 together.
- First work, final work, support provenance, component results, exposure identity, task version, time and session are retained within the measured bounded envelope.
- Optional textbook activities, formal grades and teacher confirmation remain outside this matrix.
