# Retained-work evidence examples

These examples describe the records exercised by the browser and SCORM regressions. They do not contain real learner data.

## Correct first check

- immutable first mathematical submission;
- component results and whole-task qualification;
- check 1 practice credit;
- no prior mathematical help;
- task/contract version, mathematical fingerprint, presentation source, time and session;
- fresh verification receipt for mapped targets.

## Supported correction

- immutable first submission and support-before-submission status;
- actual wrong filled components and blanks recorded separately;
- targeted diagnosis only after the help event saves;
- preserved draft returned after repair;
- final correction retained beside first working;
- reduced practice credit, with no fresh mastery from the corrected identity;
- a different fresh-demonstration action.

## Fourth unsuccessful check

- all four checks and latest component diagnosis retained;
- zero practice credit for that instance;
- focused repair remains available;
- the learner may save a correction without creating check 5 or mastery evidence;
- a different fresh task remains available without resetting earlier work.

## Complete target path

- one fresh unaided verification establishes 25;
- a second structured verification receipt in another explicit variation category establishes 50;
- a distinct transfer plus mapped reasoning evidence in the same presentation/session establishes 75;
- a distinct retention task in another session at least 48 hours after the transfer origin establishes 100.

Compaction represents exact first/final duplicates with reversible `sameAsFirst` references and shares repeated fingerprints/provenance through dictionaries. It does not shorten or discard answers.
