# Remaining 39 lessons: teaching audit

## Scope and evidence

This is a complete reading audit of the current 39 lessons outside accepted CE1-03. It identifies work to do; it does not certify their teaching or mark them revised. The lead commissioned an existing independent second reader, who read the six career and nine workplace lessons and used two bounded read-only readers for the 24 financial and personal-information lessons. The parent reader checked the hidden allowance, unsupported budget claim and partial-month savings issue independently. No learner answers or storage were inspected.

The baseline is hash-bound in `BASELINE.json`. The new six career manuscripts are separate proposals. Canonical course content and previous Before snapshots are preserved. Current web law, tax and product information was not refreshed in this audit: the records remain dated course examples. Source freshness belongs in each later content decision.

## Review questions

Read each lesson from its opening through its final response. Can a student explain the question and why it matters? Are terms and document purposes taught before they are needed? Does the worked example show why each step follows? Does practice transfer a manageable part before the new case? Do the visible facts, fields, hints, feedback, models and sign-offs agree? Does the media teach what its viewing prompt says it teaches? These questions require whole-lesson judgement, not component counts.
