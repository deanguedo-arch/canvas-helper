## Live primary-source checks

- [Alberta youth employment laws](https://www.alberta.ca/youth-employment-laws): selected age, retail/restaurant, school-day and supervision distinctions fit the supplied cases.
- [Alberta refusal of dangerous work](https://www.alberta.ca/refuse-dangerous-work): the lesson uses Alberta's undue-hazard threshold and reporting process rather than treating a general Canadian rights video as the complete provincial process.
- [Alberta Employment Standards complaints](https://www.alberta.ca/file-employment-standards-complaint): ordinary wage complaint timing is distinguished from urgent matters.
- [Alberta Human Rights complaints](https://albertahumanrights.ab.ca/complaints/making-a-complaint/): the one-year filing limit is retained; internal discussion does not replace that deadline.
- [Alberta diploma credential requirements](https://extranet.education.alberta.ca/pasidevnet/Docs/Business/alberta_high_school_diploma.html): current English diploma requirements include the 100-credit total and the separate required categories. The learner-facing [graduation guidance](https://www.alberta.ca/graduation-requirements-credentials-and-credits) remains the appropriate public starting point; individual current CTS course records still need source confirmation.
- [FCAC unauthorized transactions](https://www.canada.ca/en/financial-consumer-agency/services/rights-responsibilities/protection-unauthorized-transactions.html): federally regulated institution investigation duties are scoped; no automatic refund promise is taught.
- [FCAC payday loans](https://www.canada.ca/en/financial-consumer-agency/services/loans/payday-loans.html): the comparison is tied to an illustrative amount and period, not an available personalized offer.
- [CRA TFSA explanation](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/tax-free-savings-account/what.html), [RRSP guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/t4040/rrsps-other-registered-plans-retirement.html), [FHSA deductions](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account/tax-deductions-fhsa-contributions.html), and [RESP payments](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/education/education-savings/managing-plan.html): the course's short account distinctions are consistent at the level taught; full eligibility/tax planning is not supplied or assessed.
- [CRA sales tax place-of-supply rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-place-supply.html) and [Alberta business guidance](https://www.alberta.ca/immigrate-and-start-a-business-in-alberta): support the GST/no-PST clarification, with taxable-supply conditions retained.
- [Alberta consumer complaints](https://www.alberta.ca/file-consumer-complaint): the Consumer Investigations Unit is not a refund or mediation service.
- [Service Canada SIN fraud/data breach guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/services/sin/fraud-data-breaches.html): the current dedicated guidance does not require notifying the SIN program for suspected misuse or a breach. Preserve the course's distinction from older general advice; do not turn notification into an invented required task.
- [Alberta Securities Commission](https://www.asc.ca/): appropriate local authority for the investment-verification naming improvement.
