# CE1-05 Alberta context — revision 2

Checked October 5, 2026. This is a planning teaching case, not an individual graduation audit or complete admissions guide.

## Sources

- [Alberta graduation information](https://www.alberta.ca/graduation-requirements-credentials-and-credits): credits/credentials and graduation versus admission.
- [Current Alberta diploma requirement set](https://extranet.education.alberta.ca/pasidevnet/Docs/Business/alberta_high_school_diploma.html): diploma categories. Linked records confirm three credits each for PE 10 and CALM, ten complementary credits and ten eligible additional 30-level credits.
- [2026–27 public-school graduation guide](https://chhs.cbe.ab.ca/documents/b2e783e7-6b95-436f-a1ff-b778275e5775/CHHS-REG-Booklet-6.pdf): current course-level and credit overview. Its generalizations about pathways are not copied as universal admissions rules.
- [CBe-learn Art](https://cbe-learn.cbe.ab.ca/fine-arts): Art 10, 20, 30; five credits each and prerequisite sequence. A real public-school example, not a promise of every school's offerings.
- [CBe-learn Information Processing](https://cbe-learn.cbe.ab.ca/information-processing): one-credit CTS modules; INF1030, INF1050, INF1070 and INF2050 have no formal prerequisite.
- Alberta's indexed Information Processing program confirms INF3060 requires INF2050. The old education.alberta.ca PDFs now redirect or are unreliable and are not installed as learner links. Current provincial course listing names the modules; its direct download returned 403 in the research tool. The lesson includes the narrow facts students need and requires no download.
