# CALM 10 · teacher assessment masters · review draft

These masters are teacher-only. Brightspace Assignments, Quizzes and Grades hold submissions and marks. Learner-page text boxes are saved drafts. All named people, employers and financial figures below are fictional unless a student is explicitly asked to cite a current public source. No student needs to reveal family finances, a real pay statement, SIN, account number, password, personal credit record or employment history.

## Gradebook and submission setup

Create weighted categories **Applied 60%, Tests 25%, Final portfolio 15%**. Applied items A1–A7 have weights **10, 8, 8, 10, 10, 8, 6** within the whole course; tests T1–T3 **8, 9, 8**; P1 **15**. The category and item settings must be checked with a fictional grade before enrolment. Create a Brightspace Assignment for each A item and P1. Accept a text entry or accessible file; keep large files in Assignments rather than SCORM. Label status in teacher communication as draft saved, submitted, feedback returned, or assessed. A learner-page progress bar has no grade meaning.

For every applied task, give the case materials and prompt below, the source-check requirement, and the relevant analytic criteria from `assessment-and-review.md`. Score each criterion **0–3**: 0 missing or unsafe, 1 partly correct with consequential gaps, 2 correct and supported, 3 correct and supported under a complication. Normalize the criterion total to the task weight. The teacher may use the supplied case for any student; equivalent oral or audio explanation can replace writing when the assessed skill permits it. Do not score personal career prestige, wealth, disclosure, accent or length.

## A1 · career and high-school plan · 10%

**Student case:** Jordan enjoys visual problem solving, has coordinated a school event, and values predictable hours. They are considering two related Alberta occupations with different tasks. Use two current occupation profiles and current program or training pages. You may substitute two occupations that fit a fictional learner you design.

**Submit:** a two-route comparison with daily duties, working conditions, entry routes, training costs or unknowns, Alberta labour-market evidence with measure/region/date, and a high-school sequence that names prerequisites to verify. Include one route that remains viable if the first choice changes. Add a one-paragraph revision after this new information: Jordan’s transport is unavailable on Tuesdays. State what changes and what remains open.

**Criteria:** occupation evidence; accurate source and date; route and prerequisite logic; realistic trade-offs and balance; revision. **Exemplar direction:** a strong answer distinguishes a provincial profile from a one-day local posting, records unknown entry requirements as questions, and revises timing or access without claiming the career is impossible.

## A2 · Alberta workplace rights and safety · 8%

**Student case:** A fictional 15-year-old retail worker receives a schedule for a late shift. The posted schedule and entered time differ by 30 minutes. The worker is also asked to use an unlabelled cleaning liquid without task-specific training. A co-worker says the shift and chemical are both fine because they have done them before.

**Submit:** a case file separating observed facts, missing facts, a current Alberta youth-employment source and a current workplace-safety source. Give a safe immediate response to the chemical task, a factual question about the schedule/time record, an appropriate help route, and a reasoned explanation of why the co-worker’s example does not settle either issue. Do not diagnose a violation without the facts needed for the applicable rule.

**Criteria:** case facts and source scope; hazard recognition; safe action using rights to know, participate and refuse where applicable; records and help route; careful reasoning. **Exemplar direction:** stop the unknown chemical task, request label/training/supervision, preserve schedules and records, check the age/industry/time rule in the current Alberta page, and seek help through the correct process. A broad accusation without source or missing-fact analysis is weaker.

## A3 · supplied online workday simulation · 8%

**Student case:** Use the Prairie Library Hub posting, first-shift note, 10:05 a.m. shift board and fictional lead feedback in lessons CO1-01 through CO1-05. These are complete fictional records. No outside workplace, supervisor signature or stranger interview is required for this assignment.

**Submit:** two truthful skill statements tied to posting duties; an onboarding question about record access; a priority sequence for the printer, activity and private-record request; a two-sentence update to the shift lead; a reflection that uses both praise and growth feedback; and a revised career hypothesis with a next research or practice test.

**Criteria:** application evidence; preparation and safety/privacy judgement; prioritization and communication; accurate reading of feedback; justified revision. **Exemplar direction:** decline to share or access another person’s record without authorization, tell the lead about the printer early, propose a safe activity backup, and distinguish one late report from a permanent ability label. **Curriculum note:** this task does not satisfy `CO1-SP04` supervised participation. See the alignment exception in `assessment-and-review.md`.

## A4 · paycheque, budget and purchase · 10%

**Student case:** Mika’s fictional statement shows 12.5 hours at $18.00, gross $225.00, and listed deductions of $16.00, $10.80 and $3.60. A separate fictional monthly plan has $1,200 available income, $630 essentials, $180 flexible spending, $120 planned saving and $90 transport. A transport change adds $75 monthly. Mika is also comparing a phone plan that costs $35 for six months then $55 for six months with $40 setup, against a $48 monthly plan with no setup. Ignore tax for this comparison and state that assumption.

**Submit:** annotated pay statement with calculations, a revised balanced monthly budget, 12-month costs for both phone plans, and a choice that explains at least one feature besides price. **Check figures:** gross $225.00; deductions $30.40; net $194.60. Original monthly remainder $180; after the transport change, $105 if all other lines stay fixed. Phone A is $580 over 12 months; Phone B is $576. A $4 modeled difference does not settle coverage, cancellation or device terms.

**Criteria:** gross/net reading; arithmetic and units; balanced revision; total cost and terms; justified trade-off. **Common correction:** do not treat a deduction as a budget expense a second time or select a plan from promotional monthly price alone.

## A5 · borrowing comparison and recommendation · 10%

**Student case:** Two fictional fixed-rate ordinary loans each advance $2,400. Offer A has a nominal 12% annual rate divided monthly, 24 equal month-end payments and no fee. Offer B has a nominal 8% annual rate divided monthly, 36 equal month-end payments and a $90 fee paid separately up front. No missed or extra payments; calculate with full precision and display cents. These are classroom models, not lender quotes. If discussing a Canadian mortgage, switch to the stated semi-annual conversion convention and do not reuse the ordinary-loan monthly rate.

**Submit:** payment formula or transparent calculator inputs, monthly payment, total payments plus fee, cost above principal, a recommendation for a fictional borrower with $90/month available, and one sensitivity test changing a rate, term or budget. **Check figures:** A monthly payment about $112.98, total cost about $2,711.43, borrowing cost about $311.43. B monthly payment about $75.21, total including fee about $2,797.46, borrowing cost about $397.46. When a real schedule rounds each payment to cents, the final amount can differ slightly. A has lower total cost but exceeds the stated monthly capacity; B fits the capacity in this simplified case but costs more overall.

**Criteria:** comparable assumptions; correct calculation; affordability versus total cost; treatment of fee and uncertainty; justified recommendation. **Common correction:** the smaller payment is not automatically cheaper and the lower rate is not enough to settle a fee-and-term comparison.

## A6 · saving, investing and revision · 8%

**Student case:** A fictional learner wants $1,200 for an emergency buffer and has variable monthly income. They can transfer $60 in ordinary months but only $25 in a low-income month. They are comparing an accessible savings deposit with a market investment for money possibly needed within one year. A simulation may use illustrative return, fee and inflation assumptions; none is a promise.

**Submit:** a goal and contribution schedule, explanation of why the emergency money needs access, an account-versus-product comparison, a simulator record with assumptions and units, and a revised plan after two low-income months. Do not solve the shortfall by simply assuming a higher return. Identify where current product terms or qualified support would be needed for a real decision.

**Criteria:** goal and cash-flow feasibility; liquidity and product distinction; transparent simulator assumptions; risk/fee/inflation interpretation; reasoned revision. **Exemplar direction:** two $25 months instead of $60 reduce planned contributions by $70; either extend the goal date, adjust other feasible cash-flow lines, or combine both, while protecting essentials.

## A7 · fraud and information protection · 6%

**Student case:** A fictional message claims a bank account will be locked in 20 minutes and provides a link. A second message from a school program asks the student to log in using its known portal without a link. A third marketplace seller asks for a picture of an identity document before discussing a purchase.

**Submit:** classify each as suspicious, legitimate-looking or uncertain; quote the evidence for that classification; show independent verification that does not use a message link; choose a safe response; and make a recovery plan for the changed case in which the fictional learner already entered a reused password. Explain which organization or current official reporting source applies to the chosen event. Do not use the blanket claim that the CRA never calls: genuine CRA contact is possible, and verification must use a trusted independent route.

**Criteria:** indicator analysis; uncertainty and independent verification; safe response; recovery order; source and reporting route. **Exemplar direction:** open a known banking app or independently typed site, do not use the urgent link, change a compromised password on the genuine service, review other accounts where reused, and contact the relevant institution using known contact details.

## P1 · integrated final portfolio · 15%

Use the new Morgan life-change case in the learner portfolio page. Select earlier A1, A3 and at least two financial pieces as evidence, then add a one-page synthesis that explicitly revises a pathway, work decision and financial plan. Include a before/after decision table, source dates, calculation assumptions and one unresolved question. The final mark evaluates **new integration and transfer**, not the same pages a second time. A short teacher conference may be written, audio or live. Ask one follow-up about why a revision was made and one about what evidence would reverse it.

## Teacher feedback and reassessment

Return one observed strength, one specific error or omission, and one next action linked to a lesson. For calculations, point to the line or assumption that needs correction. For legal or financial claims, request the dated source and scope rather than supplying an unsupported rule. Permit a revised applied submission after targeted practice; use a changed fictional case or the alternate test form for reassessment. Record old and new attempts in Brightspace. Do not clear browser drafts to force a resubmission.

## Formal test masters and imports

The teacher-only `formal-test-bank.json` holds six distinct forms: T1-A/B for careers and workplaces, T2-A/B for everyday money and borrowing, and T3-A/B for saving, investing and protection. Each form has 16 multiple-choice items and four written responses, 28 marks total. The questions use new cases rather than copying learner practice prompts. Each multiple-choice item has an explanation; each written item has a marking guide. Keep this bank outside the learner workspace.

Run `node projects/calm10-2026-draft/meta/build-test-imports.cjs` from the repository root to regenerate the six files under `meta/brightspace-imports/`. The local builder checks the six-form structure, item counts, IDs, distinct stems and choices, even answer-position distribution, and the application/reasoning mark threshold. It does **not** prove that Brightspace accepts the CSVs. Import one form at a time into a school test course, inspect question text, choices, correct-answer weights, feedback, marks and written-response scoring, then test a fictional student attempt before scheduling the live quizzes. Set course weights T1 8%, T2 9%, T3 8% in Brightspace, and use the B forms for reassessment after targeted review.

Review every dated legal or financial premise and the teacher marking guides before opening the tests. A local CSV export is not a published or graded Brightspace assessment.
