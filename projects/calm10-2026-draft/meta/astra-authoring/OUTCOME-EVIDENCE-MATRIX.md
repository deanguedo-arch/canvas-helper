# Outcome and evidence matrix

This maps the 62 locally indexed skills/procedures to the new authored tasks. It does not certify a learner or claim the new tasks are already in the live course. Source: official draft PEW0770, published September 22, 2026, read September 28, 2026 at https://curriculum.learnalberta.ca/curriculum/en/gfc/CALM/PEW0770 . Local IDs are indexing aids; the official curriculum remains the authority.

## Reading the statuses

- **authored-inquiry / authored-scenario**: complete instruction and an observable response have been authored; implementation and learner performance remain unverified.
- **authored-tool**: supplied cases and outputs are complete; the specified interactive calculator must be implemented and checked.
- **conditional-personal**: scenario learning exists and the portfolio invites actual low-disclosure reflection, but fiction alone does not meet a personal-application requirement.
- **simulation-only**: a simulated experience supports learning; the corresponding real experience is absent.
- **unmet-accepted**: explicitly excluded by the user, not silently satisfied by another task.

All external live links are optional for the authored lesson. Required investigation uses the dated in-course records. Dated records must be rechecked before any claim of current real-world advice. CO1-SP04 is an explicit accepted gap; CO1-SP05/06 remain limited to simulation. CE1-SP04 does not establish a verified personal school plan. No online sequence guarantees that every student reaches every outcome.

## Reconciliation rule

After each implementation batch, update the operational outcome-coverage.json with these evidence locations and honest status/limits. Preserve its historical mapping/version. Do not use teacherReview: required to create a hidden learner dependency; metadata review status is distinct from the self-contained course flow. Keep the existing official statements, then cross-check them against the current official draft at a curriculum version change. Knowledge and understanding have a separate inventory in KNOWLEDGE-AND-UNDERSTANDING.md.
