## Understanding across the course

- Exploration and evidence can improve a plan while leaving uncertainty: CE1-01–07, CO1-05 and portfolio Parts 1/4.
- Responsibilities, rights and safe action depend on context and authority: CO2-01–04 and CO1-02–04.
- Financial choices depend on resources, timing, goals and trade-offs: FL1/FL2/FL3, especially the new independent cases.
- Protecting information and verifying claims supports financial security: FL4-01–05.
- Revision is part of competent decision-making: every guided activity, each independent model/self-check and the final portfolio.

The inventory names taught content and evidence opportunities. Rendering, usability trials and student learning results are still required before making stronger claims.
