Existing reference design: applied tasks A1–A7 total 60% (10, 8, 8, 10, 10, 8, 6 respectively), tests total 25%, portfolio 15%. Retain those references and weights in the historical metadata. The requested self-contained learner experience has no required teacher assessment, external submission or supervised experience.

Completing a lesson or checking its criteria does not generate the applied-task or portfolio grade. Do not convert text presence into a score of 100. If a later request requires automatic graded assessment, it needs its own assessment design and validation; that policy was not authored here.

No artificial timer or mandatory video duration manufactures these hours. Some learners will need substantially more or less time. The long packets include author specifications and feedback for every option; students should not be forced to read all implementation notes or all distractor feedback before continuing.

## Workload recording during implementation and review

For each lesson record: word count of learner-visible core teaching; document-reading time; first practice attempt; revision; independent writing/calculation; optional media time separately. Label estimated versus observed. Do not count hidden models and every distractor rationale as mandatory linear reading.

For a future voluntary learner trial, observe whether the student can:
1. State the expected product after Start here.
2. Find the evidence without being told the location.
3. Explain a document term in their own words.
4. Use feedback to make a specific revision.
5. Finish and find the saved/completed work after reopening.

Record the exact point of hesitation, task duration and whether the cause was missing facts, vocabulary, navigation, calculation or writing. Revise the task at that point; do not merely add another generic introductory paragraph. No learner trial has been conducted in this authoring pass.

## Scope of acceptance

The package authors all planned lesson tasks and permits asynchronous progress. It does not guarantee all learners reach every curriculum outcome, create an actual personal school plan, fulfill supervised participation or certify assessment grades. These distinctions remain in the author metadata while the learner flow is clear and self-contained.
