# CALM 10 assessment and teacher operations · build draft

This package is an authoring draft for PEW0770 (published 2026-09-22). Brightspace Assignments, Question Library, teacher feedback, and grades remain the authority. A response saved in the learner page is a **draft**, never a submitted or teacher-assessed item. The school must approve field-testing and credit reporting before use.

## Common rubric scale

Score each named criterion 0–3. **0:** absent or unsafe; **1:** partially correct, with important gaps; **2:** correct and supported for the case; **3:** accurate, well supported, and responsive to a complication. Give one concrete next step when a criterion is below 2. Reassessment follows targeted practice and a changed case or alternate test form. Never score response length, personal wealth, family disclosure or career prestige.

## Task setup and exemplars

- **A1:** Supply two fictional Alberta destinations, such as an apprenticeship route and a technical-program route. Model an evidence table with source title, URL, accessed date, entry conditions and unresolved questions. Feedback identifies missing verification rather than choosing a career.
- **A2:** Give a fictional 15-year-old worker a changing schedule, unclear deduction and unlabelled cleaning product. Ask what is known, what needs current Alberta verification, what is safe now, and who can help. A model response separates the immediate safety step from a legal conclusion.
- **A3:** Supply the Prairie Library Hub posting, onboarding note, shift board, and fictional lead feedback inside the course. Students choose safe actions, write a concise supervisor update, interpret the feedback, and revise a career hypothesis. Grade the reasoning and revision; do not award a supervised-participation claim.
- **A4:** Supply a fictional biweekly pay stub with gross $720, deductions $104.60 and net $615.40. Students distinguish deductions from expenses, then adapt a budget when transport costs rise. An exemplar explains its pay-period conversion; exactly doubling biweekly income is only a rough monthly estimate.
- **A5:** Compare a $2,400 loan at nominal 12% with 24 versus 36 equal monthly payments and no fees: about $112.98/$311.43 total interest versus $79.71/$469.72. If another offer has a $75 fee, include it separately. Verify the convention and rounding; these are illustrations, not quotes.
- **A6:** Give a fictional learner a $1,200 emergency goal and variable part-time income. Compare a savings account with an investment product at the same horizon. A strong revision changes the contribution before assuming a higher return.
- **A7:** Use fictional versions of a spoofed bank message, a legitimate reminder and an ambiguous marketplace offer. A strong response uses a known independent channel and says what to do if credentials were already shared.
- **P1:** New scenario: a training program changes an admission requirement while transport and part-time income also change. Students update pathway, work plan and budget, identify prior evidence that changed, and defend one revision in a short teacher conference or equivalent written/audio exchange.

## Tests and formative checks

Current draft: 3 tests × 2 forms × 20 items = **120 formal items** in teacher-only `formal-test-bank.json`, plus **240 separate formative choices** in the learner lessons (six per lesson). The local builder generated six Brightspace CSV files and checked structure, item counts, duplicate stems, and the application/reasoning mark threshold. Answer keys, rationales and written marking guides stay outside the learner bundle. **Form equivalence, current-fact review, a school Brightspace import and fictional student attempts are still required before opening test slots or recording grades.**

## Curriculum alignment exception: supervised participation

The user requested a course that can be completed online with supplied scenarios. A3 is therefore a simulated workday assessment. The draft procedure `CO1-SP04` requires participation in a supervised school or community work-related experience. A simulation does **not** satisfy that procedure. The school must decide, before claiming full field-test alignment or awarding credit under the draft, whether it will provide an approved supervised route or document a permitted variance. Keep this exception visible in the outcome ledger and course review; do not ask individual online students to arrange their own placement.

## Pacing and checkpoints

Use 15 weeks at about five planned hours per week as a starting schedule. The 75 hours include instruction, practice, supplied simulations, research, assessment, feedback and revision; they are not a provincial seat-time claim. Check A1 after CE1, A2 before the workplace simulation, A3 after the changing-shift case, A4–A6 during financial planning, and P1 at the end. Intervene when a student has drafts without submission or repeated calculation errors.
