# Online supervised work route · school implementation proposal

**Status:** Ready for school review; not activated in the learner course. The supplied Prairie Library Hub simulation teaches decisions and provides A3 evidence, but does not establish participation in a real supervised experience under draft procedure CO1-SP04. This proposal creates a fully online route if the school can operate and verify it.

## The actual work

The school posts a short queue of genuine, bounded tasks for a real school or community recipient. Examples:

1. **Accessibility check:** review a school-owned, public learning page using a supplied checklist and deliver a prioritized issue report. The student reports observations, not medical or learner information.
2. **Resource guide:** make a one-page plain-language guide to a public service, with current citations and a recipient request. The recipient uses or formally reviews the guide.
3. **Digital communications task:** create two versions of a school event notice from a real brief, including accessible text and alt text, for recipient feedback.
4. **Inventory or planning task:** organize non-personal, school-supplied materials into a usable checklist or schedule, with a change request from the recipient.

A task must serve a real need, have genuine deliverables, require learner decisions, and receive feedback from a school-approved adult who observes or reviews the work. A fabricated case, self-selected hobby project, automated score or merely viewing a workplace video is not this route.

## Student path inside Brightspace

1. **Choose:** select a published task brief in the school Brightspace course. The brief states the recipient, deliverable, time window, permitted tools, boundaries and named supervisor role. Students do not have to solicit an outside placement or provide a stranger's contact details.
2. **Prepare:** complete CO2 rights/safety and CO1 preparation lessons; submit a brief plan covering task steps, privacy boundaries, a question for the supervisor and how to ask for help.
3. **Work:** carry out the approved task online. Keep a short decision log and draft files in Brightspace Assignments or the school's approved collaborative space. The supervisor gives one checkpoint response; the student makes a visible revision.
4. **Verify:** the approved adult records the observed task, date/approximate participation time, one demonstrated skill and one growth action in a school-controlled record. The learner does not upload private third-party contact information into SCORM.
5. **Reflect:** submit the deliverable, feedback record, decision log and revision of the CE1 career plan. The course evaluates learning from evidence; the teacher separately records whether authentic participation occurred.

The school may offer a text-first task, accessible tool route, flexible schedule or supported live check-in. Keep equivalent rigor without making transport, money, disability disclosure, personal employment or family networks a condition of participation.

## School setup and acceptance

Before publishing a task, the school names the adult supervisor and recipient, confirms age-appropriate responsibilities, relevant consent/insurance and safe-use requirements, approved communication and privacy tools, a response-time expectation, and a no-fault replacement task if the recipient becomes unavailable. The teacher opens a Brightspace Assignment for preparation, one for the deliverable/reflection, and a restricted verification record. A single teacher may supervise several asynchronous school tasks if they can actually review student decisions and give feedback.

For CO1-SP04 review, require evidence of **real assigned responsibilities, real supervision during the task, actual participation, observed feedback, and school verification**. A finished document alone is not sufficient. The school must decide whether this remote route satisfies the draft field-test and credit rules; the course should retain the exception until that decision and one pilot are documented.

## Workload and connection to the learner course

The course's ten-hour CO1 module currently budgets teaching, simulation, application and feedback. If activated, the school can replace part of its simulation/practice time with about six planned hours of authentic remote participation and reflection, subject to the actual task. A3 remains the common online simulation assessment for equitable case reasoning. Authentic route verification is recorded separately from A3's mark and from the learner page's saved-draft counter.
