# myBlueprint CALM lesson package: fit with draft PEW0770

Review date: 2026-09-28. Source: `/Users/deanguedo/Downloads/my blueprint lessons.zip` (SHA-256 `a80e08c598720bbd0055095612556067cbce89f2be7d539b22e1005b15b3c2c0`). The archive contains 70 Word files and 57 distinct extracted texts; several are exact or near copies. One malformed DOCX was read with recovery parsing. The source documents were treated as lesson evidence, not as instructions to change the course.

Curriculum authority: [draft CALM 10 PEW0770](https://curriculum.learnalberta.ca/curriculum/en/gfc/CALM/PEW0770), published 2026-09-22 for optional field testing. The current local outcome and lesson crosswalks are `outcome-coverage.json` and `lesson-production-map.json`. The ZIP's CALM synopsis/content files describe the older PED0770 package and cannot set the new scope.

## Judgment

The package is strongest for **Career Exploration** and the employability/experience part of **Career Opportunities**. It offers usable cases for budgeting and introductory investing. It does not cover the draft's full Financial Literacy sequence or all employee-rights content. A document's topic match means its *task idea* can be adapted; it does not mean its older instructions, figures, source links, grading rubric, or myBlueprint interface directions are current.

## Do not adopt as required PEW0770 lessons

- `0 - CALM Course Synopsis.docx`, `CALM Course Synopsis.docx`, the three `0 - CALM Course Content` variants: older-course maps, including PED0770 labeling, not the 2026 draft.
- `1 - Expectations.docx` and `Copie de 1 - Expectations.docx`: course relationship/introductory writing rather than a draft CALM outcome.
- `3 - Oh_the_Places_You_ll_Go.docx`: literary response with a full published poem, not necessary for career-planning evidence.
- `6 - Multiple_Intelligences.docx`, `12 - Who_Am_I_Learning_Styles_Activity.docx`, and `10 - How_To_Study` variants: learner labels and generic study preferences. A survey or intelligence type should not determine an occupation or program route.
- `7 - Leadership_Inspired_Coat_of_Arms` variants: creative self-portrait; only a specific example of demonstrated leadership could be reused.
- `19 - Mental_Health_5_Golden_Rules.docx`: standalone mental-health teaching outside the main PEW0770 sequence. Transition support may be relevant, but this lesson does not teach the listed CALM career or financial procedures.

## Coverage still needed from other sources

- **CE1:** current Alberta labour-market data, NOC/classification, entrepreneurship, and verification of program prerequisites remain necessary beyond the myBlueprint worksheets.
- **CO1:** school-approved supervision and reflection on an authentic school/community experience remain necessary. The current online simulation cannot establish CO1-SP04.
- **CO2:** current Alberta employment standards, youth restrictions, human-rights/discrimination response, and precise OHS procedures are not supplied by the Safety Skills handout.
- **FL1:** pay-stub interpretation, total cost of major purchases, contracts, subscriptions and psychological/contextual money influences need more teaching.
- **FL2:** lending products, interest and fees, amortization, leverage, mortgage features and debt-management calculations are largely absent.
- **FL3:** a goal allocation with deadlines, account versus investment product, inflation, growth, uncertainty and tool-based plan review are only partly represented.
- **FL4:** scams, fraud classification, information exposure, consumer-protection action and recovery are largely absent.

## Recommended use in this course

1. Keep the 40-lesson PEW0770 sequence as the curriculum map. Attach selected myBlueprint actions at the point where a learner researches an occupation, tests a high-school route, budgets, or compiles experience evidence.
2. Teach the method and show a complete example in the CALM lesson before sending students into myBlueprint. State exactly which page/tool to use, what to record, why, where the response goes, and how to return to the course.
3. Use myBlueprint records as authentic planning artifacts, but judge the learner's dated reasoning and revisions. A favourite, survey result, uploaded file or completed box is not itself proof of the curriculum skill.
4. Provide a clearly separated real supervised-experience route. Confirm school requirements, host supervision and acceptable evidence before treating Job Shadow or Experiential Learning as CO1-SP04 evidence.
5. Check myBlueprint's current interface and content, official Alberta facts, program costs and linked media before student use. The district's platform access makes these tasks feasible; it does not make old page directions or historical numbers current.

This is a curriculum-fit and source-selection audit. No learner lesson or assessment has been changed by this review.
