## 4. Student reflection and plan revision

1. Describe one task you performed and the skill it required. Give a specific example.
2. What did you expect before participating? What did you actually observe? Explain the difference.
3. How did supervision, training or a safety rule affect your work?
4. Which feedback will you use next, and what is the concrete action?
5. Revisit your CE1 pathway plan. Keep, change or postpone a step and explain the evidence for the revision.
6. What remains uncertain? Identify a source or person who could help verify it.

Teacher records participation verification separately from the quality of reflection. Students may submit an equivalent written, recorded or teacher-conference reflection when the assessed skill permits it.
