When should leaders choose what is best for their nation over what is best for the world?  When the Tar Sands are a major contributor to global warming, should Alberta leaders choose jobs in Alberta or a healthier planet?   What gets more votes?  Who makes the money?  What are the motivations for these decisions? 

 And what happens when nationalism goes unchecked?  Ultranationalist governments are on the rise around the world.  Can we look to the past to see what mistakes leaders made as ultranationalism grew in Spain, Italy, Germany and much of Europe causing World War II and the Holocaust?
