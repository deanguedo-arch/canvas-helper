You have access to both Part A and Part B of your Unit Exams whenever you are ready!

There are not specific days on which you must complete the exams. However, if you have due dates or there are suggested due dates, it is in your best interest to complete exams on or before those dates.

The exams are in each of the Units, but they are not visible YET.

The exams become visible when you have met the requirements.

To access Part A:

     Submit ALL Related Issue assignment booklets: number of booklets will vary for each Related Issue. 

              Only then will you see Part A.

To access Part B:

     Complete and submit Part A exam

             Part B will then be visible

**NOTE: Once you read the instructions and click START, your time begins.

  • You will have a restricted amount of time to complete each test. There will be reminders about the time and there is a buffer of 15 mins after time runs out.
  • You will NOT be able to access the test again. 
  • Make sure you are ready to write when you start the test.

Contact your teacher if you require special access or special conditions.
