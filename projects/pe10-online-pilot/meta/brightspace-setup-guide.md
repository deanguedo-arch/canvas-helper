# PE10 online pilot — Brightspace setup guide

## Pilot boundary

This build is preview-only. Do not upload it for learner use until the teacher approves the exact course, assessment model, activity-dimension rules, safety approach, privacy approach, rubrics, and linked health resources.

The learner shell stores text-only drafts locally. Brightspace remains authoritative for assignment submissions, uploaded evidence, grades, and teacher feedback.

## Create five Brightspace assignments

Create one assignment for each item below. Keep each at 10 points and use the matching rubric shown in the learner course.

1. Personal Activity and Safety Plan
2. Movement Skill and Cooperation Observation
3. Fitness, Fuel, Hydration, and Recovery Plan
4. Health and Media Literacy Inquiry
5. CPR/AED Awareness and Final Active-Living Reflection

Recommended Brightspace settings:

- Accept PDF or DOCX for the printed assignment and CSV or PDF for log evidence where appropriate.
- Keep teacher feedback and official marks in Brightspace.
- Do not ask learners to upload medical records, diagnoses, body measurements, calorie logs, or verifier contact information.
- Provide an alternate submission route when a standard evidence format creates an accessibility or privacy barrier.
- State clearly that CPR content is awareness only and is not certification.

## Submission links

All learner submission buttons are intentionally disabled. Add no placeholder or guessed Dropbox URLs.

## Activity evidence review

- Confirm that the learner has at least 3,000 logged minutes.
- Review the five-hours-per-dimension and 30-hour maximum checks as evidence-readiness rules, not automatic grades.
- When an alternate evidence plan is approved, document the agreement privately in Brightspace and ask the learner to record only a brief plan reference in the course.
- Evaluate honesty, relevance, reflection, progression, safety reasoning, and outcome evidence. Do not grade the learner's body, ability level, equipment access, or comparison with others.

## Safety and policy check before release

- Recheck EIPS Administrative Procedure 317 and current Safety Guidelines for Alberta Schools.
- Confirm local expectations for activity approval, supervision, concussion reporting, return to physical activity, community-based evidence, and emergency response.
- Replace or remove any course wording that conflicts with current division direction.
- Confirm accommodation, accessibility, privacy, and records-handling expectations with the appropriate school or division staff.

## Promotion sequence after teacher approval

Promotion is a separate task. Enable Studio Edit and SCORM 2004 only after the approval record identifies the exact accepted build. Then prove Apply/reload/Undo, run the exact-head readiness gate, export, verify ZIP integrity and the SCORM bridge, and test save/restore in Brightspace with a learner account.
