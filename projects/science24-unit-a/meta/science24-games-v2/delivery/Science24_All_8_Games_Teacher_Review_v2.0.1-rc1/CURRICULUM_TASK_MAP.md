# Science 24 task and curriculum evidence map

Official authority: https://education.alberta.ca/media/159714/sc1424.pdf (program page locators A31-32, B36-37, C42-43, D48-49). The supplied crosswalk uses design shorthand and is retained as reference. This map limits claims to actual learner responses. The official Unit D title is Motion, Change and Transportation Safety; the supplied course uses Safety in Transportation.

These games are supplemental practice. Worked examples teach but do not establish independent evidence. Self-review checkboxes are not teacher-rated explanations. Teacher verifies wording, course level and alignment before approval.

## Excluded or partial coverage

A1/A4 technological and environmental consequences: context only; no independent full outcome claim. B4 fossil-fuel formation/impact: no primary task. C5 longevity: contextual sanitation only, no independent longevity analysis. C1 health determinants and D1 scientific evidence/opinion/regulations: partial context, not full claims. Nutrition, historical chemistry, complex inheritance, ethics, legal driving rules and the rest of the official program remain in course teaching.

No secure course assessments, exam wording or assessment answer keys are included. Game solutions cover only these authored supplemental scenarios.
