SCIENCE 24 — A FRESH COMMUNITY EVENT
Standalone follow-up practice. Use your own calculations and units.

The centre needs four 8 W lamps for five hours, two 45 W laptops for
three hours each, and one 170 W projector for 1.5 hours. Every service
must run for its full stated time. The event's cap is 0.650 kWh.

1. Calculate the energy for each service and the original event total.
2. An alternative projector uses 130 W and provides the same stated
   service. Can that change fit the cap while keeping every service?
   Calculate the revised total, energy saved and remaining allowance.
3. Explain why simply shortening a required service would not meet the
   event's requirements. Distinguish power from energy in your answer.
4. A separate motor receives 1000 J and transfers 650 J to useful motion.
   Calculate its efficiency and the energy transferred to other outputs.
   Explain why a device's low power rating alone cannot answer this.
