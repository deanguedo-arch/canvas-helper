Prepared 7 October 2026 for the B2 playable implementation.

## User-supplied authorities

1. `Science24_Games_Visual_Standard_v0.1.md` and `Science24_Games_Visual_Reference_A1_v0.1.png`: approved colour family, typography direction, panel hierarchy and scientific/content separation.
2. `Science24_All_8_Games_Production_Handoff_v2.0.zip`, B2 design/state/scenario/engine files: scoped learning purposes and starter scenarios. These were inspected, not treated as completed runtime code.
3. `D2LExport_156563_26-27 _ S1 _ Science 24 _ Per 1(A) _ Sec 3_202610725.zip`: the current user-supplied course boundary. The B2-relevant files were extracted for inspection; copyrighted textbook/workbook pages and secure assessment contents are not redistributed in this game.

## Exact course locators inspected

- `Content/unit b science 24.pdf`, Chapter 6, section 6.3, PDF pp. 21–23 / printed pp. 108–110: power as a rate; W = J/s; energy = power × time; Wh and kWh.
- Same file, section 6.4, PDF pp. 24–26 / printed pp. 111–113: useful output, efficiency and energy conservation choices. The formula at PDF p. 25 / printed p. 112 is useful output divided by input, multiplied by 100.
- Same file, section 5.5, PDF p. 12 / printed p. 94: input equals useful plus other output.
- `S24 Unit B Workbook 2023.pdf`, PDF pp. 13–15: energy calculations, unit distinctions, efficiency and conservation/application demands. Task forms informed new examples; the supplied questions are not copied.
- `Content/Science 24 Formula Sheet.pdf`, PDF p. 1: energy, power, kWh and efficiency relationships and unit conversion context.

Relevant formula pages were inspected as rendered images as well as parsed text. The complete source texts were not used as infallible statements of current conditions. In particular, historical electricity-generation descriptions and a watt-per-hour wording error in the textbook were not carried into the game.

## Alberta outcome claim

The supplied project brief and crosswalk place B2 within **Science 24 Unit B, general outcome 2**, with the official program locator at printed/PDF p. 37. This implementation targets electrical-device energy use, rate, useful output and efficiency. It does not claim to cover the whole outcome, generation technologies, all of Unit B, or the full Science 24 course.

Official program locator recorded in the project: `https://education.alberta.ca/media/159714/sc1424.pdf`.

An attempt to open that public URL during this implementation returned a fetch error; this package does not pretend that a fresh official-document retrieval or curriculum-edition audit passed. The outcome locator is inherited from the user's supplied design sources, while the numerical/task content was checked against the supplied current course materials.

## External scientific check

U.S. Energy Information Administration, **Measuring electricity**:
`https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/electricity/measuring-electricity.php`

Accessed in this work on 7 October 2026. Used to cross-check the distinction between watts/kilowatts as power and watt-hours/kilowatt-hours as energy, 1 kW = 1,000 W, and calculation of energy over time. No live electricity rates, real product specifications or tariff advice are included.

## Authored content and changes

All mission data, service floors, equipment equivalences and narrative contexts are illustrative teaching assumptions. The transfer scenario was newly authored to avoid repeating P03's numbers. The optional five-device lab uses the approved visual's general setting and device categories with corrected, unambiguous per-device arithmetic. See `RULES_AND_CONTENT.md`.

Open explanations are not automatically graded. Automated evidence establishes tested behaviour and selected calculations; it does not establish educational effectiveness, mastery, exam success or a teacher content sign-off.
