# Source and editorial decisions

## Source hierarchy and pedagogical reference
The better Science 24 D2L export ending `202692353.zip` supplies the real Unit A textbook, workbook, guided notes and formula sheet. Unit A teaching follows the fourteen-topic sequence, not the older Power/Time/Energy subject matter. The latter v3 HTML supplies the **lesson progression and shell reference**: context, developed explanation, worked reasoning, error diagnosis, supported attempts, a changed-case example, independent practice, transfer, later retrieval, and required check.

The fourteen main lessons are rewritten at a first-time Science 24 learner's level. New scenarios, numerical values and application questions are authored practice, not reported experimental observations. Original textbook question pages retain their source wording, figures and context. The selected 21 question-bearing pages are not claimed to be every eligible textbook question. A current Alberta outcome-code compliance audit and textbook question-by-question exhaustiveness review are not included in this release.

## Important distinctions
- The source PDF contains 54 physical pages with **gaps in its printed-page numbering**. An unnumbered historical MSDS insert is physical page 15. It is not fabricated printed page 20 or 21. The reader uses an explicit map, not a constant offset.
- Historical MSDS and older safety material remain unchanged in the embedded source. Lesson 2 explicitly distinguishes current SDS/WHMIS terminology and provides official CCOHS/Health Canada references.
- Household symbols and workplace WHMIS pictograms are not treated as interchangeable. The authored safety diagram is a decision sequence, not an invented certification pictogram.
- Greenhouse teaching distinguishes energy absorption/re-emission from the limitations of the glass-greenhouse analogy. The NASA explanatory reference is linked in the lesson. Historical source framing is not silently presented as a current scientific debate.
- Alloys are described as metallic mixtures that may contain non-metal elements; steel's carbon is not called a metal.
- Coefficients count whole particles or formula units as appropriate. The specific water example correctly refers to molecules.
- Hypothetical temperature, mass, emissions and energy-budget data are labelled as practice/model data. Percent reduction is explicitly taught as a mathematical application rather than claimed to be an original observation.
- Practical procedures in the embedded original material are **not home assignments**. Course tasks are based on supplied observations, written reasoning and supported browser inputs.
- Workbook answer keys were used as source-checking material only and are not included in the learner resource library or package.

## Assessment disposition
The build contains a finite **authored** optional bank and new required checks. Required prompts are disjoint from the optional bank and embedded practice. It does not claim a lossless import of every question-library or quiz record in the D2L export. It does not replace secure school assessments or the school's grading scheme.
