Submission Instructions

For Digital Assignment Booklets: Submit from Google Drive

Step 0: You only need to do this step once in Brightspace. If you have already done this, skip to Step 1. If you have not done this, scroll down to the section that shows you a step-by-step guide on how to link your Google Account to your Brightspace Account.

Step 1: In the dropbox, select Choose Existing

Step 2: Select Google Drive (If you do not see "Google Drive" make sure you do Step 0!)

Step 3: Search for the file you want to submit and hit Add.

Step 4: Hit Submit

For Print Assignment Booklets: Scan using Adobe Scan

Step 1: Scan using the Adobe Scan Instructions

Step 2: In the drop box, select Upload

Step 3: Locate the file you want to submit and hit Add.

Step 4: Hit Submit

Linking a Google Account to a Brightspace Account

Step-by-Step Guide

1. Scroll down on the main page and find the Google Apps widget and click "Link to your Google Apps account".

2. Ensure that the email address is correct then click on "Create Link".

3. You will be returned to the main screen where you will need to click on "Authorize".

4. After you do you will be prompted to log into your Google account.

5. Click on "Allow".

6. You should now be logged into your account. You can verify this by seeing your Google Drive listed in the Google Apps widget at the bottom of your Dashboard.
