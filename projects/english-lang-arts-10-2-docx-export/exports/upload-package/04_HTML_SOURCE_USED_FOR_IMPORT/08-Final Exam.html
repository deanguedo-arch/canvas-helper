Final Exam

All students attending an Elk Island Public School are required to complete the Common Exams for English 10-2.

Review

Your exams have a Part A and Part B.  Part A focuses on writing and Part B focuses on reading comprehension.  Make sure to review how to write a Visual Reflection (mainly learned in Unit 1) and review the reading strategies you learned in Unit 2 and Unit 4.

The final exam can be written once all course assignments and assessments have been completed; you do not have to wait until the last week of the semester.

The exam must be written at Next Step. There is no option to complete the exam online.

When you are ready to complete your exams, please speak with your teacher.
