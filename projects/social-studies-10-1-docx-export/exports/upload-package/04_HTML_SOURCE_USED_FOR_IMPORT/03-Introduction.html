Cartoon and Image Analysis

Methods of Cartoon and Image Analysis

In Social Studies you will be expected to interpret political cartoons and images. These will be  put in assignments and on exams, which will then require you to think about what they "are trying to say".

Why do teachers do this?

Let's face it…it's not that hard to memorize a few facts about a school subject. Basic recall (remembering things someone told you) is not a very advanced way of thinking. To get to higher levels of thinking, you have to take a piece of information and evaluate it. An excellent way to do that is to analyze and interpret an editorial cartoon or image.

SWAP Method

Symbols:      Describe the symbols you see drawn
Words:        What do the words tell you?
Action:       What action is taking place?
Point:         What point is the cartoonist making?

Paragraph:    Write a paragraph which analyzes the cartoon based on the SWAP observations above.

Understand:

1. Go to the handout SWAP and first, take a look at the cartoon.
2. Step by step go through the questions on the handout.

Saving cartoons from the website mentioned above

When you find the cartoon you want to use, make sure you left click the thumbnail of the cartoon.  This will open a bigger version.  Then right-click this bigger image and select Save Image As from the list that appears on your screen after right clicking.  This will give you the good quality image rather than the tiny poor quality thumbnail image.
