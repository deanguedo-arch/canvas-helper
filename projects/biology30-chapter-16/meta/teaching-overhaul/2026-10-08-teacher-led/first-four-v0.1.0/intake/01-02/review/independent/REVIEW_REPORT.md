## Exact science and source reconciliation

- One unreplicated chromosome has one DNA molecule. One replicated chromosome has two sister chromatids/two DNA molecules before sister separation. In the typical human G1 example,46 chromosomes/46 DNA molecules become46 replicated chromosomes/92 chromatids after S. DNA amount doubles; the two homologous sets remain. At sister separation each sister becomes a chromosome; a still-undivided parent count must be distinguished from the count in one eventual daughter. The candidate explicitly gives that boundary.
- Model worked examples: L1 has two types and two sets, n=2 and2n=4; replication gives4 replicated chromosomes and8 sister chromatids before separation. L2 starts2n=6, one basic set3; after S it has6 replicated chromosomes/12 chromatids; normal division yields6 chromosomes per daughter. - Chromatin is DNA plus associated proteins, not a separate hereditary molecule and not a stage when DNA is absent. Condensation changes packaging; copying changes amount. The protected one-DNA-molecule glossary shorthand is visibly qualified for replicated chromosomes.
