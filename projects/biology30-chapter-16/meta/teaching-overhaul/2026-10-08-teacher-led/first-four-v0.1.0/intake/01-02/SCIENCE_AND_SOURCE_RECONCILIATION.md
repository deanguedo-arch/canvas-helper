# Scientific boundaries

DNA condensation and DNA replication are different processes. Chromatin remains DNA plus associated proteins; visibility is not DNA first appearing. The short native chromosome definition describes an unreplicated molecule; visible teaching explicitly reconciles the replicated two-sister state. A whole chromatid is one copied chromosome structure, not merely an upper/lower arm. Homologues share gene positions and can share or differ in alleles; sisters originate by copying one chromosome.

Ploidy counts complete chromosome sets, not condensed shapes. Karyotype interpretation uses length, centromere position and banding together, not length alone. A band can contain many genes; expected chromosome count cannot certify all sequences/conditions. TypicalXX/XY source models do not justify inferences beyond the chromosome evidence. X/Y partial correspondence is retained. Some cells do not actively cycle; a stage-frequency argument is bounded to a suitable actively cycling sample, not every healthy tissue or a universal90%clock.

Normal mitosis and cytokinesis are related but distinct and can overlap. Nuclear division without cytokinesis can leave two nuclei within one cytoplasm. Growth/maintenance/repair are normal uses of cell division; loss of regulation can contribute to cancer, but rapid division alone is not a diagnosis.
