Worked example

Use the two images to explain a mechanism

Compare the teacher's animal-cell illustration with the plant micrograph above. Explain how each cell becomes two cells, using its boundary as evidence.

  1. Locate what changes. In the animal illustration the middle narrows inward. In the plant micrograph a partition is developing between the two dark chromosome groups.
  2. Name the mechanisms. A contractile ring produces the animal cleavage furrow. Vesicles join to establish the plant cell plate.
  3. Connect structure to mechanism. The animal's flexible membrane can be drawn inward. The plant's rigid outer wall stays in place while a new internal partition grows outward.
  4. State the shared result. Cytokinesis separates cytoplasm into daughter cells. Neither the furrow nor the plate is the event that copies DNA.

The animal illustration's “identical” label describes the normal nuclear genetic outcome of the model. It does not demonstrate exact equality of every organelle or prove that any resulting tissue is functional.
