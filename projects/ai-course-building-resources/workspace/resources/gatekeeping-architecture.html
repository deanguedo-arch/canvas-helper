The Human Layer

The Process Check-In

The Process Check-In is its own separate layer. It is how you conduct the readiness check in the Strict Funnel, or how you conduct the macro gates in the Multi-Macro Scaffold. It is a dedicated chance to talk to students, review their process portfolio, and provide high-leverage feedback, with unlocking the prompt as the end goal.

With the Strict Funnel

One comprehensive Process Check-In occurs at the very end of the unit to verify readiness before the final summative prompt unlocks.

With the Multi-Macro Scaffold

Multiple smaller Process Check-Ins occur throughout the unit, acting as the relational gates between each chunk of learning.

The Teacher's Decision Toolkit

Regardless of which pacing architecture is chosen, the teacher evaluates the process evidence during the check-in and makes one of four operational choices:

Release Prompt

Ready now. Student has proven foundational knowledge and provided sufficient process evidence.

Targeted Scaffold

Patch a specific gap. Student is close, but needs one specific intervention or resource before unlocking.

Teacher Conference

Resolve evidence mismatch. Quiz scores and process uploads do not align; human verification is required.

Delay Access

Not ready. Student lacks foundational knowledge; releasing the prompt would cause predictable failure.

What the human layer adds

Automation spends 100 points. The Process Check-In adds engagement capacity on top.

The architecture gives the structure. The teacher's toolkit adds the missing human work: targeted feedback, emotional safety, ownership, and judgment when the evidence is messy.
