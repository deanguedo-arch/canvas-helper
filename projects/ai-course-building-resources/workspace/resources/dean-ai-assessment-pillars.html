Start Here: The Assessment Framework

Use this short overview before moving into the shift from product-only assessment to product, process, and defence.

Why Assessment Has to Change

Traditional online assessment is vulnerable because final products alone no longer prove learning. AI makes polished work easy to produce, but polish is not proof.

To maintain assessment integrity in an online environment, we must shift our focus from merely evaluating a final submission to evaluating a complete body of evidence that proves independent understanding.

info Our Responsibility vs. Alberta Education

This framework does not replace Alberta Education diploma exams. Alberta Education owns provincial standardization. Our responsibility is to ensure that school-awarded course marks are valid, visible, and professionally defensible before students sit for their diploma exams.

Core Assessment Model

Every major summative assessment is evaluated across three distinct buckets. Teachers use professional judgment to shift these weights based on course demands. We do not change weights based on AI level, but rather on pedagogical intent.

100%
Product 50% / 50%
Process 25% / 25%
Defence 25% / 25%

Product

The student can meet the learning outcome.

The traditional output. This is the final written response, exam, presentation, project, source analysis, or issue response.

  • check_circle Evaluates final performance
  • check_circle Aligns with curriculum standards

Process

The student can show how the work was created.

Evaluates perseverance, planning, and proper completion. Includes draft history, outlines, source organizers, and AI-use logs.

  • check_circle Makes learning visible
  • check_circle Proves disclosure and self-advocacy

Defence

The student can explain, defend, and apply the learning.

Evaluates self-reflection, metacognition, and the ability to articulate why specific choices were made. Includes oral conferences or transfer tasks.

  • check_circle Validates ownership of knowledge
  • check_circle The ultimate proof of learning

Board-Ready Summary

"We are not trying to catch students using AI. We are building assessments where learning is visible even when AI exists. Every summative needs a product, a process trail, and a defence. Students can choose different AI-use routes, but more AI means more responsibility to prove ownership."

Simple.

Three buckets: Product, Process, Defence. Weights remain constant.

Defensible.

Rooted in teacher professional judgment and documented evidence.

Usable.

Levels, permits, and validity gates provide clear boundaries for staff and students.
