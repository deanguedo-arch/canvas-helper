AI Assessment Portion

Product. Process. Defence. Assessments become valid when final work, creation evidence, and human explanation all point to the same learning.

Why The Model Exists

Traditional online assessment is vulnerable because final products alone no longer prove learning. AI makes polished work easy to produce, but polish is not proof. The model shifts attention from a single submission to a defensible body of evidence.
