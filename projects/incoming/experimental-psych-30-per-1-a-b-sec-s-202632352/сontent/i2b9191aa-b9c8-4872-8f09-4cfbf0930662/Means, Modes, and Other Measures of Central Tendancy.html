Means, Modes, and Other Measures of Central Tendency
After collecting a set of numerical data (or data that can be converted to numerical values), researchers often calculate averages to help them interpret information and predict events.  Basic statistical concepts such as the mean, mode, and median are the most common measures of central tendency used by researchers.  Central tendency refers to how clustered a group of numbers is.  Mathematically, central tendency refers to the average in a set of data.  

In addition to researchers, many other individuals use measures of central tendency in various aspects of their jobs.  For example, teachers calculate averages (report card marks), meteorologists make weather comparisons (current temperature to average value), and coaches determine who to place on the opening roster (batting averages).
