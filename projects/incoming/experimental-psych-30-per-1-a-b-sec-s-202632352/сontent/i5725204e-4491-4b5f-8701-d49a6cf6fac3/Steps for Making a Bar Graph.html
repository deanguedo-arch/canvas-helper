Steps for Making a Bar Graph


  • On graph paper, draw a set of axes (x and y).  Be sure to leave room for titles and headings and use as much of the page as you can when you draw the axes. 

  • Give the graph a title. 

  • Label the horizontal axis (x-axis) with the manipulated variable.  Include each category of the manipulated variable under the appropriate bar.

  • Label the vertical axis (y-axis) with the responding variable.  Add a scale that marks the value of the responding variable. 

  • For each manipulated variable, draw a solid bar to the height of the corresponding value of the dependent variable.  In the example below, each activity (manipulated variable) requires a box be drawn to the level of blinks (the responding variable).
