Ethics Defined

Ethics is defined as a set of moral principles or principles of conduct governing an individual or a group of people.  People, even entire cultures, have disagreements over whose principles are best.  There is no simple answer to who is right and who is wrong.  The best anyone can do is to keep an open mind and be willing to adapt and change his or her thinking when presented with new or conflicting information.      

Summarized from the Science Teachers' Workshop organized by the Alberta Heritage Foundation for Medical Research (AHFMR - 2006), ethics is the attempt to

think carefully and systematically about our options, then choose the best possible course of action
consider everything that is at stake for everybody affected, not just the most obvious implications (and especially, not just our own interests)
balance differing values and perspectives, as there is rarely a perfect answer.
focus on profoundly important matters, such as respect, fairness, and compassion
ask better questions then have been asked before about options and values
 

Some examples of ethics questions (AHFMR - 2006) relating to health include the following:

Who should decide which treatment a patient gets ... doctors, insurance company, government regulators, patient, patient's family?  What if the treatment is dangerous, extremely expensive, or usually doesn't work?
Is it acceptable to conduct research on children who cannot consent to participate?
Should we force people to stop smoking or wear seatbelts, or should people be free to injure themselves?  Is it fair to make people who take avoidable risks pay more for health care?
It is a bad act, an acceptable option, or perhaps even a duty of kindness to hasten the death for a suffering person who wants to die?
What exactly do the words "health", "disease" and "disability" mean?  Who gets to define these terms, and for what purposes are they used?  How do we avoid stigma for people identified as disabled or mentally ill?
Is it acceptable to choose or engineer the genetic traits of ones' child?
When should other people be told that a patient has an infectious disease?
Would it be acceptable to transplant the heart of a pig into a human being with heart failure?
