Introduction

Money has come a long way over the years – first replacing barter to make it easy to exchange goods and services – all the way to the new crypto currencies that have entered the picture.

But what makes something able to serve as money? What makes people willing to accept something as money? This Topic takes a closer look at what we use as money and the characteristics that enables something to be used as money.

Additional Learning

Today, our money has little, if any, value in and of itself. Our bank notes really only have value in terms of what we can acquire with them. The metal in our coins isn't worth very much either. There isn't 10 cents' worth of silver in our 10-cent piece or 5 cents' worth of nickel in our 5-cent piece.
