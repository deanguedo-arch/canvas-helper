# Chapter 16: scientific and source reconciliation

These are developer records, not student-facing lesson commentary.

Chromosome count is separated from chromatid number and DNA amount. Anaphase counts specify whole undivided cell versus one pole. Meiosis I separates homologues; meiosis II separates sisters. Gametophytes produce gametes by mitosis. Conjugation changes genetic combinations rather than multiplying bacteria by itself. Replicated chromosomes are not called a homologous pair. Two inspected source figure candidates were excluded: the p. 552 packaging graphic uses ambiguous chromosome-pair language, and the p. 578 conifer figure labels its sporophyte region “n”. The authored chromosome and life-cycle figures carry the intended unambiguous identities. No family/class survey or new physical observations are required.
