Lesson 3: Refinement & Exhibition Planning

Finishing artwork is not the same as refining it. Professional artists revisit, revise, and prepare their work so that ideas are communicated clearly and intentionally. How work is presented shapes how it is understood.

You will focus on revision, decision-making, and presentation rather than creating entirely new work.

 

What You Are Learning

In this lesson, you will:

  • Learn how to revise and refine artwork based on critique
  • Make intentional decisions about presentation and display
  • Understand how exhibition choices affect meaning
  • Prepare work for portfolio submission or exhibition

This lesson moves you from making to polishing and presenting.

 

Technique Focus

1. Revision & Refinement

Refinement means:

  • Strengthening what works
  • Adjusting weak areas
  • Clarifying intent

Refinement is purposeful, not perfectionistic.

 

2. Presentation Techniques

Presentation includes:

  • Cropping and borders
  • Mounting or matting
  • Scale and spacing
  • Clean edges and surfaces

Good presentation supports meaning rather than distracting from it.

 

3. Exhibition Planning

Exhibition planning considers:

  • Sequence and grouping of work
  • Viewer movement and experience
  • Relationship between artworks

Even a small display tells a story.

 

How your work is shown is part of what it says.
