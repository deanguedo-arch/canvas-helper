Sketchbook Tasks

Page 1 — Material Testing

Begin by choosing at least three different materials that you can experiment with. These materials might include paper scraps, magazine images, fabric pieces, paint, markers, or printed text.

On one page of your sketchbook experiment with how these materials interact. Try gluing pieces of paper over each other, drawing or painting on top of collage elements, or layering materials in different ways.

As you experiment, write short notes describing how the materials feel visually. Some combinations may appear rough, heavy, calm, chaotic, or energetic.

The goal of this page is exploration rather than perfection.

Page 2 — Identity and Culture Planning

Choose one idea connected to identity or experience. This idea might relate to personal identity, cultural background, a meaningful place, an important memory, or an experience that shaped you.

Write this idea in the center of the page. Around it sketch images, materials, or textures that might represent that idea. For example, if your idea relates to place, you might include maps, photographs, or textures connected to the environment.

This page helps you begin thinking about how materials and imagery might communicate meaning.

Page 3 – Mixed Media Identity Collage

Use a page in your book to create a mixed media collage that communicates identity or meaning using at least three different materials.

Your collage should show clear layering and visible textures. The materials you choose should feel intentional and connected to the idea you are exploring.

Avoid creating a flat collage where everything is glued down evenly. Instead, allow some areas to build up with thicker layers while others remain more open.

Your final artwork should demonstrate experimentation, thoughtful material choices, and a clear connection to the idea you explored in your planning pages.

Page 4 — Reflection

After completing the planning and the finished artwork, write a short reflection describing your material choices. Explain why you selected the materials you used and how those materials contribute to the meaning of the artwork.

Consider how texture and layering influence how the artwork feels visually and emotionally. Reflect on what you found most challenging while working with mixed media.

Writing about your choices helps strengthen your understanding of how materials communicate meaning.
