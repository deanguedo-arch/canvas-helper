What Strong Sketchbook Work Looks Like

✔ Many lines, not one perfect outline
✔ Evidence of trial and error

✔ Notes beside drawings
✔ Willingness to abandon accuracy

✔ Clear experimentation

✘ One careful drawing only

✘ Erasing all marks

✘ Avoiding difficult tools

Common Mistakes (and How to Avoid Them)

Mistake: Trying to control every line
Fix: Let the tool lead the mark

Mistake: Judging drawings too quickly
Fix: Finish the page before deciding

Mistake: Avoiding expressive marks
Fix: Discomfort usually means growth
