Final Product for Historical Artwork

Using your plan, you will now create your final artwork.

While working on your piece, focus on the following:

Craftsmanship

  • Take care with your materials and techniques.
  • Work slowly and thoughtfully to produce a strong final result.

Revision

  • Be willing to adjust your work if something is not working.
  • Artists often change their ideas while working.

Experimentation

  • Try new approaches or techniques if they improve your artwork.
  • Artistic growth often comes from experimentation.

Problem Solving

  • If you encounter difficulties, think about possible solutions.
  • Consider composition changes, colour adjustments, or technique improvements.

 

Process Documentation

While creating your artwork, document your process by:

  • Taking progress photos, or
  • Recording sketchbook notes about your decisions and adjustments

This documentation helps show the development of your work from idea to completion.

 

By the end of this stage you should have:

  • A completed artwork
  • Evidence of your creative process
  • Demonstration of craftsmanship and thoughtful decision making

Your artwork should show a clear connection to the artist or movement you researched, while still expressing your own ideas and style.

Artist Statement

After completing your artwork, you will write an artist statement explaining your work and the influence behind it.

Your artist statement should answer the following questions:

  • Who is the artist or movement that influenced your work?
  • What did you learn from researching this artist?
  • What visual ideas, techniques, or themes influenced your artwork?
  • What choices did you make when creating your piece?
  • What message, idea, or meaning were you trying to communicate?

Your statement should clearly explain how your artwork connects to the researched artist while showing how you developed your own interpretation.

Your artist statement should demonstrate that you:

  • Understand the artist or movement you researched
  • Can explain how artistic influence works
  • Can describe your own artistic decisions and ideas

This reflection shows your critical understanding of artistic influence and creative development.
