How reproductive systems work

Reproductive organs produce gametes and provide pathways for fertilization. Hormones coordinate sperm production, egg development and changes in the uterus. Follow each structure and signal to explain how these processes work together.

By the end of this chapter, I can:

  • I can identify reproductive structures and connect each with its function.
  • I can distinguish gametes from the cells and tissues that support their development.
  • I can trace FSH, LH and gonadal hormones through male and female feedback pathways.
  • I can interpret coordinated ovarian, uterine and hormone changes.
  • I can explain how reproductive tissue damage, including STI-related damage, can affect function.
