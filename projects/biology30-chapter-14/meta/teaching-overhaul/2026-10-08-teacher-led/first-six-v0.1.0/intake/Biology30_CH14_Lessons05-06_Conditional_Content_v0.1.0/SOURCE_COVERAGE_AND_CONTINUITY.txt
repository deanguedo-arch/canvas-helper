Source reconciliation
The historical owner already distinguishes the released secondary oocyte from a completed ovum. Candidate preserves that correction and explains broad egg/ovum labels without changing source images. The text does not treat a single-cell zygote as the structure that implants. It does not require strict left/right alternation of ovulation, universal monthly timing, simultaneous visibility of every illustrated follicular stage or clockwise migration of a follicle around an ovary.
Authoritative checks include the original publisher's anatomy/oogenesis account at https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/27-2-anatomy-and-physiology-of-the-ovarian-reproductive-system and the original ultrasound study on ovulatory side at https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10739814/ . External sources were used to verify bounded corrections, not replace teacher progression or introduce clinical recommendations. Source-review report records additional independently checked evidence and any source limitations. No blanket endorsement of every sentence in a source is implied.
