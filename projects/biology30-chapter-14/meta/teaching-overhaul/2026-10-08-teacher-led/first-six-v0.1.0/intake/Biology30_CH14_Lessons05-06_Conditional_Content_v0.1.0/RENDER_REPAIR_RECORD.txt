Static learner packet: observed failure and repair

Initial automatic HTML-to-PDF conversion retained task text but clipped the right side of both large illustrations and joined option labels. Independent review of all22 initial pages confirmed the defect. Text presence alone was not accepted as rendered usability evidence.

Repair: create a static reading representation with unchanged source images fitted proportionally inside page bounds, distinct option bullets, exact prompts/givens and masked comparison answers. No course source/runtime or assessment meaning was modified. A first repaired version had14 pages. All14 were independently inspected; the full figures, numeric stimulus and17 tasks were present and readable.

Further repair: keep each guided/check/writing task together with its options or response instructions to remove split-choice and orphan-label friction. The final packets have8 pages for lesson05 and7 for lesson06. A final grouping adjustment keeps the complete original optional source exercise together on lesson05 page8 instead of leaving its save instruction alone; source-review recheck covers the changed pages. Candidate copy also explicitly defines diploid and changes the optional cell-size exercise's unseen-drawing framing into complete stated-model givens.

Final rendering evidence is limited to these static PDFs and their page images. It does not prove how the original course CSS, browser controls, enlargement, saved responses or mobile layout work.
