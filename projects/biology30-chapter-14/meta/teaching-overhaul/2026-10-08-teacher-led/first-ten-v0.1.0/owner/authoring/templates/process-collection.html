How to keep a copy of your work
  1. Review your completed checks and formative practice runs below.
  2. Open the submitted responses to see attempts, explanations and first-try results.
  3. Select Print or save as PDF.
  4. Choose a printer or your browser’s Save as PDF destination.
  5. Save or submit that file according to your teacher’s directions. Nothing is submitted automatically.

Unfinished work is stored separately from completed history. The print view includes completed activity details, your Frayer collection, and collected optional responses.

Writing and flash-card self-assessments are not automatically graded. Completion is not a mastery certificate.
