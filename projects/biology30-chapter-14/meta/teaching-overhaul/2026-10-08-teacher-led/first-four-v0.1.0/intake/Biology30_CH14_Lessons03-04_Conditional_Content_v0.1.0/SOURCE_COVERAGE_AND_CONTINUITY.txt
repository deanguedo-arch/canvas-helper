Scientific reconciliation and sources:
1. Textbook493 says FSH causes interstitial cells to produce sperm. The supplied p480 anatomy and teacher17 diagram separate Sertoli and Leydig cells. Endotext Fig12 independently confirms FSH receptors on Sertoli cells and LH receptors on Leydig cells, testosterone support and upstream feedback: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK279031/figure/endocrin-male-reprod.F12/ (checked8Oct2026).
2. Teacher13 says two cycles of meiosis. Candidate preserves the historical correction: one process with meiosisI andII, producing four spermatids from one primary spermatocyte, followed by differentiation. Official OpenStax27.1 spermatogenesis section supports this: https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/27-1-anatomy-and-physiology-of-the-testicular-reproductive-system .
3. Teacher19 groups hGH with steroids. Endotext identifies GH as a protein: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK279056/ . The candidate makes only the necessary classification correction; it does not generalize the androgen model to all substances.
