## Core scientific findings

### Final-review route

1. **Normal route and changed function.** Textbook 480–481, especially Table 14.2, and teacher slides 8/12/13 support testicular production, Sertoli support, epididymal maturation/storage, the vas-deferens/ejaculatory-duct/urethral route and accessory-fluid contribution. Fructose is an energy contribution; the prose does not claim it is the only fuel or that low fructose establishes failed sperm production. The original table compares functions row by row, and the visible instructions prevent reading its rows as one continuous pathway.
2. **Female route and event separation.** Textbook 482/484 and slides 22–24 establish anatomy; new copy correctly uses secondary oocyte at ovulation and blastocyst at implantation instead of copying older ovum/zygote shorthand. The implantation distinction is independently supported by [NICHD's account of the implanting blastocyst](https://www.nichd.nih.gov/news/releases/Pages/embryo.aspx). It does not imply implantation occurs immediately at fertilization.
3. **Infection versus endocrine function.** Textbook 488–489 supports asymptomatic infection and oviduct scarring. New prose says scarring can disrupt gamete encounter/transport while ovarian hormone production can continue. It does not infer universal infertility or a personal diagnosis. [CDC's PID explanation](https://www.cdc.gov/pid/about/index.html) independently supports possible absent symptoms and tubal scarring/blockage. No treatment instruction is imported.
4. **Sources, targets and feedback.** Textbook 493–494 and slides 17–18 support the hypothalamus → GnRH → anterior pituitary → FSH/LH sequence and separate target roles. Candidate correctly rejects textbook p493's incorrect FSH/interstitial-cell/sperm sentence. FSH acts on Sertoli cells; LH acts on Leydig/interstitial cells. Inhibin especially restrains FSH, and testosterone feedback can reduce upstream stimulation. The measured outside signal is explicitly distinguished from testicular production. The wording is conditional rather than an absolute clinical prediction.
5. **Ovarian sequence.** Textbook 496–498 and slides 24/27/29–32 support follicle development, sustained pre-ovulatory estrogen positive feedback, LH surge, ovulation, corpus-luteum output and endometrial support. Candidate does not copy rigid calendar rules, a zygote implantation claim or a claim that LH becomes progesterone. The early hCG sentence is consistent with the p497 support mechanism and explicitly connects back to lesson 09; it does not attempt to teach the complete later-pregnancy/postpartum process.
6. **Constructed GnRH worked case.** Matched brief pulses and specified receptor functionality identify the difference at pituitary reception. The stated reduced FSH/LH output is a given, not a quantitative prediction fabricated from an unspecified mutation. A hypothalamus is not measured. Downstream statements are conditional on otherwise responsive gonads and stop short of predicting every concentration. The primary [Tello et al. receptor study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3367945/) supports impaired receptor signalling and reduced gonadotropin response; indexed primary text was accessible, while direct PMC opening presented a verification interstitial. [The supplied de Roux record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17161329/) was also retrieved through indexed abstract text as contextual corroboration. Neither source makes this invented two-tissue comparison a real experiment or authorizes patient-level inference.
