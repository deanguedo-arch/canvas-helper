In the male model, LH stimulates interstitial cells; testosterone then participates in negative feedback. FSH acts on Sertoli cells that support sperm development.In the female model, follicle growth and rising estrogen are followed by a positive-feedback switch when estrogen stays sufficiently high. The LH surge helps trigger ovulation. The corpus luteum then supports a progesterone-rich luteal phase.
