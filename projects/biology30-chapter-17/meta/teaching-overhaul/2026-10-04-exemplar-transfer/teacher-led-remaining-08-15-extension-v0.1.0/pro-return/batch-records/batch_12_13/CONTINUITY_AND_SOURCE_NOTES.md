# Continuity and source decisions

## What is now genuinely taught

- Two-trait independent expectation is derived before departure is interpreted
- A genotype supplies alleles while phase supplies their arrangement on homologues
- Copying produces sisters; crossing over exchanges corresponding material between nonsister chromatids in prophase I; segregation and fertilisation keep separate jobs
- A recessive tester has a fixed contribution and, under the stipulated expression rules, allows all four contribution/genotype/phenotype mappings
- Counts need sampling, scoring, expression and survival assumptions; physical linkage does not mean permanent inseparability
- Recombination frequency counts both reciprocal recombinant classes among all scored offspring; it measures combinations, not every crossover event
- Genetic distance uses the short-interval approximation; multiple exchanges, expected50% versus observed samples, long map spans, and variable base-pair relation are fully explained
- Three consistent distances may settle an order; only two may leave alternatives
- Source602 notation, zero detection and human-evidence analysis have visible preparation, with the conditional limits recorded
