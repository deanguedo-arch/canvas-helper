A genotype can hide chromosome arrangement

AaBb tells you which alleles are present but not how they are arranged on the homologues. AB/ab places A with B on one chromosome and a with b on the other. Ab/aB places A with b and a with B. This arrangement is called .

The original combinations are parental. Crossing over between the loci can generate recombinant combinations. Which combinations count as parental therefore depends on the starting arrangement, not simply whether the letters are both uppercase.

A test cross can reveal gamete combinations

Cross a heterozygote with aabb. The tester contributes only ab, so the offspring's or can reveal the heterozygote's gamete contribution. Under , the four combinations are expected in equal proportions.

If the heterozygote has AB/ab and the genes are linked, AB and ab gametes often occur more frequently than Ab and aB. The corresponding parental offspring classes are larger, and the recombinant classes smaller.

Read the actual counts, not just the presence of four categories. can produce all four combinations if crossing over occurs, but the proportions can differ from 1:1:1:1.

Linkage is about probability, not an absolute lock

Closer genes are less likely to be separated by crossing over between them. Genes farther apart may show more recombination. Multiple crossovers can complicate the relationship between observed recombinant frequency and physical distance.

A small sample can depart from equal counts by chance, so a slight imbalance alone is not conclusive proof of linkage. Large, consistent differences provide stronger evidence under the stated model.

Keep the explanation at the correct level: crossing over changes combinations within chromosomes, and offspring counts can provide evidence about those gamete combinations.

Worked example

Identify parental and recombinant classes

An AB/ab heterozygote is crossed with ab/ab. Offspring reveal 42 AB, 40 ab, 9 Ab and 9 aB gamete contributions.

  1. The stated parental phase is AB/ab, so AB and ab are parental.
  2. Ab and aB are recombinant combinations.
  3. There are 82 parental and 18 recombinant observations among 100.
  4. The excess parental classes is consistent with linkage in this model.
  5. The counts do not mean only 18 cells underwent any crossing over anywhere; they concern the two loci being scored.
