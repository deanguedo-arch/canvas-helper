Sex-linked inheritance

Why does “an affected son” differ from “an affected child”?

Learning goal

Use a typical XX/XY model to solve X-linked crosses and distinguish conditional from joint probabilities.

Before you begin

The denominator must match the group asked about. Each gamete contributes one sex chromosome in the typical model.
