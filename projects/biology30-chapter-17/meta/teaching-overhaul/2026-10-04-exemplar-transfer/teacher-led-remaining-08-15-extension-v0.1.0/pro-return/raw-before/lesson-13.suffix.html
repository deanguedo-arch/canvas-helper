Read the explanation

Suppose A–B is 6 map units, B–C is 14 and A–C is 20 in a simple additive map. Since 6 + 14 = 20, B lies between A and C. Either A—6—B—14—C or its reversed orientation expresses the same relative order. Two distances alone may leave more than one order possible. Place the genes tentatively and ask what additional comparison would distinguish the alternatives. Do not invent a third distance merely to finish the drawing.

Maximum 3200 characters. Explain the biology in your own words.

Compare after saving your own response

Map units reflect recombination probability. Recombination varies across chromosome regions and organisms, so no fixed base-pair conversion applies universally. Multiple crossovers can be undetected, and two-locus recombinant frequency approaches the independent limit of 50%.
