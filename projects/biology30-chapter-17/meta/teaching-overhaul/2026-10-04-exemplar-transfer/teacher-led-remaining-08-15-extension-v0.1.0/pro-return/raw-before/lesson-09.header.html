Learn · Chapter 17

Polygenic traits and environmental influences

Why do many traits vary continuously instead of falling into two groups?

Learning goal

Explain polygenic and environmental contributions, and distinguish genotype, gene expression and measured phenotype.

Before you begin

Single-locus squares are simplified models. A phenotype need not be controlled by only one gene.
