## Lesson-02 — Mendel’s experiments and segregation

### Purpose and starting knowledge

Answer: how can a recessive phenotype return when the allele has neither blended away nor been recreated? Native lesson01 provides:

- `ch17-l01-teaching-01`, **Start with a defined genetic model**: allele, genotype and phenotype; two alleles at a diploid autosomal locus, one inherited from each parent.
- `ch17-l01-teaching-02`, **Dominance describes the heterozygote**: homozygous and heterozygous combinations; complete dominance; dominant does not mean common, strong or healthy.
- `ch17-l01-teaching-03`, **Notation keeps the reasoning visible**: one letter per gene and different letter forms for its alleles; phenotype alone can leave genotype unresolved.

These are actual native passages, not presumed acceptance of the earlier expanded manuscripts. A gamete, homologous pair, locus, copied sister chromatids, chromosome reduction and fertilisation will be defined in the current path before the student must use them.

## Lesson-05 — Two-trait crosses and independent assortment

### Purpose and actual earlier prerequisites

Answer: how do separate allele choices become a justified joint offspring prediction? Use these actual earlier passages:

- Native02 `ch17-l02-teaching-02`, **Segregation explains reappearance**, and `ch17-l02-teaching-03`, **Expected ratios are probabilities**; this calibration strengthens their causal path but does not assume the old returned02 manuscript was accepted.
- Native03 `ch17-l03-teaching-01`, **Write gametes, not whole parental genotypes, on the edges**: a square combines one gamete from each parent.
- Native03 `ch17-l03-teaching-02`, **Count the outcome that was actually asked for**: genotype versus phenotype grouping, denominator and expected number.
- Native03 `ch17-l03-teaching-03`, **Independent offspring do not compensate for earlier outcomes**: multiply specified independent events, not any two quantities.
- Native04 `ch17-l04-teaching-01`, **Choose a tester with a known contribution**, and `ch17-l04-teaching-03`, **Use family information before calculating**: use known contributions and preserve unknown genotypes. Its separate mixed-letter `aa`/`B_ × bb` sentence is a protected source conflict, not a clean example to reuse.

Native03 does not teach conditional subgroup probabilities. The old expanded manuscript cannot be substituted for that missing native prerequisite.

Repeating the same full qualification after every calculation adds no new inference, so one connected account plus local reminders will replace that repetition. The current path checks all mutually exclusive, exhaustive probabilities total one and explains expected counts versus guarantees.
