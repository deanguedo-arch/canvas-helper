## Words for this lesson

Use these definitions when you need help with a term.

**dihybrid cross**  
A cross between individuals heterozygous at two studied loci.

**independent assortment**  
Independent segregation of alleles at different loci when the chromosome arrangement permits it.

**product rule**  
Multiplication of probabilities for specified independent events.

**gamete combination**  
The set of one allele from each studied locus carried by a gamete.

#### Read the explanation

For AaBb × AaBb, the A locus gives a 3/4 dominant-phenotype probability and 1/4 recessive probability. The B locus gives the same. If the loci assort independently, the probability of both dominant phenotypes is 3/4 × 3/4 = 9/16. The other phenotype combinations are 3/16, 3/16 and 1/16, producing the familiar 9:3:3:1 expectation. This ratio is specific to the stated heterozygote cross and allele relationships. For a different cross, recompute. In AaBb × aaBb, probability of A\_ is 1/2 and probability of B\_ is 3/4, so both dominant phenotypes have probability 3/8. A memorized 9/16 would be wrong.
