<h3>Worked example: an impulse arrives but no message is released</h3><p>Suppose the action potential reaches a terminal normally, but its voltage-gated calcium channels cannot open. Predict what happens next.</p><ol class="pro-steps"><li><strong>Keep the successful event.</strong> Conduction along the axon occurred; there is no evidence of a failure there.</li><li><strong>Locate the blocked event.</strong> Without the normal Ca²⁺ entry, the usual trigger for vesicle fusion is missing.</li><li><strong>Follow the consequence across the gap.</strong> Much less, or no evoked, transmitter is released, so the postsynaptic receptors do not receive the usual chemical signal.</li><li><strong>Limit the conclusion.</strong> The receiving cell might still respond to a different input. This result concerns communication through the affected terminal.</li></ol>
