Learn · Chapter 17

Test crosses and unknown genotypes

What can offspring reveal about a parent with a dominant phenotype?

Learning goal

Use a homozygous recessive tester to distinguish possible parental genotypes and explain uncertainty from small samples.

Before you begin

A dominant phenotype can be AA or Aa. An aa offspring must receive a from both parents.
