The connected question is: **How do inherited allele contributions explain offspring characteristics, and what can those characteristics tell us about the contributions?** The path moves from distinguishing information from expression, through the retained Mendel experiment and segregation account, to predicting from known parents, investigating an unknown parent, and finally the retained investigation of two characteristics together.

The teacher-led sequence gives each idea a reason to appear. Concrete evidence or a representation is introduced, the learner is directed to notice a specific relationship, and an explanation makes that observation useful. An example earns space for the new inference it supplies. There is no example count, paragraph count, practice quota or target length. No new assessment or saved activity is added.
