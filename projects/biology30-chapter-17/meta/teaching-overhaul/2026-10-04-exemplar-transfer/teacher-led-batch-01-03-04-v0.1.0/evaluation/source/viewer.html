Traits are determined by specific genes, or segments of DNA. Different variations of genes are known as alleles. In most cases, one allele is dominant over the other, influencing the way such genes are expressed…

➢The dominant allele (represented by capital letter) is always expressed when present

➢A recessive allele (represented by lowercase letter) is only expressed in the absence of the dominant allele.

The allele that encodes brown eyes (B), for example, is dominant over the recessive allele that encodes blue eyes (b).

GENES, ALLELES AND TRAITS

 

 

 

 

➢This means that although we receive genes from both parents, our characteristics are NOT determined by the “blending” of our parents traits. It is determined by the dominant vs. recessive interaction of the alleles we receive.

➢Phenotype refers to the outward expression of traits. For example, having brown eyes or having blue eyes.

➢Genotype on the other hand refers to the combination of alleles an individual has for a given trait.

➢For example, your teacher’s genotype for eye color is ‘bb’. Since she only has recessive alleles, this means that her phenotype is ‘having blue eyes’

 

PHENOTYPE VS. GENOTYPE

 

Note: A phenotype isn’t always a visible trait like color. It could be a behaviour or a physiological trait, such as ‘able to digest lactose’ or ‘not able to digest lactose.

 

 

 

This child’s dad donated an allele encoding brown eyes (B). Her mom donated an allele encoding blue eyes (b). Because the “brown eye” allele or “B” is dominant to the “blue eye” allele or “b”, her eyes appear brown rather than blue.

ALLELE COMBINATIONS

➢Heterozygous - having two different alleles

oOne parent gives a dominant allele, the other parent gives a recessive allele

oResults in dominant phenotype

Because of the Law of Segregation, as long as the mode of inheritance of a particular trait and the genotypes of two parents are known, we can use a Punnett Square to determine the possible genotypes of offspring.

●Autosomal dominant mode of inheritance = trait is dominant & inherited on an autosome (as opposed to the sex chromosomes)

 

●Autosomal recessive mode of inheritance = trait is recessive & inherited on an autosome (as opposed to the sex chromosomes)

An individual with the dominant phenotype could be either homozygous (BB) or heterozygous (Bb). By performing a test cross, we can determine the true genotype of such individuals.
