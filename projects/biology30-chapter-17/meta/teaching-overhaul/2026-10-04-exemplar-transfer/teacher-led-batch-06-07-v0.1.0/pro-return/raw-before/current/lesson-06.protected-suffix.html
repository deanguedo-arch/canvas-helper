Written explanation 1

Maximum 3200 characters. Explain the biology in your own words.

Compare after saving your own response

Incomplete dominance produces an intermediate heterozygote phenotype, such as pink R1R2 flowers between red and white homozygotes. Codominance expresses both contributions, such as a roan coat with red and white hairs. In both models, the alleles remain distinct and segregate.
