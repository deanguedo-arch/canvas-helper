## Original progression and defensible transfer

Slide50 image42 has R₁R₁ above the left red flower, R₂R₂ above the right white flower, arrows from both parents, and a lower pink R₁R₂. This is a stated inheritance representation, not a photograph that independently establishes its own genotype. It is suitable at the moment students compare the heterozygote with the two homozygotes. The subscripts name versions of the same gene; they are not powers, copy counts or two separate genes.

Printed594 Figure17.11 supplies the essential next stage: a red/white parental cross, one gamete type per homozygous parent, pink F1, then R₁R₂ × R₁R₂ with a 1:2:1 genotype and phenotype interpretation. It supports teaching unchanged allele segregation, one allele contribution per gamete and random fertilisation before interpreting the grid. Its caption supports the stated pigment-dose account. The caption's phrase “When only one allele is present” must be explained as **one red-associated allele copy**, while the diploid locus still has two allele copies. Do not imply the heterozygote has only one allele total.

The source-backed causal distinction is: expression of the heterozygote has changed; the allele versions and their segregation have not. Explain enough of the normal chromosome/gamete account to connect to the retained Mendelian path without repeating a whole life-cycle lesson. Equal contributions and random fertilisation create two distinct routes to a heterozygote; add their mutually exclusive probabilities before grouping phenotypes. Three genotypes remain three phenotype groups under the stated incomplete-dominance rule, while complete dominance would combine two of those genotype groups. Expected counts are model predictions, not scheduled small-family outcomes.

## Labrador prerequisite and changed worked case

The saved optional `ch17-source-written-OBJ_2131251` asks BbEe × BbEe, using the exact provided rule. It is two-gene masking/interaction, not incomplete dominance or codominance at one locus. The task has no original hint/model to reproduce; do not invent a protected model field.

The parent's proposed explicit hypothetical pigment model is a sound preparatory transfer: P/p determines purple versus amber when pigment can be deposited; D/d controls deposition, with dd pale regardless of P/p. Cross **PpDd × ppDd**. Parent1 has PD, Pd, pD, pd at 1/4 each; parent2 has pD and pd at 1/2 each. All eight contribution pairs have probability 1/8. The colour marginal is 1/2 P_ and 1/2 pp; the deposition marginal is 3/4 D_ and 1/4 dd. Thus purple P_D_ is 3/8, amber ppD_ is 3/8 and pale __dd is 1/4, obtained by adding P_dd and ppdd. The sum is1. This is a changed parental structure and changed result, rather than a relabelled double-heterozygote9:3:4 answer.

At the Labrador image, explain what B_, E_, bb and ee mean; trace why __ee combines genotypes that differ at B; and establish that the stated model must be applied after gamete combination. Leave the exact BbEe × BbEe numerical answer for the optional task. State that independent assortment is an assumption for this classroom calculation, not a claim that phenotype effects operate independently: the deposition rule is precisely an interaction in expression.
