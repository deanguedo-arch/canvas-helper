Learn · Chapter 17

Multiple alleles and ABO blood groups

How can a population have three alleles when one person has two?

Learning goal

Use the ABO model to distinguish population allele diversity from individual genotype and solve blood-group crosses.

Before you begin

Codominance allows both allele contributions to be expressed. A diploid individual usually has two alleles at one autosomal locus.
