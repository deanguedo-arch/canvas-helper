Incomplete dominance and codominance

How can a heterozygote differ from either homozygote?

Learning goal

Compare incomplete dominance and codominance while preserving ordinary allele segregation.

Before you begin

An inheritance model determines the phenotype of a heterozygote; segregation still gives each gamete one allele.
