## Exact source cautions

1. **Slides35/40/41, independence wording:** the statement applies to the specified model and loci. It must not become a universal population claim that tall plants occur equally with each pod-colour allele, or that any pair of genes must assort independently. Separate evidence consistent with independence from proof that every gene pair follows it.
2. **Slide37, both original images:** repeated TG/tg labels and the16 identical cells are representational redundancy. Do not turn them into16 distinct possible outcomes or assume a four-by-four grid is required for any two traits.
3. **Slide39 image40:** ratio already visible. Honest guided reading can begin with phenotype differences and defer explaining the printed ratio, but cannot pretend its label is hidden.
4. **Slide41 OOXML text:** notes are called “meiosisII” while the sentence describes chromosome alignment at metaphaseI. Orientation of homologous pairs belongs to metaphaseI; meiosisII separates sisters. Original image44's middle MetaphaseII label is appropriate to its middle stage and must not be misapplied to the top orientation stage.
5. **Slide41 image47:** exchanged segments are visible. Universal identity claims for sister chromatids after crossing over would contradict the actual image. No linkage phase/map calculations are needed here.
6. **Textbook printed591/PDF8 bottom-right into printed593/PDF10 main prose:** independent assortment spans these pages. Investigation17.A occupies printed592/PDF9 and continues in the box at the **top** of593/PDF10. That physical activity is not a new assignment in this trial.
7. **Textbook Figure17.10, printed593/PDF10 caption:** “For any dihybrid cross” overstates the9:3:3:1 result. Specify the TtGg × TtGg cross, ordinary equal segregation, independent loci, random fertilisation, complete dominance/reliable phenotype expression and no selective loss in the scored sample. Its underscore statement is also overbroad: the blank is T or t at the height locus, G or g at the pod-colour locus, not any of four alleles.
8. **Printed593/PDF10 historical procedure:** textbook explicitly says F1 plants self-pollinated; slides38–39 refer to crossing F1 individuals. Both use TtGg × TtGg genetically.
