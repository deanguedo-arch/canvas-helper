# Chapter 17 connected teaching progression

Version 0.1.0, 6 October 2026. This record describes the remaining teaching proposal and its dependencies. It does not approve technical integration or release.

## Central question

How do inherited allele contributions produce observable differences, and how far can the available evidence justify a prediction?

The chapter first separates inherited information from appearance, then explains transmission, changes the expression and chromosome models, and finally examines the strength and use of the resulting evidence. A prediction requires an explicit model, justified parental contributions, an appropriate denominator and a conclusion limited to what the evidence supports.

## Preserved entry sequence

1. Genes, alleles, genotype and phenotype establishes the ordinary diploid locus, gene products, dominance as an expression relationship and the uncertainty of appearance alone
2. Mendel's experiments and segregation develops observations from chosen flower-colour parents through P/F1/F2 before interpreting them, then explicitly changes to seed shape for chromosome/gamete reasoning
3. Single-trait crosses and probability teaches gamete weights, contribution pairs, genotype/phenotype grouping, specified-event multiplication, exclusive addition and finite-sample expectations
4. Test crosses and unknown genotypes uses required offspring contributions to separate forced genotypes from compatible hypotheses and small-sample evidence from proof
5. Two-trait crosses and independent assortment derives complete gametes, separates assortment from random fertilisation, models two-locus probabilities and checks conditional evidence
6. Incomplete dominance and codominance changes heterozygote expression without changing segregation, contrasts intermediate and jointly detectable forms, and introduces a pigment/deposition bridge for an existing two-gene task
7. Multiple alleles and ABO distinguishes population diversity from an individual's allele pair, connects antigen expression to genotype, develops known-parent and evidence-constrained crosses, and introduces explicit joint/conditional denominators

## Teaching choices

- Original slide functions and concrete evidence guide the progression where scientifically sound. Their shorthand is developed into written teaching rather than copied as a lecture script
- Every important source image receives an introduction, a specific noticing task and an explanation of what its actual pixels support
- New practice requires only paper or the learner's own notes unless an existing saved native task supplies the response area. No new response field or saving system is introduced
- Existing guided questions, checks and optional saved tasks remain untouched. Some necessarily repeat foundational examples; the handoff calls these formative or review rather than falsely certifying fresh transfer
- The review retains the original14-topic table and adds connected explanations, not a replacement list with reduced coverage
- Source/key problems, misassigned optional textbook tasks and outdated clinical/policy wording remain explicit teacher-review issues outside learner copy
