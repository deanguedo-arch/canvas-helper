Read the explanation

Nutrition, temperature and other conditions can influence a phenotype. Plants with similar genotypes may reach different heights under different light or nutrient conditions. The measured difference does not automatically establish a difference in DNA sequence. Gene expression describes which genetic information is used and how much product is made. Cells with the same inherited genome can have different functions because they express different sets of genes. Environmental or developmental signals can change expression. Some regulatory changes involve chemical modifications associated with DNA or chromatin. These are often discussed as epigenetic changes. Do not treat every such change as a mutation or assume it is always inherited through many generations.

Multiple genes can contribute different effects, and environmental conditions can influence expression and development. Many combinations produce a range of measurements. A single complete-dominance Punnett square is therefore not an adequate model for every trait.
