# Biology 30 chapter pass process

This process uses the full Chapter 11 candidate coordinated by Scout as a teaching exemplar, then applies the approach to Chapters 12–20 without replacing their established course design or saved-work behaviour. Begin with [the teaching standard](CHAPTER11_TEACHING_STANDARD.md). The immediate handoff is [the Scout prompt](SCOUT_NEXT_PROMPT.md); it has been prepared, not sent.

## 1 Responsibilities

**Scout coordinates authoring.** Ask Scout to organize the current source files, use the agreed regular ChatGPT Pro route, manage full manuscripts and targeted revisions, and return a versioned handoff. Do not assume its own reasoning setting and a separate ChatGPT composer setting are the same. Verify the chosen model or preset visibly and report an unavailable setting instead of substituting silently.

**Codex owns preparation and integration.** Prepare student-data-free source packages, bind them to real files and hashes, identify owners and preservation contracts, review consequential claims, and integrate accepted copy without summarizing it. Keep saved-state compatibility and technical acceptance with the native course owner.

**Dean owns teaching acceptance.** Approve the approach, exact versioned lesson copy and any consequential assessment or media changes separately. AI reviewer agreement is evidence to inspect, not a substitute for this decision.

Use explicit attachments or a verified, already authorized task/computer connection. A cloud task does not automatically inherit the local repository or another chat's files. Do not grant extra access, create a new task, send a message or start a long-running authoring job merely because this process mentions it. Do not promise unmeasured usage savings.

## 2 Finish the Chapter 11 baseline first

1. Bind the full candidate, its repair notes and the native source to a version and hashes. The current binding is [BASELINE_RECORD.json](BASELINE_RECORD.json). Preserve the raw candidate, earlier three-lesson manuscripts and their separate review records.
2. Extract complete learner-facing copy for all 13 actual lesson routes. Include transitions, figure guidance, worked/guided/independent questions, hints, model responses, feedback, review and extension instructions. Keep developer notes and asset specifications separate. Do not substitute a summary or a headings-only export.
3. Produce a full chapter progression map: central question, prerequisites, each lesson's question, concepts introduced, evidence expected and forward connection. Check for unexplained terms or dependencies across lessons.
4. Reconcile every substantive claim and required task with the textbook, Alberta scope source and teacher materials. Retain exact locators. A missing source is an evidence gap, not permission to invent its contents. Address the targeted issues listed in the teaching standard before requesting exact-copy approval.
5. Request complete revised files only for lessons needing correction, with a precise change log. Avoid another generic "make it better" rewrite that loses the reason this candidate works.
6. Present a readable, versioned evaluation candidate to Dean. Distinguish the new full Scout candidate from the old `57000` three-lesson preview. Record approval by lesson ID and manuscript hash, or record the requested revision.
7. After the relevant approval and integration instruction, integrate those complete manuscripts into the native shell. Retain its fonts, spacing, vocabulary, reader and controls. Resolve new formative saving expectations before implementation; do not silently change their behaviour.

The selected teaching approach is already the direction. Steps 2–7 are the work needed to turn that direction into accepted exact copy and a native working course; they have not been completed by writing this process.

## 3 Calibrate before scaling

After Chapter 11's standard and sample copy are accepted, use Chapter 19 `lesson-04`, Calculating expected genotype frequencies. Supply prerequisite lessons 01–03 and the actual chapter's sources, questions, figures and keys.

Require a full lesson that explains symbols, allele versus genotype frequencies, the reason for the chosen method, the calculation, a plausibility check, interpretation and relevant model assumptions. Include a genuinely changed-condition guided and independent attempt. Source the actual tasks rather than inventing a new assessment under an existing ID.

Review this as a student who has only the provided prerequisite instruction. If the model teaches calculation or interpretation poorly, repair the standard's quantitative guidance before scaling. Dean's acceptance of this calibration permits using the demonstrated approach on later batches; it does not approve every other chapter's copy.

## 4 Prepare one chapter at a time

The frozen [chapter inventory](../2026-10-02-source-handoff/inventory/CHAPTER_INDEX.md) records actual routes and ownership cautions. It is a starting index from 2 October, not proof that every future source is current or complete. Revalidate the target chapter before its pass.

Each chapter source packet must contain:

- Current canonical entry and required native files, with a revision binding and SHA256 manifest; a clear list of teaching owners versus derived outputs.
- Complete existing lesson text and all instructions, questions, options, keys, feedback and required/optional status. Preserve stable IDs and expose assessment demands to the author without treating old keys as infallible science.
- The actual textbook chapter PDF or selected source pages, printed-page to PDF-page mapping and precise source locators. Include relevant diagrams and data, not a link that the author cannot read.
- Available teacher notes, decks, daily plans and verified curriculum scope. Name missing sources explicitly; never imply the original Chapter 11 source export fully prepares Chapters 12–20.
- Approved art and labeling answer maps, vocabulary entries/aliases, relevant reader targets and media inventory with verification status.
- This working teaching standard, the complete supplied standard and approved Chapter 11 exemplar manuscripts. Use the quantitative calibration when relevant.
- A preservation contract for routes, activity/question/option IDs, state owners and namespaces, old attempts, progress rules and safety restrictions. No student answers, credentials or private records belong in an authoring packet.

Give Scout the exact chapter, actual lesson IDs, source boundary and output destination. Verify attachments are available before requesting writing. If a packet is incomplete, continue source preparation rather than authoring unsupported material.

## 5 Map the whole chapter before writing

Ask Pro to establish the chapter's central explanatory question, starting knowledge, destination and conceptual progression. Map every existing lesson, including review and optional material. State what to keep, what does not yet teach enough and what the revision will add.

For each lesson, map substantial concepts to their source, explanation, meaningful visual, worked reasoning, guided/independent work and feedback. Identify dependencies and material that belongs elsewhere. Do not move routes, split or merge lessons, replace assessments or change the sidebar as an incidental content edit.

Agree on that map before writing the chapter. This prevents isolated polished pages that do not connect, repeated explanations and missing later material.

## 6 Author complete connected batches

Keep the whole chapter map visible throughout. Normally request two or three connected lessons per batch so each can remain complete and be revised without regenerating the entire chapter. Batch size can vary with lesson complexity; completeness matters more than the number.

Return each lesson as a complete manuscript, not a delta or partial notes. Include every explanation, example step, learner question, hint, model response, feedback item, diagram-reading sentence and transition. Include full captions and accessible descriptions for required visuals. Never shorten later lessons to finish within one response.

Maintain a chapter continuity ledger after each batch: terms taught, prerequisites satisfied, examples already used, misconceptions addressed, unanswered questions and planned next steps. Supply that ledger with the next batch so a new chat can continue without relying on hidden conversational memory.

Use meaningful independent transfer. Do not reveal the exact existing required check as the worked example, and do not turn model-supported practice into evidence of independent mastery. Keep substantive teaching visible; collapse optional help, not the missing essential explanation.

## 7 Review and revise against evidence

Read the actual manuscript alongside the sources and tasks. Check science, first-use definitions, causal steps, diagram guidance, fair practice demands, complete feedback and natural classroom voice. Distinguish an observation from an inference and a correct vocabulary distinction from an intentionally false misconception item.

Read the first and last lessons with equal care. Check the chapter as a connected path after the batches are joined. A complete chapter has no instruction, term, figure or prerequisite that exists only in an unavailable resource.

Return a targeted revision request listing the exact lesson, passage, problem, evidence and expected repair. Ask for the entire corrected lesson and a change log, preserving unaffected copy. Record reviewer identity, what was read, unresolved disagreements and whether the finding is verified, a design choice, or untested. Do not recycle self-review as independent acceptance.

## 8 Return an integration handoff

For each batch return one organized folder with:

- `CHAPTER_PROGRESSION.md` and `CONTINUITY_LEDGER.md`.
- `manuscripts/lesson-XX.md`: complete student copy, separately versioned. Never overwrite a previous accepted/raw return.
- `SOURCE_MAP.json`: lesson and concept IDs, source filenames, printed/PDF pages or slide numbers, and missing/conflicting evidence.
- `PRACTICE_MAP.json`: distinct new formative IDs, demand, taught prerequisites, hint, model/criteria, feedback and explicit saved/unsaved and required/optional proposal.
- `ASSET_PLAN.json`: existing asset paths or proposed IDs, placement, specification, labels/arrows, caption/description and approval status. A plan is not an approved image.
- `INTEGRATION_MAP.json`: target lesson IDs, precise replacement boundaries and untouched contracts. Keep learner copy out of developer instructions.
- `CHANGELOG.md`, `OPEN_ISSUES.md` and `MANIFEST.json` with input/output hashes and statuses.

These are requested handoff filenames, not claims that they already exist for Scout's Chapter 11 return. A useful evaluation HTML can accompany the manuscripts, but it is not the canonical source or a reason to replace native runtime code.

## 9 Accept and integrate without losing the teaching

Record separate statuses for source readiness, authorship, content review, teacher acceptance, integration, technical testing and release. For a teacher decision, use explicit wording such as:

> I approve Chapter [number] lessons [actual IDs] in manuscript version [version and hashes] for lesson-copy integration using the Biology Scout teaching standard. Keep the native design, assessments and saving. The listed unresolved items remain excluded. This does not authorize packaging, deployment or Brightspace certification.

Approval can cover a batch. It need not wait for the whole chapter, but chapter-continuity review is still required when assembling the final candidate. Do not reinterpret enthusiasm about the approach as approval of every sentence.

Codex integrates through the current owners. Chapter 11 uses native HTML plus `scripts/lib/biology30-pilot3/runtime.ts` when behaviour needs an approved change. Chapters 12/13 use their workspace HTML/runtime. Chapter 14's teaching owner was recorded as `meta/external-generation/scripts/content.py`; inspect it before changing assembled HTML. Chapters 15–20 retain current HTML teaching rather than resurrecting historical `lesson-content.json`. Revalidate ownership in every packet.

Preserve the native styling and course surfaces. Keep required activities and historical attempts immutable, bold clickable vocabulary connected to the correct entry, reader mappings, approved labeling answers, Process Collection and print/PDF behaviour. Chapter 13 lesson 07's worked/guided sequence stays before failure analysis. Do not replace a correct explanatory distinction with a false misconception statement.

Compare every rendered accepted teaching passage to its manuscript. Document unavoidable integration differences and obtain a decision for a substantive rewrite; do not quietly summarize. New essential artwork needs review before the affected lesson is release-ready.

## 10 Verify at the right boundary

During Build, inspect the changed preview and run only proportionate checks. Saved-answer, destructive, security or failed-preview changes require an immediate focused risk check. Ordinary manuscript work does not call for a repeated full-course test suite.

At an explicitly requested rollout checkpoint, validate the accumulated affected routes, assets, keyboard use, mobile/desktop presentation, exact copy, new and pre-existing saved work, progress, printing and appropriate Studio/SCORM behaviour. Test actual Brightspace separately before claiming LMS readiness. Deployment or a ZIP package is not implicit in an authoring approval.

The Chapter 11 repaired package's 56-check report is author-provided evidence, not an independently repeated test or a science approval. Its font fallbacks, image-based reader and session-memory fallback are evaluation-specific and must not become production defaults.
