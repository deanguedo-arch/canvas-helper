## New activity contract: substantive change from v0.1.0

The current instruction explicitly requires more than the earlier paper/thought-only unsaved pattern. Every newly introduced optional ungraded task must have:

- Its complete stimulus in view: the actual example, diagram, table or record needed for the response.
- A meaningful student action appropriate to the inference, with an in-place labelled response textarea.
- A native **Save response** control/specification using fresh `ch17-kinch-v020-…` identifiers and the native `S.notes` saving path, with save/reload/All My Work compatibility and existing conflict/failure behaviour preserved.
- An optional useful hint and intentional model/criteria/explanatory-feedback comparison, so the learner can attempt first and then diagnose their reasoning.
- Explicit optional/ungraded status outside required progress; no repurposed v010 IDs, silently added completion gate or claimed automatic grading.

This is an activity contract, not a quota. Worked teacher narration, an opening phenomenon and directions for reading a figure do not automatically become three separate saved assignments. Where the copy actually asks the learner to produce a practice response, however, “think about it” or an inert fragment label cannot substitute for the requested interface. The number and placement of new tasks should follow their instructional purpose.

“Inert” identifies the boundary of the returned authoring artifact; it does not remove the requirement to include the native-control markup and precise interaction specification. Conversely, the presence of a textarea and Save label is not proof of persistence. This instructional reviewer will inspect the actual task, response affordance, hint/model relationship and documented intended behaviour. The native reviewer must separately establish actual save, reload, All My Work, namespace, history/conflict/failure and progress behaviour on the authorized implementation. Until exercised, those functions remain untested. No runtime transplant or second state owner is authorized by the new saving requirement.

The CH11 exemplar's existing unsaved textareas remain observed historical behaviour. They do not override this user's explicit new save requirement. ## How the later findings will be stated

A necessary explanation can recur when the learner changes representation or inference: a gamete list, a fertilisation grid and phenotype grouping do different work. Several near-identical solved crosses do not become useful merely because one is labelled “transfer.” The review will describe the actual contribution of each source example and each new task, rather than demand or reject a number in advance.

Assumptions should appear where they justify a step. They should neither be hidden nor repeated as an identical catalogue after every calculation. Complete explanatory feedback is required; answer-only feedback and feedback that first teaches an essential operation are separate shortcomings. Genotype and phenotype ratios, denominators, totals, expected versus observed counts and the limits of an inference should be made clear at the point they are used, without introducing advanced calculations unrelated to this trial.

Protected assessment prompts/IDs/options/keys/feedback remain unchanged. General method overlap is unavoidable and will be identified explicitly. A copied protected cross/event with renamed symbols remains a direct structural rehearsal; the review will not label it safe merely because the spelling differs. The exact new tomato adaptation and squash case will be judged from their actual complete drafts and source/task records, not from an assurance in a plan.
