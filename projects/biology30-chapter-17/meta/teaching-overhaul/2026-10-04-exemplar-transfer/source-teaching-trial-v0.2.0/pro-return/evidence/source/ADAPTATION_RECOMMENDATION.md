## Scoped mathematical limitation

The following limitation applies only to the **ordinary diploid model with two biallelic loci, complete dominance at each locus, independent assortment, equal segregation, random fertilisation and full expression/scoring**. It is not a universal theorem about every biological inheritance system.

A parent with four distinct gametes must be heterozygous at both loci (`AaBb`). A parent with two distinct gametes must be heterozygous at exactly one locus and homozygous at the other. If that fixed homozygote is recessive (`aaBb` or `Aabb`), both phenotypes can occur at both loci; the resulting four nonzero phenotype classes necessarily have weights 3,3,1,1 out of 8. These are the source/protected 1/8 structure after exchanging locus names if needed.

If the fixed homozygote is dominant (`AABb` or `AaBB`), its dominant allele reaches every offspring at that locus. The result has only two nonzero phenotype categories, with probabilities 3/4 and1/4. Thus no alternative within this restricted ordinary 4×2 model preserves all four nonzero source groups while avoiding that mathematical structure. Switching dominance models, using unequal/linked gamete weights or adding selected denominators would introduce material beyond this bounded lesson's intended demand. Changing only the event while solving all four original categories would still display the protected 1/8 result.

The smallest defensible candidate change is therefore the proposed homozygous-dominant second parent, with a clearly recorded cost. The generic 4×2 template conflict remains unresolved; only Dean's later decision can settle the acceptable teaching/assessment balance. No assessment prompt, key or feedback should be edited to conceal it.

## Squash: preserve the distinct inverse-inference move

Actual slides 46–47 give yellow/disc-shaped × white/spherical fruit. Under the source's simple model, `W` gives white and `w` yellow; `D` gives disc-shaped and `d` spherical fruit. Parental phenotypes first permit `wwD_ × W_dd`. A yellow, spherical offspring must be `wwdd`.

That offspring requires `d` from the yellow/disc parent. Its disc phenotype requires `D`, so that parent is forced to `wwDd`. The offspring also requires `w` from the white/spherical parent. Its white phenotype requires `W`, so that parent is forced to `Wwdd`. These are forced within the stated parentage, ordinary-transmission and complete-dominance model; no frequency estimate is needed. This is the different reasoning purpose that justifies retaining squash after tomato, rather than adding another unexplained prediction exercise.

Gametes `wD/wd` and `Wd/wd` then give `WwDd`, `wwDd`, `Wwdd`, `wwdd`, each one quarter. White combines two categories, probability one half; yellow/spherical is one quarter. With 80 scored offspring plants, the source method gives expected 40 white-fruited plants and 20 yellow/spherical-fruited plants. Neither parent is a double heterozygote, so this cross does not by itself test independent assortment. Its role is inverse genotype inference followed by two-locus prediction and grouping.
