The B locus gives the same. If the loci assort independently, the probability of both dominant phenotypes is 3/4 × 3/4 = 9/16. The other phenotype combinations are 3/16, 3/16 and 1/16, producing the familiar 9:3:3:1 expectation. This ratio is specific to the stated heterozygote cross and allele relationships. For a different cross, recompute. In AaBb × aaBb, probability of A_ is 1/2 and probability of B_ is 3/4, so both dominant phenotypes have probability 3/8.
