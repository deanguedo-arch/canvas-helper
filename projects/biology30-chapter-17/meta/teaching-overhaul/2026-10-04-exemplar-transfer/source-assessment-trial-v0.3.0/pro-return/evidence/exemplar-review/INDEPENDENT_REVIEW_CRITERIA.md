## How final actual copy will be judged

The explanatory review will read the complete final teaching, optional models and new assessment prompts in learner order. It will trace first-use meanings, causal steps, visual symbols and figure controls, then attempt each new task using only what is actually taught. An assessment fails this scope if a consequential prerequisite exists only in a hidden model, unavailable source, later lesson, or assumed teacher explanation. A complete worked solution must state the given facts, justify the method, carry out every consequential operation, check a relevant probability total, and interpret the answer under its stated assumptions.

For the restored tomato and dependent task, the review will check all representations together: worded parents, genotype labels, phenotype labels, gametes and weights, grid dimensions/cells, genotype-class counts, phenotype grouping, total probability, marginal checks, expected-versus-guaranteed language, prompt, hint, model and corrective feedback. A surviving “all red/no yellow” sentence from v020 would be a substantive error in the restored context. The native A/B/C/D location-label explanation and exact figure control block must survive unchanged.

Each required writing model must answer every actual prompt demand, and criteria must specify the evidence needed for this particular response. Feedback must identify the causal or quantitative mistake and show how to recover. No automatic accuracy grading of writing is claimed. Teacher keys/models/criteria must be clearly separated from learner-facing teaching and from the initial prompt display in the inert teacher reading.

Repetition will be judged by instructional contribution rather than its word count: a repeated assumption may be necessary when notation/model changes, but repeated qualifications that do not affect a decision should not obscure the line of reasoning. No automatic example or practice quota will be applied. Native layout/function preservation and full text/math/feedback retention are separate from browser proof.

The assessment representation check will examine the actual HTML and matching course-data objects: exact retained IDs and native control attributes; identical prompt/options/key between owners; one option exactly equal to the MC answer string; full cue/explanation/difficulty; complete writing model/specific criteria/3200 limit.
