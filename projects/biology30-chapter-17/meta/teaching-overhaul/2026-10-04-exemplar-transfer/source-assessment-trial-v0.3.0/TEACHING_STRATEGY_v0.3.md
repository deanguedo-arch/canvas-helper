1. Motivate the concept with an answerable question. Chapter11 lesson01 “A cold hand gives us a starting point” follows detected cooling to processing and response, rather than decorating a definition list with an anecdote.
2. Supply the smallest sufficient prerequisite model. Lesson02 connects continuous axon, first-use action potential and terminal communication to structure/function. Detailed ions belong in04–05. Economy comes from dependencies, not word reduction.
3. Define terms at meaningful first use. Lesson02 explains action potential as a brief membrane electrical change regenerated along the axon before using that term in reasoning.
4. Teach connected causes. Lesson06 “Follow release, diffusion, binding, and clearance” explains why calcium entry permits fusion and how released transmitter reaches receptors. Labels alone do not perform these jobs.
5. Give a visual a viewing task. Lesson02 asks the learner to follow the continuous yellow axon beneath blue coverings; gaps are in insulation, not in the cell. Visual guidance prevents an actual mistaken inference.
6. Work reasoning completely. Lesson06 “Worked model: include inhibition in the calculation” states starting voltage, contributions, threshold and simplified model before adding, checking and interpreting. It also has a release-failure example with a different reasoning purpose; extra examples are justified by contribution.
7. Guide a real decision. Lesson02 “Explain a diagram error” supplies a hint distinguishing the axon from its covering. Necessary explanation precedes the attempt; help does not constitute independent evidence.
8. Require fresh transfer. Lesson02 “Compare two different failures” asks the learner to locate terminal release failure versus insulation loss and assess an input-branch repair. This is different inference from correcting a drawing.
9. Explain feedback. Its “Model and self-check” connects each predicted failure with location and missing function, and explains why extra dendrites do not repair it.
10. Defer detail explicitly. Lesson02 points to04–05 for ion mechanisms and to later grey/white matter teaching. A deferral needs an identified later home and enough present explanation for current demands.
