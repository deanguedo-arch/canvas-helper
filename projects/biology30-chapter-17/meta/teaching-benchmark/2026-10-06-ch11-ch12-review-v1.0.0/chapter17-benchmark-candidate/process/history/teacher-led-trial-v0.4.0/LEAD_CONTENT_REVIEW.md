## Science qualifications

Controlled crosses are not all technical testcrosses. True breeding is tied to breeding history and the stated one-gene model, not one plant's appearance. Dominance describes heterozygote expression, not gamete probability. Normal DNA copying increases physical copies without creating additional allele versions. Fertilisation and segregation do different jobs.

The added flowering-plant qualification is accurate: meiosis yields haploid spores, followed by haploid mitotic reproductive development.
