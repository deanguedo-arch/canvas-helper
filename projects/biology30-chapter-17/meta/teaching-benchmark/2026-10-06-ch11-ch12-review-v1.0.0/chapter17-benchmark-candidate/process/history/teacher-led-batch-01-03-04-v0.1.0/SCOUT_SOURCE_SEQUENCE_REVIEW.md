## Proposed connected sequence, by instructional job

Chapter thread: identify what is inherited and how appearance relates to it → retained Mendel investigation explains segregation → predict from known contributions → infer unknown contributions from evidence → retained two-locus lesson extends the method. The reference transfer is orient, observe, explain, apply; it is not copied hooks/headings or internal lesson-number narration.

### Lesson01

- **Question and concrete evidence:** start with why selecting parents can change cultivated/bred populations. Needed starting knowledge is resemblance and visible variation, not a glossary. Use wheat retention or a single dog contrast; direct attention to what is easier to harvest or visibly differs. Explain that selection chooses who reproduces, rather than changing an allele because an organism needs a trait. Readiness: distinguish a visible observation from an inferred inheritance mechanism.
- **Track the inherited information:** introduce DNA-bearing chromosomes, corresponding loci on homologues, ordinary diploid autosomal copies, and one allele contribution from each parent's gamete. Define gene identity, version and copy with one accurately stated example. No meiosis-stage retelling, DNA replication chemistry or gene-expression machinery is needed here. Readiness: explain why an individual has a pair at a locus and why two copies need not be two different versions.
- **Connect genotype and phenotype:** define a pea complete-dominance model before reading slide12. Compare BB/Bb/bb rows; the two purple rows establish a many-to-one mapping, while bb gives the recessive form. Explain homozygous/heterozygous, case notation, and dominance as the heterozygote's expression relationship. Extend phenotype beyond visible colour and explicitly separate dominance from frequency, advantage and gamete preference. Readiness: categorize pairs and state what each phenotype allows without guessing an unknown second allele.
- **Worked reasoning and closure:** provide givens, notation/model, a reasoned genotype-versus-phenotype comparison, uncertainty and a conclusion. Avoid copying the required answer verbatim. Explain why notation/model is needed before a cross without prematurely doing the retained Mendel experiment. Close with the experimental question of following contrasting plants through generations. Note that chromosome-map optional application belongs after linkage/mapping instruction.

### Lesson03

- **Predict from known parents:** activate one allele per gamete, not the full Mendel narrative. Define mouse B/b, expression and blackBB/Bb versus brownbb before specifying heterozygous black×brown. Introduce Punnett purpose and parent roles. Derive B/b half-weights and testerb weight1 before reading the right panel of image22 or a readable2×1 table. Readiness: identify valid edges, combine contributions, interpret1:1 genotype and phenotype outcomes, and check total1.
- **Change the second parent:** Bb×Bb has four quarter-weighted contribution pairs. Work cells locus-by-locus, regroup the two heterozygous routes, then interpret phenotype3:1. Place the existing Aa×Aa native figure here only after defining its new letters and location markers. Readiness: choose a numerator for a requested genotype or phenotype and state the all-offspring denominator.
- **Change what a generation supplies:** BB×Bb explains allblack offspring despite genotype variation; selected Bb×Bb descendants can have brownoffspring. Read image33 as a question about a later mating, not a fully specified pedigree. Readiness: distinguish appearance from contribution and explain persistence of a hidden recessive allele.
- **Probability and expected counts:** define independent births and products for specified outcomes; separate mutually exclusive alternatives and overlapping descriptions before adding. Explain probability×sample size as an expected count and finite sampling variation. Previous births do not change the next chance. Use a changed sample size/outcome for modelling rather than reproduce protected25among100 or an exact required question. Readiness: justify the arithmetic and its biological interpretation. Close with the limitation: known-parent prediction fails if one allele pair is unknown. Defer lethal/multiple-allele optional extensions until their models are taught.

### Lesson04

- **Establish the unresolved pair first:** use the dominant mouse appearance to state BB orBb; B_ records uncertainty. Show both hypotheses before naming testcross. Explain why the recessive tester's bb phenotype identifies a known pair under the supplied model and supplies onlyb. Readiness: select an informative tester rather than an unresolved dominant partner.
- **Compare predictions and then infer:** image22's leftBB×bb gives allBb; rightBb×bb gives halfBb/halfbb. Derive these contributions and weights in readable tables. An observedbb offspring requiresb from each parent; combine the unknown parent's observed black appearance with its suppliedb to forceBb. Repair only authorized teaching's notation typo. Readiness: trace evidence backwards before constructing another forward cross.
- **Interpret an all-dominant sample:** each independent offspring from the heterozygote hypothesis has dominant probability1/2; a finite run retains nonzero probability. Work a different run length/mixed specified outcome and explain conditional “ifBb” evidence, not posterior certainty without prior information. Increasing an all-dominant sample strengthens comparative evidence but never makes the finite probability zero. Readiness: calculate the retained four-outcome demand and distinguish support from logical exclusion.
- **Use additional family information and close:** a dominant individual with a known recessive parent must retain the recessive contribution and therefore be heterozygous. Present a genuinely different inference representation or constraint rather than just rename the protected MC2. Explain the method: force known alleles, retain unresolved possibilities, then predict. Close by asking whether following a second characteristic changes gamete combinations, connecting naturally to retained lesson05. Defer the multi-allele rabbit optional application; preserve the suffix's historical conflict in records.
