## Deliberate transitions and notation

New01 does not report Mendel’s experimental result before the retained02 staging. It closes by asking what controlled crossing could reveal about versions concealed by appearance. New03 explicitly switches from plant flower colour to the mouse fur B/b model, then separately explains the native A/a representation. New04 retains the mouse model while changing the kind of evidence. Its closure motivates tracking plant height and pod colour together in the unchanged05 reference.

Source letter changes are never treated as proof of assessment freshness. The03 Bb×bb and BB×Bb conflicts remain open. The contribution, probability and observation distinctions persist across the batch: an allele version is not a physical copy count; a grid cell is not a scheduled child; an expected count is not observed data; a probability under a parent hypothesis is not the posterior probability of that parent.
