Alleles pass from parents to offspring through gametes. Use inheritance patterns, probability and pedigrees to explain how different allele combinations can produce different traits.

By the end of this chapter, I can:

  • I can translate genes and alleles into genotype and phenotype predictions.
  • I can solve single-trait, two-trait, ABO and sex-linked probability problems.
  • I can use pedigrees as evidence while preserving uncertainty.
  • I can interpret linkage data and genetic testing claims with appropriate limits.
