How to use vocabulary and Frayer work
  1. Search the chapter terms or select a topic.
  2. Open a definition for its meaning, example and common confusion.
  3. Choose up to eight words for your Frayer work. Each chapter keeps a separate selection.
  4. Complete four responses per word, with 240 characters maximum per response.
  5. Select Collect this word. Editing it later returns it to draft status.
  6. After completing your own four valid responses, open the course model to compare. Print your work from All My Work.

Overlong Frayer text is not saved until shortened. No student answer is prefilled. Scientific accuracy is not automatically graded.
