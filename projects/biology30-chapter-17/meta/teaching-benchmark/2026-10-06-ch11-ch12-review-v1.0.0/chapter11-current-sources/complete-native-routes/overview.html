How your nervous system works

Receptors detect changes inside and outside the body. Neurons carry nerve impulses, and neurotransmitters carry chemical messages across synapses. The brain and spinal cord process this information and help direct a response.

By the end of this chapter, I can:

  • I can explain how neurons receive, carry and pass along information.
  • I can trace a signal from a sensory receptor through the central nervous system to an effector.
  • I can explain resting potential, action potentials and communication across a synapse.
  • I can compare somatic and autonomic control, including sympathetic and parasympathetic responses.
  • I can identify major brain regions and connect their structures with their functions.
