# Biology teaching-standard proposal — v1.0.0

**Teacher-only proposal.** Derived from the supplied teaching standard and the complete Chapter11 exemplar/reconciliation. Not promoted as a general standard and not Dean-approved. Chapter19 lesson04 calibration remains a later, separately authorized step after approval of the exact Chapter11 copy.

## 1. The unit of quality is an explained biological relationship

A learner should be able to explain how a structure, event or condition makes the next event possible. A list of correct terms can be useful reference material, but it is not sufficient teaching. The lesson’s essential explanation must remain understandable without opening a textbook, word popup or video.

Chapter11 gives a useful test of this distinction in lesson02, **Use structure to explain function**: “A useful answer must explain what the shape makes possible.” The ensuing reasoning connects dendritic branching to receiving contacts, the cell body/axon origin to integration, axon length to conduction distance, and terminal branches to release sites. Those connections—not the number of headings or length of the paragraph—are the feature to preserve.

A biological explanation can concern structure and function, a mechanism through time, a comparison, a quantitative relationship, a model’s limits, or evidence from an investigation. It does not need to imitate the nervous-system story when another organization would teach its own subject better.

## 2. Start with an answerable question and a deliberately small model

The opening establishes what the student is trying to explain, what they already need to know, and what the initial model omits. Lesson01 starts with a cold hand and separates a stimulus, receptor, processing and effector response. It does not begin by requiring the student to know membrane channels. Lessons02–06 then supply the cell structure, pathway and mechanisms that the initial model left unexplained.

Before approving a lesson, identify its starting knowledge in actual preceding text or an explicit prerequisite reminder. A phrase such as “as you know” is not evidence that the learner has been taught the concept. The first use of action potential in02 now gives a compact meaning—a brief electrical change regenerated along the axon—while the detailed ion mechanism stays in04–05. This is enough to orient the learner without turning the structure lesson into a duplicate ionic lesson.

The same decision applies prospectively to other Biology domains: define enough to understand the current task, and defer detail only when the later connection is made explicit. This proposal supplies an authoring decision rule, not new content for another chapter.

## 3. Develop concept-sized chunks through purposeful transitions

A chunk should resolve a question or explain a relationship that is used next. Its size is determined by the reasoning, not a fixed word limit or identical card template. Transitions should state what the learner can now explain and what remains to be explained.

The Chapter11 progression provides a concrete sequence: information jobs → cell structure → connected pathways → resting readiness → regenerated voltage change → chemical communication → a disrupted communication step → target tissues → central pathways/protection → cooperating brain regions → investigation of claims → integrated explanation → optional broader interpretation. CHAPTER_PROGRESSION records the complete questions, prerequisites and exact supporting passages for all 13 routes.

The later lessons must meet the same explanatory threshold as the early ones. A chapter review can synthesize and vary earlier reasoning, but cannot be used to replace unprovided teaching. The complete review12 therefore works through receptor-to-effector reasoning at multiple scales before its new independent task. Optional13 retains complete explanations and feedback rather than becoming an unframed list of videos.

## 4. Make mechanisms explicit at the appropriate scale

For a physiological process, state the starting condition, what changes, where it changes, why the change matters and what follows. Do not substitute a label such as “depolarization,” “inhibition” or “integration” for its explanation.

Lesson04 distinguishes concentration, charge and permeability. Lesson05 reads a voltage-versus-time graph at one membrane region before moving to propagation along an axon. Lesson06 follows AP arrival, Ca2+ entry, vesicle fusion, transmitter diffusion, receptor effects and clearance; it then makes the next action potential conditional on threshold. Lesson07 changes one named step in that established normal sequence and follows the consequences.

Keep scope and uncertainty explicit. A useful school-level model is not a license to make a universal claim. A single-neuron/fibre model does not by itself establish whole-muscle recruitment, and a source’s abbreviated account of a refractory interval does not automatically settle a more precise native explanation. The open source conflicts in this handoff demonstrate why preserving assessment identity and approving scientific explanation are separate decisions.

## 5. Teach the student how to read each important visual

An introduced visual needs a specific viewing task. Locate the structures, state the meaning of the boundary, explain the arrow direction or graph axes, and connect what is visible to the explanation. Mention scale, omitted features or other limitations when they could create a misconception.

In02, the learner is asked to follow the continuous axon beneath the blue coverings rather than treat the coverings as separate neurons. In03, the spinal withdrawal path and the separate route carrying information toward the brain have different jobs. In05, the horizontal axis is time, not distance down the axon. In08, hierarchy connectors mean “part of,” whereas arrows on a pathway can mean direction of information. In11, the methods diagram is a conceptual comparison, not an actual patient scan.

A diagram’s existence or attractive appearance does not establish accuracy. Keep original assessment letter mappings distinct from explanatory artwork. Figure10’s local synapse curves, Figure17’s regional brackets and Figure20’s overlapping text remain explicit review issues; this proposal does not excuse them because adjacent prose is stronger. Asset specifications must identify structures, exact labels, arrows, legend, source, caption, accessibility description and acceptance status. A specification is not finished art.

## 6. Show complete worked reasoning, including why each step follows

A worked example should expose the biological decisions, not merely reveal an answer. Explain why a structure belongs in the path, why a channel movement changes voltage, why a method answers a question, or why a numerical operation is justified. Use additional examples when a meaningful variation would reveal a misconception or test transfer; do not require a fixed number in every lesson.

The revised03 example uses the textbook’s driver/cat/brake situation. It identifies the visual information, explains why brain processing is needed for the selected response, follows output to the foot muscles and separates the effector from the action of pressing the brake. This teaches a method without supplying a near-verbatim answer to the unchanged required tennis-ball prompt.

Lesson06’s additive-voltage example explicitly supplies its starting value and model contributions, performs the calculation and compares the result with the stated threshold. It does not represent those illustrative values as measured data or a universal equation for every neuron. Lesson11’s worked PET model similarly separates a difference in a supplied measure from an unsupported inference about intelligence.

## 7. Move from supported attempt to fresh application

Guided work should require a real decision while offering a purposeful hint, partial structure or reminder. An independent application should change the situation enough that the learner has to select and explain the method rather than copy the preceding answer. All demanded reasoning must be available before the attempt, or supplied explicitly as a condition in the prompt.

The reconciliation makes the cooling mechanism available before the 01 guided task and provides the target-tissue distinction before the 08 knee/pupil comparison. In the practice map, sections listed under taught_before_attempt actually precede the prompt; later reinforcement is labelled separately. This check is more informative than saying a concept occurs somewhere in the chapter.

The27formative fields remain optional and ungraded. Each has a specific prompt, hint, retained complete model, teacher-side criteria and explanatory feedback. A hint may be used after a first independent attempt, but an assisted answer must not be described as independent evidence. The current handoff does not introduce tracking or scoring of that distinction.

New formative fields must not reuse a historical assessment identity. Existing native activities keep their question, option, writing, labeling and feedback IDs. Adding a clearer explanation does not authorize changing an old question’s key or marking its saved attempts differently.

## 8. Feedback should explain the result and its limits

Feedback should connect the learner’s decision to the mechanism or evidence and name the consequential misconception. A model answer can show a suitable chain of reasoning without implying that only its exact wording is acceptable. Criteria for an open response should describe the necessary ideas, not claim a reliable automatic mark.

For example, the 06 receiving-cell failure task distinguishes the presence of transmitter in the cleft from functioning receptors. The07 independent case uses a normal terminal AP and a response to directly supplied transmitter to narrow competing explanations, while preserving the need for another measurement. The11 independent PET task asks for limitations and a better comparison rather than rewarding an overclaim about one active region.

Native feedback behaviour remains with the native owner. The27new models are self-comparison support, not a new grade/completion system. A future saving feature is separately scoped in SAVING_PROPOSAL; saving a response must not quietly create a mastery claim.

## 9. Match the writing to the actual instructional demand

For anatomical or structure–function teaching, orient the view and connect a visible feature to what it permits; then vary the function or damage location. For a physiological or molecular mechanism, establish compartments, entities and sequence, and explain each transfer or change. For a comparison, select a common dimension and make the distinction causally useful rather than provide unrelated fact lists.

For quantitative teaching, name the variables, units, initial conditions and model assumptions before calculating. Show why an operation is valid and interpret the result biologically. A later genetics or population-calculation calibration would need those same checks against its own actual source conventions; this handoff does not author or certify such a lesson.

For experimental evidence, distinguish the research question, manipulated/measured variables, controls or comparison, observations, interpretation and limits. A diagram, simulated example or supplied table is not evidence that a learner performed a physical investigation. For a review, retrieve and combine earlier mechanisms with fresh applications. For an optional extension, provide sufficient teaching and boundaries without inventing a required outcome or requesting personal disclosure.

These are different explanatory patterns, not one repeated “concept/image/video/example/practice” sequence. Choose the representation because it advances this lesson’s reasoning.

## 10. Use media as a purposeful supplement

A video should have a verified identity, a reason for placement, a watch-for task and a follow-through connection. A video is not mandatory in each lesson. Essential science must already be available in the lesson, including when the player or network is unavailable.

The handoff retains22existing video identities and their prompts; the actual deck relationship targets confirm identity, not playback or captions. No timestamp, transcript, caption availability or new URL is invented. Media status must remain separate from the source and manuscript statuses until it is actually reviewed.

## 11. Preserve evidence and classify source conflicts openly

A source map must distinguish a curriculum locator, historical statement, empirical observation, illustrative assumption, native assessment identity and independent scientific support. Those are different kinds of evidence. A historical key may conflict with a more careful explanation; an AI-authored exemplar may include reasonable precision that the attached textbook does not establish. Neither problem is solved by silently changing the question or adding a citation to an unrelated paragraph.

Keep raw and revised copy, exact changed passages, source locators and uncertainty. Use teacher-only records for developer/source notes. Do not inject audit commentary into learner explanations. The Chapter11 source conflicts remain visible for Dean, including the tennis-ball key, membrane descriptions, bladder wording, blood–brain barrier distinction and the X-ray/CT item.

## 12. Keep content approval separate from implementation and release

A manuscript can be complete yet require a teacher decision. A statically rendered document can be readable while native interaction, storage and LMS behaviour remain untested. A passing hash or field-count check establishes identity, not good instruction. Author self-review is not an independent review and not teacher acceptance.

The adoption sequence proposed here is: Dean reviews the exact complete Chapter11 copy and resolves or explicitly disposes of its open issues; the approved set is identified by hashes; Codex integrates only the approved teaching boundaries while preserving native owners; technical checks are then performed on that actual integration. Later calibration uses the specifically identified Chapter19 lesson04 with01–03 supplied as prerequisite context, under a new authorization. Neither that calibration nor wider standard promotion has occurred here.

The acceptance question is practical: can a first-time Alberta Biology30 learner follow the explanation, read the representation, attempt the taught reasoning and learn from the feedback without having to invent the missing connections? Approval should point to the actual passages and tasks that support that answer.
