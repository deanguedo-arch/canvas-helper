Purpose and status
Use this record to produce source-grounded lessons that a first-time learner can understand and attempt, then repair ordinary defects before delivery. It consolidates inspected sources and current direction; it is not a claim that all historical feedback has been reviewed, every course follows it, or the method has demonstrated student learning gains. Stable rule IDs below allow each batch to show exactly which rules it used.

Cross-project working teaching and review rules
These rules combine current direction with safeguards derived from historical cases. Historical instructions retain their original scope; this record does not itself authorize course changes or deployment. The broad applicability of a rule is a production-design judgment, not proof of universal pedagogical effectiveness.
U01 Start from the actual learner task. Freeze the version, outcomes, complete prompts, materials and allowed prerequisites. For each required response, identify the teaching that makes every consequential step possible. A missing resource is a stated gap, not permission to invent it. [S2 sections 2/10; S5; S7 pp2–4]
U02 Teach before demanding. Explain necessary terms at first meaningful use, causal links and consequential choices. Optional reading, popups and video support the lesson; they cannot silently hold its only essential explanation. Keep required explanation visible. [S2 sections 1/4/5/8; S1 README]
U03 Use a connected sequence. Give the lesson a real question, relevant starting knowledge, developed explanation and earned conclusion. Chunk by the reasoning, not a word quota. Connect forward using topics. Complete later lessons as fully as earlier ones. [S2 sections 3/4; S1 COPY_CHANGES]
U04 Model the work. A worked example includes its question, evidence/givens, justified steps, checks and interpreted conclusion. Follow with suitably supported practice and meaningful independent application. Change a consequential condition, representation or inference; cosmetic substitutions do not establish transfer. Adapt these functions to the subject rather than imposing identical headings. [S2 sections 4/7; S4 p4; S7 p3]
U05 Make feedback actionable. Give an explained solution or criteria, identify a likely mistake and support another attempt. Keep the learner's attempt distinct from a revealed model. Completion, saved writing and correct recognition alone are not proof of mastery. [S2 section7; S5; S7 pp3–4]
U06 Teach through the actual visual. State where to look, what arrows/boundaries/symbols mean, what changes and why it matters. Inspect pixels and accessible alternatives. A prompt, storyboard or caption plan is not a finished usable asset. [S2 section6; S1 contract]
U07 Apply the learner lens during generation, then require a fresh-context review. Give the reviewer only learner-visible content, permitted prerequisites and source boundaries before its first report, withholding author rationale and expected findings. Mask comparison answers until the first attempt. Require attempted answers and a step-to-visible-support map; no gap-filling with expert knowledge. [S6; S5]
U08 Repair and recheck within authorized scope. Correct ordinary clarity, missing-step, example, feedback and citation defects before delivery; repeat the affected attempt and inspect adjacent material. Escalate consequential changes to objectives, assessments, accepted copy, protected state or scope. A calibration-only task produces candidate repair text rather than changing the course. [S6; S1 contract; S8]
U09 Record observable evidence. Use Pass, Needs repair or Not verified per claim, with exact location, attempt/action, result, repair and recheck. Keep scientific accuracy, learner sufficiency, teacher acceptance, implementation and LMS operation separate. Do not average away a blocking defect or turn reviewer confidence into a percentage. [S5; S7 p4; S1 status]
U10 Preserve provenance and learner work. Bind sources and outputs to versions. Record kept/adapted/lost/uncertain features. Never silently overwrite newer owner content, reuse an old question ID for changed meaning, or confuse authoring permission with deployment. Test actual behavior before asserting save/recovery or LMS reliability. [S1/S3; S4; S8]
U11 Verify the delivered thing. Review screenshots/renderings and behavior from the exact artifact that will be delivered, not a concept image, previous preview or design promise. Prove a representative complete path before propagating it. This safeguard is supported by the Unicorn handoff's documented visual-integration failure and ELA's missing plan-to-draft path. [HIST-GAME-01; S4]
U12 Preserve accepted parts through targeted repairs. Freeze accepted bytes/meaning; fix the named defect at its real display size and recheck affected neighbors. Updated corrected facts outrank obsolete original prompts for the same versioned task. Generator checks, independent review, user acceptance and production integration each need separate evidence. [HIST-CALM-01]
U13 Test the actual requirement, not a proxy. Assert outputs/state/invariants under realistic counterexamples; tests that repeat an acceptance list, find a keyword or sample only a small visual feature cannot establish full correctness. Follow child actions as well as parent guards. Keep test counts separated by evidence layer. [HIST-PAYCHECK-01; HIST-GAME-02; HIST-CREDIT-01]
U14 Transfer the production method as well as visible features. Preserve how sources were read, visuals selected/generated/reviewed, corrections recorded, accepted material carried into its owning builder and post-placement checks performed. A feature-parity list alone does not reproduce a successful process. [HIST-BIO20-01]
U15 Verify a correction's identity and dependencies. Before declaring a source wrong, match the exact question, figure, page, edition and assumptions. Trace an accepted correction through every dependent explanation, practice, key, feedback and derived asset while preserving historical response meaning. Do not reintroduce already repaired defects in a blanket refresh. [HIST-SCIENCE-01; HIST-CHEM-01]

Biology rules
B01 Use verified Alberta scope and exact textbook/slide/media locators. Teacher materials guide language and emphasis; old assessment keys are not infallible scientific authority. Resolve genuine errors with authoritative evidence and preserve protected questions pending teacher decision. [S2 section2; S1 contract]
B02 Explain physiology as condition → detection/source → message/route → target mechanism → effect/feedback. Explain structure through location, relation and function. For genetics/calculation define symbols, assumptions, allele contributions, probability operations, denominator, checks and biological meaning. [S2 section5; S5]
B03 Preserve the current native shell, full saved optional tasks, original prompts/options/keys/feedback/IDs, required-versus-optional progress, vocabulary/Frayer, reader mappings, approved labeling answers and state ownership. Verify which features exist in the actual owner. Historical builders and portable HTML are not replacement authorities. [S1/S3]
B04 Keep a chapter continuity ledger: terms taught, prerequisites supplied, examples used, misconceptions, unresolved dependencies and next topics. Work in complete connected batches, generally two or three lessons when suitable. Do not move routes or replace assessments as incidental authoring. [S2 process sections5–8; S1 README]
B05 Preserve source/model limits. Distinguish observation from inference, simplified inheritance assumptions from human/clinical claims, constructed data from actual observations, and a finite sample from proof. No personal family medical/genetic disclosure is required. [S1 accepted subset; S2; CH17 progression libfile_c889a315547c8191bafeaf10ccb2d43a]

ELA rules
E01 Preserve source teaching and every input's functional destination, rather than retaining labels alone. Separate optional teaching/practice, planning notes, prose components and complete sections. A complete section replaces its components in assembly; planning notes do not automatically become paragraphs. [S4 pp2–5]
E02 Keep guided planning, direct drafting and Word routes available. Learner-edited draft is authoritative: no silent replacement after a plan/evidence change. Practice model prose stays outside the learner assignment unless deliberately used as labelled practice. Verify exact save/reopen/export text and project isolation. [S4 pp1/5, F02–F07/P08]
E03 Teach assignment-specific writing. Personal Response can use suitable forms without forced private disclosure. ELA30-2 Visual Reflection, Literary Exploration and Persuasive Writing in Context require their actual task and rubric mappings; renaming a Critical Essay tool is insufficient. [S4 pp4–5]
E04 Supply checkable literary evidence and demonstrate interpretation. An instruction labelled Example is still an instruction. Do not invent quotations, plot events or an interpretation of an unavailable reading. A clearly labelled original practice text can model the move before transfer to the assigned reading. [S4 pp8–10; S5 actual Critical Essay inspection]

Additional subject and project rules
M01 Mathematics: teach the earliest consequential method decision, allow valid alternative methods, distinguish a correct intermediate result from a finished answer and give targeted repair before fresh unassisted application. Track actual structural coverage and fresh-item exhaustion; generated catalogue size and labelled mixed practice cannot establish broad readiness. [HIST-MATH-01]
C01 Chemistry: teach physical meaning before tool operation; show equation transformations/cancellation, physical states, coefficients, heat-flow sign and appropriate mass/mole denominators. Validate atoms/charges and arrows in visuals independently of attractive rendering. Keep supplied-data differences explicit instead of changing keys silently. [HIST-CHEM-01; HIST-SCIENCE-01]
S01 Science24: verify exact question/figure/page identity before correcting a key, then trace the correction across every dependent explanation/practice/check/transfer artifact. Preserve response-version meaning. A source image or beautifully generated art is not exempt from science review. [HIST-SCIENCE-01]
V01 Instructional graphics: assess exact data, units, proportional scale, date positions, connector endpoints, required/recommended distinctions, every visible label and alt-text correspondence at target desktop/mobile sizes. Reflow when needed while preserving meaning; do not confuse preserving data with freezing every coordinate. [HIST-CALM-01]
G01 Layered games: validate the actual character/garment/pose/layer invariant and representative complete runtime states before bulk expansion; freeze the build under test. Keep concept approval, fit approval, implementation and release distinct. Exact asset counts and art direction are per-project contracts. [HIST-GAME-01/02]
P01 Humanities course shells: retain learner-facing sources/resources and approved worksheet/library behavior. Removing backend terminology does not mean hiding pedagogical provenance; a prohibition on inline booklet screenshots does not prohibit a native PDF library. Numeric layout targets remain project-specific and require readable, usable output as well as pixel comparison. [HIST-AB30-01]

Accepted examples and regression cases
Accepted examples from CH17 lessons 06–07 are recorded below after independent source inspection. Their teacher acceptance is bounded to exact teaching copy, not scientific certification of every statement or functional release. [S1]
A01 Explain the model before the result. Lesson06, 'A flower cross with a different result' and 'Ask what the pink plants can pass on', explains R1/R2 notation, derives parental genotypes from breeding history, traces single-allele gametes through fertilisation, combines four equally weighted routes into 1:2:1 and interprets expected counts of 24/48/24 in 96 plants. It distinguishes sampling variation from impossible genotypes. Transfer the explicit reasoning, not the flower scenario. [S1 explicitly-accepted-subset/lesson-06.html, ch17-b2v010-l06-parents and ch17-b2v010-l06-segregation]
A02 Explain a visual's limits. Lesson06's 'Read the parents before the offspring' says its arrows omit gametes/fertilisation. Lesson07's 'What distinguishes the blood-group phenotypes?' explains that a generic red-cell image cannot reveal ABO type when antigen markers are unresolved. Its changed-parent example explains that repeated IA columns represent one distinct gamete type. Transfer the observation→interpretation→limit pattern. [S1 accepted lessons06/07]
A03 Change the actual reasoning demand. Lesson07 changes IAi × IBi to IAIA × IBi, uses an O grandfather to constrain an initially ambiguous parent genotype, and contrasts a joint probability with a conditional one. 'Which group is in the denominator?' distinguishes P(son and B)=1/4 from P(B given son)=1/2; 64 offspring predicts 16 type-B sons while 64 sons predicts 32. Transfer the explicit denominator and assumption changes rather than just new numbers. [S1 lesson 07, ch17-b2v010-l07-denominators and ch17-b2v010-l07-count-limits]
These examples were read independently from the frozen accepted subset for this consolidation. Teacher copy acceptance did not authorize correcting those links or establish reader functionality. [S1]

Preserve that visible biological reasoning and topic-based transitions. [S1 COPY_CHANGES]
F02 Known failure: ELA The Lamp at Noon Example asks the learner to focus on a conflict and turn it into an idea but never supplies a conflict or interpretive sentence. Retain useful directions; add a real model with evidence and annotation. [S5]
