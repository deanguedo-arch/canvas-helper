## Scientific adjudication

The following references support the candidate's relevant claims. This is a focused claim check, not a review of every clinical statement in the references.

1. **Cortisol pathway, feedback, metabolism and immune direction — Pass.** Endotext's publisher chapter identifies portal CRH regulation of anterior-pituitary ACTH, ACTH's cortical target, intracellular glucocorticoid receptors, inhibitory CRH/ACTH feedback and pulsatile baseline secretion. It supports hepatic gluconeogenesis, protein degradation, lipolysis and principally anti-inflammatory cortisol effects. Its coupled HPA/autonomic account supports simultaneous pathway activity. These are compatible with the candidate's qualified wording and do not support the opposite slide39 inflammation direction. Relevant PDF pages: 7–9, 21, 25–26. [Endotext, Stress: Endocrine Physiology and Pathophysiology, updated 15 October 2020](https://www.endotext.org/wp-content/uploads/pdfs/stress-endocrine-physiology-and-pathophysiology.pdf)

2. **Aldosterone controls and kidney/feedback chain — Pass.** The February 2026 publisher chapter supports cortex synthesis, distal sodium reabsorption and potassium excretion, angiotensin II/elevated potassium as principal stimuli, renal renin-mediated activation and reduced renin drive following improved perfusion. It describes ACTH influence as modest/transient while acknowledging complexity. The candidate's “not the principal sustained controller” retains that distinction without falsely claiming zero ACTH effect. Relevant PDF pages: 1–5. [Endotext, Hyperaldosteronism](https://www.endotext.org/wp-content/uploads/pdfs/hyperaldosteronism.pdf)

3. **Primary versus pituitary deficiency — Pass.** NIDDK distinguishes primary adrenal damage with cortisol and sometimes aldosterone deficiency from pituitary ACTH deficiency. Its treatment page says secondary insufficiency usually preserves enough aldosterone. The candidate's conditional primary-gland example and warning against assuming identical aldosterone loss from an ACTH problem are justified. [NIDDK definition and facts](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/endocrine-diseases/adrenal-insufficiency-addisons-disease/definition-facts), [NIDDK treatment](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/endocrine-diseases/adrenal-insufficiency-addisons-disease/treatment)

4. **Glycerol versus direct fat-to-glucose shortcut — Pass.** Primary tracer studies in humans distinguish fatty-acid mobilization/oxidation from glycerol delivery for new glucose synthesis. This supports the lesson's substrate distinction; it is not a claim that all released fat becomes glucose. [Wolfe and Peters, lipolytic response study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3826340/), [Fuel and energy metabolism in fasting humans](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0002916523183699)

ADH synthesis/release and kidney permeability are also directly supported by the actual supplied textbook Figure 13.9 and neighboring explanation. The candidate's gradient/permeability qualifications prevent a simplistic “aldosterone opens water channels” reading.
