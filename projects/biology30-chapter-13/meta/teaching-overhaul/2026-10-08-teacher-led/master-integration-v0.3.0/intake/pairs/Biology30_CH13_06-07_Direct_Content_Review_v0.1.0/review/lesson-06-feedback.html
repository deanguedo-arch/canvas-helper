Hypothalamic osmoreceptors detect increased concentration. More ADH is released from the posterior pituitary, increasing collecting-duct water permeability. More water is reabsorbed, urine volume falls and urine becomes more concentrated. Correction of body-fluid concentration reduces the stimulus.
