Written explanation 1

Maximum 3200 characters. Explain the biology in your own words.

Compare after saving your own response

Nervous communication uses electrical changes along neurons and chemical signals at junctions in defined pathways. Endocrine communication uses hormones in blood. Responsive target cells need appropriate receptors; wide hormone distribution does not mean a universal response.

Written explanation 2

Maximum 3200 characters. Explain the biology in your own words.

Blood glucose can rise after a meal. Responses such as insulin secretion and glucose storage then bring it back toward a suitable range. Variation and active correction are part of homeostasis.
