Homeostasis and endocrine communication

How do hormones help keep internal conditions within a workable range?

Learning goal

Explain homeostasis and compare endocrine communication with nervous-system communication.

Before you begin

Cells function best within suitable ranges of temperature, water balance, nutrient supply and other internal conditions.
