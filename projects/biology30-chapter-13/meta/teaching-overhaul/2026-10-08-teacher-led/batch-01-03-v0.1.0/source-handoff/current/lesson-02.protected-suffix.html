Written explanation 1

Maximum 3200 characters. Explain the biology in your own words.

Compare after saving your own response

Water-soluble hormones generally bind membrane receptors and trigger internal signalling. Steroid hormones can enter cells and bind intracellular receptors, influencing gene expression and protein production. Both require suitable target-cell machinery.

Check your explanation for:
  • Membrane receptor pathway
  • Intracellular pathway
  • Binding connected to response

A defective or absent receptor could prevent the cell from recognizing or signalling the hormone. This differs from a gland releasing too little hormone, where the receiving machinery may still work.
