Technology and the Promotion of Culture

Access to new media and communication technologies is very evident in entertainment, quick global contact at your fingertips, and in the pop culture side of the everyday lives of many people. This aspect of globalization influences economic, social, and political dimensions of society. These technologies can unify a community across great distances and geographical obstacles. With these tools, people can instantly be informed by a click of a mouse or a tap of a finger. These tools also create opportunities and challenges to maintaining unique cultures and languages.

Some who view globalization as limiting predict that these technologies will develop a homogenized pop culture that will dominate diverse cultures. Some would argue that this may even lead to a complete erosion of those cultures in favor of a single one, especially as people use these technologies more and more often. Those who recognize the potentials of globalization use these technologies to establish a space for the maintenance of their culture, language, and identity in a globalizing world. They may make the case that changes in technology and media gives a new voice to marginalized and oppressed cultures, and a way to stand up for themselves.

Lesson Summary

Cultural diversity in the twenty-first century can be challenged by global forces such as media and communication technologies. These technologies, which allow people rapid communication and exchange, can make room for expressions of cultural diversity, or they can replace such diversity with a universalized pop culture and language. In the next lesson you will examine how collective identities attempt to promote their unique culture and language in response to the challenges and opportunities globalization may present.
