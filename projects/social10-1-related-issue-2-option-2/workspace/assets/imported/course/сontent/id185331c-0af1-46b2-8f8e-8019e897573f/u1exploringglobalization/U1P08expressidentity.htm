Identity and Globalization

As individuals and collectives can express identity through language, your identity may also reflect the values and ideas you hold as important. You may have ideas about religion, spirituality, beliefs, and the connection to the land that you share with your family and community. Your role models may have an impact on whom you are or want to be. Explore the role of these ideas in how collectives and individuals express their identities. Afterwards, examine the impact that increased global contact between these diverse values and ideas may or may not have on your identity.

People can select and choose how to express their individual identities. As with your choice of language and words, you can choose to demonstrate the ideas and beliefs that are important to you and who you are. These expressions demonstrate to the people around you what you believe in and value.

As you begin to explore the concept of globalization, you will have many opportunities to think about the many ways that globalization affects your life and your identity.  Let's start by taking this short and hilarious quiz to get you thinking about your own identity.

Collective Identity

Collective identity refers to aspects of an individual's identity that are held in common with a larger group.

Look at the answers you provided to the questions above. What aspects of your identity can be linked to larger groups around you? If, for example skateboarding is an aspect of your identity, it may also link you to the collective group of individuals who skateboard.

What connects your identity to various collective identities, for example: Albertans, Canadians, human beings?

When you look around your community and observe the people around you, can you identify the values and beliefs that are significant to whom you are as a collective?

Now that you have a better idea of what contributes to your identity, you can better answer the question, "To what extent should globalization shape identity?"
