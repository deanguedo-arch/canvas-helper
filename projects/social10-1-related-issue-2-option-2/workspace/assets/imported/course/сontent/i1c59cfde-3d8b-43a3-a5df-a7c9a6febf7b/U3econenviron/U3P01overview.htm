Unit 3: Sustainable Prosperity

Overview

In the next part of the course, you will explore this issue question: Does globalization contribute to sustainable prosperity for all people? In order to answer this question, you need to understand it. What is sustainable prosperity? What are the foundations of economic globalization? When many people think about globalization, they think about its economic aspects. How did the world become so interconnected in terms of trade and finances? In Unit 3 you will look at the background to economic globalization, continuing on from the period of historical globalization that you learned about in Unit 2. You will think about what people really want out of life and whether our current way of doing things can be sustained. Does everyone want a world where all people are prosperous, or are most people content with a world where some people can live well and others suffer? What have individuals, nations, and international organizations done to create the world we want? What role does economic globalization play in creating a world that will benefit everyone?

The question this Unit addresses is: To what extent does globalization contribute to sustainable prosperity for all people?

Sustainable Prosperity has been defined as:

1. A state of economic development, where individuals, families and communities enjoy a high standard of living and a high quality of life, while respecting and preserving
the natural environment;

2. A new balance of economic competition with economic cooperation that promotes the efficient use and sharing of natural resources, technologies, knowledge and capital at the local, national and global levels;

3. The highest expression of a free-enterprise system, which affords social, economic,
political, cultural and artistic freedoms, side by side with individual responsibility,
mutual respect and special consideration for people truly in need.

Source: 2003 Joel Hodroff

In the previous unit you investigated issues surrounding historical globalization and imperialism. What about globalization today? Is it leading to a better life for all people?
