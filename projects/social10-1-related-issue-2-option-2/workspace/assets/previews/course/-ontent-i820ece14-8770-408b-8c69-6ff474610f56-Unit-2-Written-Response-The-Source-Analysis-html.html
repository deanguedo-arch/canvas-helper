The Source Analysis

In this type of writing you will be presented with three sources. These may be excerpts, graphs, political cartoons, pictures, etc. You will be expected to write an essay in which you analyze each source and identify and explain the relationship(s) that exist among the sources.

Essay Format

The essay should be in the following format:

Introduction:

  • Provide the purpose of the paper (1-2 sentences)

Paragraphs 1, 2, 3 (Sources 1, 2, and 3 respectively):

Interpret and explain the source and how it relates to globalization by following the steps below:

  • explain/ describe what you literally see in the source, or what the source is saying 
         ***make sure you indicate who would have said this (if applicable)
  • link a social studies concept (given to you in the questions) to the source
  • define the social studies concept
  • give examples as to how the source is an example of that concept
  • link the social studies concept to globalization.


Paragraph 4:

Identify and explain the relationship(s) that exists among the sources by referring to the following prompts:

  • What is the common social studies concept between all 3 sources?
  • Define the social studies concept.
  • Source 1 is an example of (the social studies concept) because…
  • Source 2 is an example of (the social studies concept) because…
  • Source 3 is an example of (the social studies concept) because…
  • Compare and contrast each source against the other two sources (you can also do this in steps c, d, e
  • Link the social studies concept back to globalization

Possible Sentence Starters


Paragraphs 1, 2, 3 (Sources 1, 2, and 3 respectively):

  • In Source One [what do you see or paraphrase if a quotation] …
  • The perspective of globalization being presented is… This is demonstrated by [prove it by referring to what you see]
  • One can see this [prove it by referring to current events/issues and USE TERMINOLOGY discussed in the unit] …


Paragraph 4 (Identify and explain the relationship(s) that exists among the sources):

  • Source One and Source Two are similar [be specific] because… Yet they are different because…
  • Common aspects of Source Two and Source Three [be specific] are… Yet they differ because…
  • Source One and Source Three demonstrate a connection because… The contrasting element present in Source One and Three is…
