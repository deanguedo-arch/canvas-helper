2.2 Specific Land Claims

Theme 2 - Aboriginal Land Claims

Respond in paragraph form using the response criteria. Using all the resources at your disposal, review TWO of the land claims from Alberta listed below.

Lubicon Lake Cree Land Claim; Siksika (Blackfoot) Submission; Woodland Cree Settlement; Blood-Cardston Claim; Loon River Settlement; Peigan Nation Claim; Nakoda (Stoney) Submission.

You may include the following elements:

  • The main events and issues of each claim
  • Include if, how and when they were settled
  • If they are not settled, explain why
  • A timeline of important events
