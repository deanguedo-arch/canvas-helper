The Registered Apprenticeship Program (RAP) provides senior high school students, with the support of a
certificated teacher, the opportunity to earn credits in high school and gain on-the-job training that can be applied
toward their apprenticeship education program.
Requirements
• 15 years of age or older;
• high school student;
• paid at least minimum wage;
• complete Education’s HCS3000: Workplace Safety Systems course;
