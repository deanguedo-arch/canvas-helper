MACBETH by William Shakespeare

GRAPHIC ESSAY



Create a thesis that argues for the role of ONE of the themes identified in MACBETH, and prove the validity of your thesis with quotations, explanations, and visuals. Remember, your thesis should be a compelling argument for what message Shakespeare was trying to convey, and should explain the connection between your chosen topic and humanity.







PROCEDURE



Follow the format of the affixed pages,



Your thesis is your title.



Select 3-5 quotations from the text to support your thesis. DO NOT translate the quotation. Ensure your quotations are properly cited (ex. V,3,41-42), properly indicate the line changes, judiciously selected (shorten longer quotations) and be from AT LEAST four different acts.



Beneath each quotation, concisely and thoroughly discuss their significance in developing your thesis.



Underneath each explanation, insert a graphic to support your quotation and its accompanying explanation. Do not illustrate the quotation. Modern or symbolic graphics best reflect your understanding on the theme. You may use original artwork, magazine photos and illustrations but NO computer clip art is allowed.



Below each graphic, explain its relevance and detail how each develops your thesis.



Conclude your graphic essay with an astute generalization about life or people relevant to the author's writing and the ideas you have explored.
