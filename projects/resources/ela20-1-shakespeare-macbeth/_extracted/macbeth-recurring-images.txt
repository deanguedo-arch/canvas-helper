Macbeth

Recurring Images

Context

There are a number of recurring images in Macbeth. Understanding and noting these
images is one way a reader navigates Shakespeare’s work. It is from these images that a
reader develops a statement of theme. Through this, the reader comes to understand
Shakespeare’s larger messages.

Assignment

Select two of the following images. For each of your selections, find two quotations from
each act. Make note of when your selected images occur, ensuring you properly cite each
quotation. Consider what each quotation says about each image; this will lead you to
stating a theme for the play.

ambition cruelty
