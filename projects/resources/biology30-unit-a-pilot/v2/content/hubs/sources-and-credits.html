How sources were used

The Alberta curriculum defines the required outcomes and performance expectations. Supplied classroom and CBE materials establish local scope and useful teaching examples. Current open and official health sources were used only to cross-check or correct explanations, health information, and technology examples.

All learner-facing scientific figures are original course redraws. Instruction was rebuilt for accessible web reading, so no presentation viewer or external lesson is required.
