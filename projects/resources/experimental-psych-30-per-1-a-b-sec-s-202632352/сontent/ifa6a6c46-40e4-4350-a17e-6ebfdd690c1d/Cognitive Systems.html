Cognitive Systems

Psychologists are most often concerned with cognitive systems when they review feedback loops.  For example, researchers might actually want to find out if negative thoughts about excess weight result in more negative thoughts about excess weight.

Researchers might design an experiment in which 50 overweight Caucasian women are asked to read negative material about being overweight.  Researchers would then track their thought processes for a week to see if they had more negative thoughts than usual about their weight (mass).  Results might show that the initial negative material about weight did indeed increase or promote more negative thoughts about weight.  Researchers may also find a very strong correlation between negative thoughts and the promotion of more negative thoughts.

If the researchers wanted to publish their results about weight and negative thoughts in a psychological journal, they would have to qualify what they write because the study was performed on women and only women.

* Would men react differently than the women in the study?

* Would race and cultural background effect the results?

* Would a difference be shown between an adolescent girl and a middle-aged woman?
