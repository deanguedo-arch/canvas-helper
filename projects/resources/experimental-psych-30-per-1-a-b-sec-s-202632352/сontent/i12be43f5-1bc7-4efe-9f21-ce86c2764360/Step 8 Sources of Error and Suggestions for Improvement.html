When reviewing sources of error in a lab report, focus on the following questions:

  • What factors could have affected the accuracy or consistency of the results?
  • Which parts of the procedure may have introduced bias, measurement problems, or uncontrolled variables?
  • What changes would improve the design if the experiment were repeated?
  • How could the next version of the experiment produce clearer or more reliable data?
