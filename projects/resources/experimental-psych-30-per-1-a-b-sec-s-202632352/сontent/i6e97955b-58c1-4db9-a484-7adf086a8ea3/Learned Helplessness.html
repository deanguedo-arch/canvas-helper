Learned Helplessness
I give up!!
For many years researchers were perplexed as to why some people, when in obviously negative situations, did not try to avoid the situations when they could.  For example, why did some people stay in abusive relationships?  Why did some people give up trying to learn new skills?   This case study provides insight into possible reasons for these behaviours.
