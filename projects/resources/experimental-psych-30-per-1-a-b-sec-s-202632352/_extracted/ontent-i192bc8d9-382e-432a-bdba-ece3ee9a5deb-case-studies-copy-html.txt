Case Studies
In this section you will review several case studies, some of which describe infamous experiments conducted in Psychology during the last century.
These case studies represent a small fraction of the experiments conducted since psychology became recognized as a science.  Some of the more gruesome and cruel experiments have been purposefully omitted.  You are encouraged to conduct your own research regarding specific topics of interest.  Libraries and the Internet provide much information for students wishing to learn more about almost anything!
As you read the eight case studies that follow, you may wish to make notes on specific concepts.  When you have finished reading each case study, please review the corresponding Getting you Thinking questions.  You need to have read the case studies before you begin Assignment Six.
