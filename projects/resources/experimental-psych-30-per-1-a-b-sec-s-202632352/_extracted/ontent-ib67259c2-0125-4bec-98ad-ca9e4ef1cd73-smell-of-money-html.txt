As you know, smell and taste are related so what we eat is influenced by what we smell. What about purchase decisions?  Can certain smells affect our spending habits?  ScentAir, one of several firms that design scents for businesses, exists because of the belief that smell affects consumer spending.  In fact, a growing body of research suggests "many of the emotion-triggering moments people remember on a given day are actually prompted by smell."  (Time, 2006)
Sony stores (SonyStyle) in the US wanted to make female shoppers more inclined to buy electronics from them and decided to add smell to their 37 US stores to help encourage this desired purchasing behaviour.  ScentAir designed a sampling of smells for Sony executives with the winning smell composed of orange, vanilla, and cedar-wood.  The cedar-wood scent was added to keep the male population happy!  Does smell make a difference!  Absolutely!What kind of smells would you put in a house if you wanted to make prospective buyers more likely to purchase the home?•    apple cinnamon?•    chocolate chip cookie dough?•    fresh bread?What kind of uses would people have for offensive odours?  In the US, the smell of burning electrical wire was created for use in a military simulation.  Other possibilities may include•    smells that deter animals from foraging in flower beds•    odours that prevent loitering near shopping malls
