Applied Psychology
All joking aside, the use of knowledge of how people behave is called applied psychology.  Teachers, advertisers, friends, and family use applied psychology frequently.   Have you ever wondered why milk is placed farthest from the entrance at grocery stores?  Do you know why phone numbers are "chunked" into three and four digit sections?  Do you know why some children do not get dessert until they eat their vegetables?The answers to these questions (and many more) relate to psychology.  Grocery store owners want customers to buy more than "just milk" so they place milk farthest away from the door in the hope that customers place some other product into their shopping baskets.Researchers in the area of memory and learning have found that "chunking" numerical information in three or four-digit groups makes the numbers easier to remember than a single sequence of seven or ten digits..
