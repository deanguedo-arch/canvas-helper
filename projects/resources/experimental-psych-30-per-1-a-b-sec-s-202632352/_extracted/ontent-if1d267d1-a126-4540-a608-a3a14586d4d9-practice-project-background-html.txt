Practice Project BackgroundDoreen Kimura, a behavioural psychologist, believes that male brains and female brains process information differently.  She noticed that with aphasias (speech disorders) caused by injury or disease, the brain damage was in the front of the brain in women.  In men, aphasias occur when the damage is in the back of the brain.
Kimura also studied the reaction time of men and women on matching exercises.  She found that, on average, men could pick out matching rotated objects (see below) faster than women.  However, women were better at matching objects based on their surroundings.  Kimura found that the farther someone has to rotate an object mentally, the longer the comparison takes. The speed at which the task is completed provides a general measure of spatial ability.
