Milgram's Obedience ExperimentsI don't have to think for myself...Stanley Milgram was interested in the justifications for acts of genocide offered by those accused at the World War II, Nuremberg War Criminal trials.  The defense of the accused was often based on obedience and the excuse that they were just following orders of their superiors.
