Eating and Behaviour
According to Wansink (2006), "we eat largely because of what's around us.  We overeat not because of hunger but because of family and friends, packages and plates, names and numbers, labels and lights, colors and candles, shapes and smells, distractions and distances, cupboards and containers."  Wansink knows this because of his extensive research and experimentation into the psychology of eating behaviours.
Wansink, a professor of marketing, became interested in doing research after he was asked a question about magazine covers.  The director of editorial research of a popular magazine asked Wansink which magazine cover of four would sell the most copies and why.  Each of the four covers had the same picture -- the only difference was the six cover blurbs or teaser phrases on the left side of the page.  Wansink told the editor that he would choose the cover with the shorter teaser phrases.  The editor, Ray, said that this choice would have cost the company over a million dollars in newsstand sales.  Ray explained that each month he took the cover samples (four or more) and asked over a thousand non-subscribers which version they would be most likely to buy.  The company did this research to better predict which magazine a "blond, 37 year-old mother of two would pick up, flip through, and buy when standing in the checkout line," (Wansink, 2006).
