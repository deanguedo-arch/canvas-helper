Brain Damage and Behaviour
Research has shown that the brain controls behaviour.  Studies have also shown that the brain can change physically when we learn new things or when we are injured in some way.  This case study, summarized from the journal Nature, suggests that ethical behaviour may be, at least in part, out of our conscious control.
