Getting You ThinkingBackground:  The photo from Guantanamo Bay (above right) shows prisoners wearing goggles, face masks, hearing protection, and mittens.  These items have been used in psychological experiments regarding sensory deprivation.  Sensory deprivation has been known to induce hallucinations and is likened to torture.  After the US Government released this photo (and others), some human rights groups said the pictures were reminiscent of scenes of torture during the Cold War.
