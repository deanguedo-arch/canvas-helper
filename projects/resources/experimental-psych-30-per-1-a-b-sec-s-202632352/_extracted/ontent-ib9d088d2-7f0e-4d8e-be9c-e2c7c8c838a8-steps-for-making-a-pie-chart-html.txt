Steps for Making a Pie Chart
Using a compass, draw a large circle on a blank page.
Give the pie chart a title.
Mark the centre of the circle with a point.  This is where each pie wedge will start.
Convert the data from percentages to angle degrees then measure a wedge for each value of the responding variable.
Example:
If 17.27% of students play no sports during the school year, the pie wedge would be 17.27% of the 360º circle, or 62º (360 x 0.1727 = 62).
Position a protractor at the centre point of the circle.  Mark 0º and      62º angles with points on the edge of the circle.  Draw a line from          these points to the centre of the circle.
Label the wedge and include its percentage.
Measure the next wedge from the edge of the first.  When completed, the entire circle should be filled and add up to 360º and 100%.  NOTE: For reasons of clarity, a pie chart should have no more than five wedges.
Order the wedges from largest to smallest, starting at "noon" and progressing clockwise.
