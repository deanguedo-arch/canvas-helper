Mode, Median, and Mean
The mode is the number that occurs most often in a set of data.  To determine the mode, you should first organize the numbers in order of size.  Using our temperature values from the previous page, the new order would be as follows:
The median is the number in the middle of the set of data after you arrange the numbers in order of size.  In many cases, you will have the same number of values on the left and right of the median value.  If, however, there is an even number of data points, you will have two numbers as your median and the values must be averaged.  In our case, we have 11 data points.  The median is data point six.
The mean is the value obtained when you add all the values in a data set and then divide the sum (total) by the number of values in the data set.  In our case, there are 11 values.
