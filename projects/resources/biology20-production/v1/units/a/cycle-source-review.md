# Unit A cycle authoring review

Scope: teacher slide text 58–77, six authored teaching sections and their worked/self-check/Advanced companions. Originals unchanged. This is not full curriculum clearance or rendered-figure review.

- Carbon: distinguish a reservoir from a net sink, and stock from flow. Include exported carbon without assuming its immediate release to the atmosphere.
- Nitrogen: retain ammonium and nitrate uptake, distinguish fixation/nitrification/assimilation, include consumers eating other consumers, and make denitrification conditional. Crop rotations do not guarantee unlimited nitrate.
- Sulfur: separate weathering, combustion, atmospheric reactions, wet/dry deposition and uptake. The [EPA acid-deposition explanation](https://www.epa.gov/acidrain/what-acid-rain) supports the sulfur-dioxide/nitrogen-oxide and wet/dry distinctions, not universal regional emission ratios.
- Safety: the [NIOSH hydrogen-sulfide entry](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/npg/npgd0337.html) explicitly rejects smell as a reliable continuing warning. The authored lesson prohibits gas-generation, collection and deliberate-smelling activities.
- Phosphorus: distinguish available phosphate from mineral-bound stores and avoid universal nutrient rankings. Respiration occurs in light and darkness. Oxygen depletion is conditional, not inevitable complete lake death.
- Geological evidence: [NPS stromatolite guidance](https://www.nps.gov/articles/park-paleo-fall-2018-stromatolites.htm) supports a microbial-mat/sediment explanation rather than piles of dead cells. Iron mineral evidence is treated separately; no exact oxygen chronology is asserted. Historical USGS iron-formation search results were not used to certify a modern chronology. Full geological/textbook reconciliation remains open.

All new numerical tables are explicitly illustrative, not empirical datasets. Self-check explanations are revealed support, not collected independent evidence. Optional times, reviewed figures, saved evidence, textbook pages and complete outcome bindings remain pending.
