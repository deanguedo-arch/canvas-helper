In Lesson 1 you practised observing leaves from two different locations. You were observing and taking measurements of a biotic component of the environment. By forming hypotheses, you speculated on the relationship between biotic components and abiotic characteristics of the ecosystem. If you had to measure abiotic characteristics of the environment, such as air temperature or depth of light penetration in a pond, what tools would you use?  
Keep this in mind as you work through Chapter 3 in your textbook.
