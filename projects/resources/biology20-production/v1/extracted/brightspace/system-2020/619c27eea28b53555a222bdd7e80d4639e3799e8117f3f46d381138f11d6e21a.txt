How to Make a Dichotomous Key for Shoes
 The six objects are all shoes.
 They all have different uses and shapes.
 There will be five steps in the key (number of objects – 1 = number of steps).
 One possible key is as follows:
 Has laces ……………………………………….. go to step 2
 Has no laces …………………………………… go to step 3
 Is a boot ………………………………………… hiking boot
 Is not a boot ……………………………………... go to step 4
 Has a big heel …………………………………… high heel shoe
 Has a small heel …………………………………. go to step 5
 Is black in colour ………………………………… dress shoe
 Is not black ………………………………………. running shoe
 Straps have buckles ……………………………… sandal
 Straps don’t have buckles ……………………….. flip flop
