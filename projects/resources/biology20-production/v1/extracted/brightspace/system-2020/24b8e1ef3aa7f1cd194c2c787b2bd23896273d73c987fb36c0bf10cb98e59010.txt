Biology 20: Important Course Information
Textbook
The textbook required for Biology 20 is Inquiry into Biology, McGraw-Hill Ryerson. You can not complete this course without accessing a textbook.
Citing References
In this course you will be doing assignments that require independent research. Here is a reference guide on how to reference your sources http://www.citationmachine.net/apa/cite-a-book If you choose to use pictures from the internet or other sources, you will have to cite those as well.  
Wikipedia
Though Wikipedia can be a great starting point, it is NOT a reliable resource. Wikipedia can be edited by anyone at anytime and though much of the information can be accurate, there is always the possibility it is not. Please do not site Wikipedia as a resource as I will not count it.
