Biology 20
In order to be successful in the Biology 20 course, please keep in mind that you should:
Check in everyday
Dedicate 10 to 15 hours per week to spending on the course
Biology 20 has a lot of material to cover in a very short amount of time.  The concepts are not complicated but there are a lot.  This being said that means there are a lot of assignments in this course.  You should be prepared to complete a lesson 3 to 4 times a week, just like you would in a traditional classroom.  The only difference being that with an online class you can choose when you put the time in.
