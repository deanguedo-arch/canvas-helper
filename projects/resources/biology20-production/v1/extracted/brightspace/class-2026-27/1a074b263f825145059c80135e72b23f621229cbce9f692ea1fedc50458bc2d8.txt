Welcome to Brightspace!
**Please follow these instructions closely!**
Turn ON some notifications:
Click your name in the top right corner and then click on notifications
Under Contact Methods make sure it's showing your correct @g.eips.ca email and then PLEASE register a mobile (cell) number.  The preferred method of contact will be emailing.
Under Instant Notifications please turn on both Announcement options and the Quizzes option (and any others you may want - but those are MUST HAVES!)
Optional: You may want to download the Brightspace App to your devices for added convenience when receiving notifications:
DISCLAIMER: Do not access your course work or assessments using a mobile device - several features do NOT work properly which will cause problems for you. Always access your course using a chromebook, laptop or PC!

Brightspace Pulse is a mobile app for students that can help you stay connected and on track with your Brightspace courses. It gives you one easy view of course calendars, readings, assignments, evaluations, grades, and news. The app will help you to make better decisions about how to handle your workload, when to submit assignments, and when to prepare for tests.
