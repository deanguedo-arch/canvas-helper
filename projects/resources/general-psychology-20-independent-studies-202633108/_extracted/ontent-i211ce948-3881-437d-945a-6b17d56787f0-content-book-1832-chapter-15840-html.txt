Fear is one of the usual reasons why a person runs away from a situation. Even well adjusted people may regress on occasion in order to reduce anxiety or stress.</p><p><i>For example: Daydreaming is a regressive activity; it involves withdrawing into the self to secure pleasure by wish fulfillment. Another example is inventing an imaginary illness in order to gain sympathy and attention from others. Bursting into tears at the slightest hint of frustration or getting drunk are further examples of regression.</i></p><p>Sometimes regressive behavior is clearly childish in nature.</p><p><i>For example: Sue had a hard day at work. As soon as she got home, she curled up in bed with her teddy bear.</i></p>
