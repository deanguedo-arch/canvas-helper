General Psychology 20 Final Project

Complete the following tasks and submit them to your teacher.
This project is worth 20% of your final mark in this course.
Part A:
Flip back through the course information and make a list of the 3 most interesting
things you learned in this course.
For each interesting idea/item, write two or three sentences explaining why you found
it interesting.
Value: 15 marks (5 marks x 3)

Part B:
From the list you created in Part A, choose the most interesting thing you learned in
this course.
Do some background research on the topic/idea and synthesize your learning by
completing one of the following activities:
Poster illustrating the concept
Series of Poems (minimum five)
3-dimensional sculpture
Crossword Puzzle
Brochure/Pamphlet
Cartoon/Comic Strip
Timeline
Photo Essay
Video
Mobile
Song Parody
Board Game
Graphic Organizer/Mind Map

Diorama
Storyboard
Series of Diary Entries (minimum five)
Letter to a prominent psychologist: Sigmund Freud, B. F. Skinner, Karl Jung,
Ivan Pavlov, etc.
Power Point Presentation of the most interesting features

Value: 35 marks

Total:   50 marks
