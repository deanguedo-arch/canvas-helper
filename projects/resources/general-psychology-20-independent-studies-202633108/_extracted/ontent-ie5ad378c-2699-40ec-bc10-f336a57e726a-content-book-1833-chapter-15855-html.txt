<p>In North American society, the status of men has always been higher than that of women.� How a society determines the status of gender is still a mystery.� It is not based on a contribution to survival, as that is either a shared role, or in many cases, the women s role.� In some societies the status of both genders is approaching equality. In others, men are dominant.</p>
