<p>According to Maslow, each person follows an identical blueprint or pattern in order to satisfy physical and psychological needs. <p>There are two important principles to remember when studying the pyramid. First, each need <b>does not have to be satisfied 100%</b> before a person becomes challenged by the next level of needs.</p>
