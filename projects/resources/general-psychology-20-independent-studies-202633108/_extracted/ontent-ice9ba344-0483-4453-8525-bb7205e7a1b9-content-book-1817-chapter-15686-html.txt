</div><p>A second goal of psychology is to <strong>explain the data that has been collected.</strong> That task may seem straightforward but it is complicated by a number or factors. One of these obstacles is the psychologist s basic theory about the nature of human beings. Are people born  bad  or  born in sin ? Do parents have to firmly discipline and mold the actions of the young in order to curb this  bad  streak they have? Are people born  good ? Do people have a natural tendency to follow a behavior pattern that is  right and good  if they are given a chance? Are people completely neutral as newborn babies with no inclination to follow either pathway? Do parents have complete control to shape the responses of a baby in either a positive way or a negative way?</p><div>
</div><p>These questions are fascinating. You may be relieved to know that opinions about these questions swing from one extreme to another among knowledgeable psychologists around the world.</p><div>
