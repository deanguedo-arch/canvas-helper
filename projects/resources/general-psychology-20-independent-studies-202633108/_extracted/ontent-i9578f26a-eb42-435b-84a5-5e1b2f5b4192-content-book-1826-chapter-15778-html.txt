<p>Memory is improved through <b>repetition</b>. If we repeat things, especially over a long period of time, we are much more likely to remember them using our long-term memory.</p>
<h5><b>Strong stimulus</b></h5>
<p>A strong stimulus - including colour, light, smell or sound - also helps us to remember things.</p>
<p>For example, which of these are you more likely to remember?</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Incident 1: The sound of a car, braking suddenly, to avoid a cat crossing the road.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Incident 2: Your mother asking you to pick up a loaf of bread on your way home.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>You are more likely to remember Incident 1, as the stimulus is stronger.</p>
