<h5><b>POSITIVE TRANSFER</b></h5>
<div>
<p>If the prior learning is helpful to later learning,�<b>positive transfer</b>�has occurred. For example, if Wayne Gretzky's daughter wanted to learn how to roller blade, she could count on her dad to help her because the skills needed for both sports are very similar.</p>
<p>A special kind of positive transfer is�<b>simulation</b>. A simulation is an imitation of a certain exercise or set conditions that is very similar to a real life situation. Real life conditions are duplicated to give the learner close to first-hand experience.�Simulations are used for the purpose of training or experimentation.</p>
<ul>
<li>For example, in first aid classes, students practice applying bandages, doing the Heimlich maneuver, and performing CPR on mannequins in order to be ready to administer help in a real emergency.</li>
