<p dir="ltr"><span>It is important to realize that stringent dieting plays a key role in problems of this nature but eating disorders are </span><span>not </span><span>simply cases of dieting that has become out of control.</span></p>
<p dir="ltr"><span>Eating disorders are </span><span>not</span><span> just about trying to live up to media images of attractiveness, though social pressure to be thin may be a key factor.</span></p>
<p dir="ltr"><span>Eating disorders can easily arise out of a response to distress and worries about areas of life other than body image or dieting.</span></p>
<p dir="ltr"><span>Food is only part of the story.</span></p>
<p dir="ltr"><span>Eating disorders are complex and can be life-threatening illnesses. They involve </span><span>biological</span><span> and </span><span>psychological factors.</span></p>

<h4 dir="ltr"><span>Clinical symptoms of Anorexia Nervosa</span></h4>
<ul>
<li dir="ltr">
<p dir="ltr">At least 15% below normal body weight</p>
</li>
<li dir="ltr">
<p dir="ltr">Person sees himself or herself as overweight even when extremely thin</p>
</li>
<li dir="ltr">
<p dir="ltr">Person is terrified of weight gain</p>
</li>
<li dir="ltr">
<p dir="ltr">Food and weight are obsessions</p>
</li>
<li dir="ltr">
<p dir="ltr">Compulsive behavior around food</p>
</li>
<li dir="ltr">
<p dir="ltr">Amenorrhea in females (menstruation stops)</p>
</li>
<li dir="ltr">
<p dir="ltr">Impotence in males</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><img src="https://www.healthtopia.net/sites/default/files/bulimia.png" width="800" height="598" style="display: block; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;"></p>
<h4 dir="ltr"><span>Clinical symptoms of Bulimia Nervosa</span></h4>
<ul>
<li dir="ltr">
<p dir="ltr"><span>Consumption of large amounts of food (bingeing), followed by purging or exercising obsessively</span></p>
</li>
<li dir="ltr">
<p dir="ltr"><span>Obsession with body weight and shape</span></p>
</li>
<li dir="ltr">
<p dir="ltr"><span>Bingeing and purging from once or twice a week to several times a day</span></p>
</li>
<li dir="ltr">
<p dir="ltr"><span>Low self-esteem and fear of failure is typical (may not always be apparent)</span></p>
</li>
<li dir="ltr">
<p dir="ltr"><span>Strong need for acceptance and reassurance</span></p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><span><img src="..\image_chapter_15883(1).jpeg" width="240" height="180" style="display: block; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;"></span></p>
<h4 dir="ltr"><span>Different types of explanations</span></h4>
<p dir="ltr"><span>It is sometimes argued that anorexia nervosa and bulimia nervosa are </span><span>variations</span><span> of the same abnormality and cannot be easily distinguished, many sufferers have experienced both types of disorder. Psychologists tend to explain anorexia nervosa and bulimia nervosa together.</span></p>
<p dir="ltr"><span>The following factors can be involved:</span></p>
<ul>
<li dir="ltr">
<p dir="ltr"><span>Biochemistry</span></p>
</li>
<li dir="ltr">
<p dir="ltr"><span>Genetics</span></p>
</li>
<li dir="ltr">
<p dir="ltr"><span>Personality</span></p>
</li>
<li dir="ltr">
<p dir="ltr"><span>Social and family environment</span></p>
</li>
<li dir="ltr">
<p dir="ltr"><span>Media images and messages about food and dieting</span></p>
</li>
</ul>

<h4 dir="ltr"><span>Ways to help someone with an eating disorder</span></h4>
<ul>
<li dir="ltr">
<p dir="ltr"><span>Call a local help line or clinic for expert advice.</span></p>
</li>
<li dir="ltr">
<p dir="ltr"><span>Understand possible causes of eating disorders, food alone is not the main issue.</span></p>
</li>
<li dir="ltr">
<p dir="ltr"><span>Don't make judgments.</span></p>
</li>
<li dir="ltr">
<p dir="ltr"><span>Try to offer, caring support and help the person feel valued for other qualities than looks.</span></p>
</li>
<li dir="ltr">
<p dir="ltr"><span>A person with an eating disorder may not accept that they need help, family and friends will need to offer encouragement and information.</span></p>
</li>
<li dir="ltr">
<p dir="ltr"><span>Find out as much as you can so that you understand what they are going through, but don't try to act an expert yourself.</span></p>
</li>
<li dir="ltr">
<p dir="ltr"><span>Encourage the person to find something that they enjoy doing to channel their energy into.</span></p>
</li>
</ul></p></body></html>
