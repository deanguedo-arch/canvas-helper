<p>If a situation (stimulus) occurs and a young child responds to it in a favourable way, a parent might attempt to strengthen that connection by providing <strong>reinforcement</strong>.</p>
<p><strong>Reinforcement</strong> is a process that increases the chances of a certain behavior occurring again.</p>
<p><em>For example, there is a pile of toys in the living room (stimulus), the child puts them away in the toy box (response). The parent is pleased with the child s behavior and rewards the child with a cookie and praise (reinforcement).</em></p>
