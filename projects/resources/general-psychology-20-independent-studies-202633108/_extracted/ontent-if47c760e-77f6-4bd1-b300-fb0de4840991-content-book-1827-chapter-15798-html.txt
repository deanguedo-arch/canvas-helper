<p><b>Plateaus</b> (periods of little or no noticeable progress in learning) are temporary standstills in the learning process. A great deal of learning may occur both before and after the plateau.�</p>
<p><strong>Plateaus occur for many reasons:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>The learner may become bored or lose interest after the initial excitement of a new topic.</li>
</ul>
<ul>
<li>The learner may make a change in the earlier work or study procedures which may cause them to lose ground in learning progress for a while.�</li>
</ul>
<ul>
<li>If a learner does not have a solid foundation in the original concepts, a plateau will likely occur.</li>
</ul>
<ul>
<li>Anxiety in the learning situation will cause tension which may lead to a plateau.</li>
</ul>
