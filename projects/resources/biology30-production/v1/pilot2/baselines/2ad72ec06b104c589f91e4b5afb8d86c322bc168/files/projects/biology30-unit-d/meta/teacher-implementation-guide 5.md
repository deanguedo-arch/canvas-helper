# Biology 30 — Unit D: Population and Community Dynamics — Teacher Implementation Guide

Candidate build: 7ba99e1543dea3a164ab64ae2bdafaf10858d47e091f12e9d865e49015556fca

The course is independent-first and asynchronous. Required learning totals 900 minutes; optional learning totals 180 minutes and never blocks completion. Learners complete 11 lesson exits, 6 artifact checkpoints, and one submission of the 24-item formative final practice.

All investigations provide safe local models or supplied-data paths. Classroom activities must not be treated as clinical, genetic, or ecological diagnosis. Practice remains non-graded; the secure Unit D test remains a separate native Brightspace activity.
