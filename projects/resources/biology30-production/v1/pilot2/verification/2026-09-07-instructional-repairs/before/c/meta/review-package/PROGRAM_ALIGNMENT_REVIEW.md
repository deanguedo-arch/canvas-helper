## Intended progression to test

The proposed progression is:

1. **A — control and regulation:** homeostasis, nervous-system signalling, sensory systems, endocrine feedback, and integrated regulation.
2. **B — reproduction and development:** reproductive structures and control systems, gamete formation, cycles, fertilization, development, health, and reproductive technologies.
3. **C — cell division, inheritance, and molecular biology:** chromosomes and ploidy, mitosis/meiosis, inheritance and probability, DNA replication, gene expression, mutation, and biotechnology.
4. **D — population and community dynamics:** gene pools and Hardy-Weinberg models, mechanisms of change, population growth, ecological relationships, succession, and management evidence.

This ordering is a review hypothesis derived from the canonical unit titles, lesson sequences, and outcome contracts. Confirm prerequisite handoffs instead of treating the order itself as proof of alignment.

## Handoffs ChatGPT should inspect

- **A → B:** Does B briefly reactivate the A ideas of control systems, feedback, signals, targets, timing, and evidence before using them in reproductive regulation? Does B teach its own reproductive mechanisms rather than assume the A endocrine lessons are fresh?
- **B → C:** Does C make the bridge from gamete formation and chromosome number in B to meiosis, ploidy, inheritance, and probability explicit? Check whether vocabulary and diagrams are introduced before the first calculation or cross.
- **C → D:** Does D make the bridge from alleles, genotypes, probability, variation, and evidence in C to gene-pool frequencies and population change explicit? Check whether Hardy-Weinberg is presented as a model with assumptions and limits rather than as an isolated formula exercise.
- **Across A-D:** Compare conceptual demand, reading demand, mathematics/data demand, vocabulary load, practice cognitive levels, visual/model complexity, and planned learner workload. A reading grade estimate alone cannot establish that one unit is easier or harder.

## What counts as alignment

For each unit and handoff, look for: (1) an outcome stated in observable terms, (2) explicit teaching before required practice, (3) practice that requires the same operation as the outcome, (4) an evidence task or artifact that shows transfer, (5) prerequisite vocabulary and representations introduced before use, and (6) a reasonable progression from recall/interpretation to analysis/application. Flag a mismatch when the contract claims a mapping but the learner course does not visibly teach or practice the operation.
