- Screen source: `04_MOCKUPS/html/*.html`
- Reference previews: `04_MOCKUPS/png/*.png`; generate browser baselines from the exact HTML before comparing production
- Assets: `03_ASSETS/asset_manifest.json`
- Content: `02_DATA/scenarios.json`
- Answers/feedback: `02_DATA/answer_feedback_rules.json`
- State: `02_DATA/state_schema.json`
