# B1 — Energy Chain Rescue Production Design Specification

**Status:** PRODUCTION_HANDOFF  
**Unit:** B — Understanding Common Energy Conversion Systems  
**Tagline:** Repair the pathway. Account for every joule.

## Learning purpose
Repair source→converter→output chains, allocate every supplied joule, identify the useful output for the stated purpose, and explain the full account.

## Curriculum claims
B1, B2, B3. These are targeted-practice claims, not complete unit coverage.

## Direct exam-preparation demands
- Identify energy forms, sources and converters.
- Trace multi-stage transformations in order.
- Account for useful and less-useful outputs without saying energy disappeared.
- Separate matter flow from energy transfer in biological systems.
- Interpret photosynthesis and cellular respiration as linked energy-conversion contexts.
