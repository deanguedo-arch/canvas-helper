# A1 — Reaction Detective Production Design Specification

**Status:** PLAYABLE_REFERENCE  
**Unit:** A — Applications of Matter and Chemical Change  
**Tagline:** Use evidence to explain a change.

## Learning purpose
Evidence-board investigation: predict, reveal records, select relevant evidence, make a claim, justify a response, revise, and transfer.

## Curriculum claims
A1, A2, A4. These are targeted-practice claims, not complete unit coverage.

## Current course placement guardrails
- Chapter 2 — Common Reactions
- Chapter 3 — Types of Chemical Reactions
- Chapter 4 — Reactions and the Environment

## Direct exam-preparation demands
- Distinguish an observation from evidence that identifies a new substance.
- Classify physical and chemical changes and recognize common reaction categories.
- Interpret temperature change as supporting—not automatically conclusive—evidence.
- Use multiple records to make a claim and justify a practical or environmental response.
- Apply the reasoning to a fresh conventional question without the evidence-board scaffolds.

## Canonical workspace
**The Case / The Evidence / Your Reasoning**

## Mission progression
| Scenario | Support level | Mission | Accepted result / evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1-W01 | worked | Peroxide Decomposition | Chemical change; decomposition |
| A1-P01 | supported | Cooling Solution | Physical change after identity comparison |
| A1-P02 | practice | Corroding Tool | Chemical change; corrosion/rusting |
| A1-P03 | practice | Antacid Record | Acid–carbonate reaction within the unit's neutralization context |
| A1-P04 | low_support | Methane Combustion | Combustion |
| A1-T01 | transfer | Boiling Water | Physical change; change of state |

## Mastery evidence
- scientifically valid result;
- relevant model/evidence use;
- explanation or calculation appropriate to the task;
- independent fresh transfer with the worked solution hidden.

## Boundaries
- No speed scoring or repeated-guess mastery.
- No personal/student data.
- No scientific labels or values baked into context art.
- Open reasoning is not certified through keyword matching.
- All authored numbers are illustrative teaching data unless the source record says otherwise.
