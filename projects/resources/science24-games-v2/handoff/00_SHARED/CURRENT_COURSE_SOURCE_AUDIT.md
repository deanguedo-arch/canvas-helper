## Guardrail findings

1. The deployed course strongly emphasizes conventional recognition, calculation, graph/table interpretation, short explanation and application questions. Each game's final transfer screen therefore removes most game scaffolding and presents a conventional Science 24 task.
2. Unit A repeatedly requires evidence of chemical change, reaction classification, formula interpretation, balancing and environmental consequences.
3. Unit B requires energy transformation/conservation, electrical-energy use over time, efficiency, biological energy conversion and fuel contexts.
4. Unit C requires disease causes/routes, societal and body defences, immunity and simplified inheritance/probability.
5. Unit D requires reaction time, speed/distance/time, graphs, momentum/collisions and safety-device reasoning.
6. The supplied course outline appears to contain a copy/paste mismatch under Unit D: wording about balanced equations/stoichiometry does not match the Unit D textbook, workbook or assessments. It is flagged and is **not** used as authoritative Unit D scope.

## Source-use rules

- Do not copy assessment questions into the game.
- Use the current course vocabulary where it remains scientifically sound.
- Recheck terminology that can be oversimplified: acid–base versus acid–carbonate, efficiency versus lower power, simple inheritance assumptions, prevention versus treatment, average versus peak force.
- Generated context images never carry answer-critical labels, values, routes, graphs or formulas.
- All scientific numbers in this package are clearly authored instructional data unless explicitly sourced.
