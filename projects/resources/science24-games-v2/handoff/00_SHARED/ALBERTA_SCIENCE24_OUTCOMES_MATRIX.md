## Coverage boundary

The eight games are **supplemental practice**, not a claim of complete course coverage. A game earns an outcome claim only where a named mission teaches the concept and the learner independently produces relevant evidence. Broader content—including historical chemistry, fossil-fuel formation, nutrition, complex genetics, ethics and evaluation of regulations—remains in the course unless separately designed.
