## Responsive behavior
Desktop uses the game-specific three-zone workspace: **Service Brief / Device Plan / Budget Check**. At tablet/mobile widths, stack the same zones in task order; never shrink critical labels to preserve the desktop composition. Previous/Next controls appear at both the top/bottom or remain immediately discoverable after long content.
