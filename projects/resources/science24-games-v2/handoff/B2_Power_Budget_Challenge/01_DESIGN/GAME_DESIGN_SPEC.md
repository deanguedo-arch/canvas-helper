## Direct exam-preparation demands
- Use E = P × t with compatible units.
- Distinguish W and kW from Wh and kWh.
- Add energy across multiple devices and operating periods.
- Evaluate a plan against both an energy cap and mandatory service requirements.
- Calculate efficiency from useful output divided by input.
