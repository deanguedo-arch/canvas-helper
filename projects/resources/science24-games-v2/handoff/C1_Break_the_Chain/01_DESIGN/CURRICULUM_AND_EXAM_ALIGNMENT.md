## Course-source guardrail
This game draws its vocabulary and skill boundaries from the supplied current Science 24 course materials listed below. It does not reproduce secure assessment wording.

- Chapter 9 — Disease
- Chapter 10 — Society's Response
- Chapter 11 — Protecting Yourself

## Exam-mode gate
The transfer screen must be solvable from conventional text/data/diagram representations. A student who can operate the game interface but cannot complete that transfer has not demonstrated the intended outcome.
