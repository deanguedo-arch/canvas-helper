# C1 — Break the Chain Production Design Specification

**Status:** PRODUCTION_HANDOFF  
**Unit:** C — Disease Defence and Human Health  
**Tagline:** Match each prevention measure to the route it interrupts.

## Learning purpose
Inspect a deterministic route map, allocate limited intervention cards, replay which routes remain, explain the result, and transfer to a new setting.

## Curriculum claims
C1, C2, C3, C5. These are targeted-practice claims, not complete unit coverage.

## Current course placement guardrails
- Chapter 9 — Disease
- Chapter 10 — Society's Response
- Chapter 11 — Protecting Yourself

## Direct exam-preparation demands
- Distinguish communicable and non-communicable causes.
- Trace a pathogen through an explicitly supplied transmission route.
- Match a prevention measure to the route it interrupts.
- Distinguish external prevention, treatment, innate defence and adaptive immune memory.
- Explain why one measure may leave another route open.

## Canonical workspace
**Case Evidence / Transmission Map / Intervention Plan**

## Mission progression
| Scenario | Support level | Mission | Accepted result / evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C1-W01 | worked | Food Preparation | Use one measure on each stated route; addressing only the board leaves the hand route open. |
| C1-P01 | supported | Shared Water Station | Choose route-specific measures; multiple supported combinations may be valid. |
| C1-P02 | practice | Indoor Respiratory Case | Ventilation and source reduction address the supplied routes; table cleaning alone does not. |
| C1-P03 | practice | Layered Community Centre | Use evidence-supported prevention; antibiotics are treatment for some bacterial infections, not a universal route blocker. |
| C1-P04 | low_support | Body Defence Sequence | Immune memory belongs to adaptive immunity; vaccination prepares a response and is not an instant cure. |
| C1-T01 | transfer | Two-Route Transfer | Choose two route-matched measures and explain any remaining route. The model does not predict real infection probability. |

## Mastery evidence
- scientifically valid result;
- relevant model/evidence use;
- explanation or calculation appropriate to the task;
- independent fresh transfer with the worked solution hidden.
