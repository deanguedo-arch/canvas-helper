# C1 Content Guardrails

## Approved concept boundary
- Distinguish communicable and non-communicable causes.
- Trace a pathogen through an explicitly supplied transmission route.
- Match a prevention measure to the route it interrupts.
- Distinguish external prevention, treatment, innate defence and adaptive immune memory.
- Explain why one measure may leave another route open.

## Prohibited shortcuts
- Do not create a quiz skin disconnected from the model.
- Do not copy secure assessment questions.
- Do not turn an illustrative model into a real-world prediction/certification.
- Do not silently simplify uncertainty, units, element-by-element counts, routes, systems or signs.
- Do not use generated art for answer-critical science.

## Teacher review questions
- Does every accepted answer follow from the supplied evidence/model?
- Are valid alternatives preserved where the science allows them?
- Does misconception feedback point to the exact issue rather than reveal the whole answer immediately?
- Does the transfer task assess the same concept in a changed context or representation?
