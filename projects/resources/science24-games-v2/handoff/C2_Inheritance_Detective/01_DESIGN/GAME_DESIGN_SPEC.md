# C2 — Inheritance Detective Production Design Specification

**Status:** PRODUCTION_HANDOFF  
**Unit:** C — Disease Defence and Human Health  
**Tagline:** Infer what the evidence allows—and what it does not.

## Learning purpose
Deduce possible genotypes, eliminate contradictions, construct a Punnett square, quantify outcomes, and solve an underdetermined transfer case honestly.

## Curriculum claims
C4. These are targeted-practice claims, not complete unit coverage.

## Current course placement guardrails
- Chapter 12 — Genetics and Health

## Direct exam-preparation demands
- Distinguish gene, allele, genotype and phenotype.
- Build and interpret a Punnett square.
- Infer possible genotypes from a fictional pedigree.
- Calculate probabilities and explain that each outcome is not guaranteed.
- Preserve uncertainty when evidence supports more than one genotype.

## Canonical workspace
**Case File / Pedigree Evidence / Genotype & Probability**

## Mastery evidence
- scientifically valid result;
- relevant model/evidence use;
- explanation or calculation appropriate to the task;
- independent fresh transfer with the worked solution hidden.

## Boundaries
- No speed scoring or repeated-guess mastery.
- No personal/student data.
- No scientific labels or values baked into context art.
- Open reasoning is not certified through keyword matching.
- All authored numbers are illustrative teaching data unless the source record says otherwise.

## Model assumptions learners must see
Fictional single autosomal locus; A is completely dominant to a; full penetrance within the authored puzzle only.
