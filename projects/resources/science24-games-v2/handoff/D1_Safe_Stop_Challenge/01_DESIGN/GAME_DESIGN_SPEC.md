# D1 — Safe Stop Challenge Production Design Specification

**Status:** PRODUCTION_HANDOFF  
**Unit:** D — Safety in Transportation  
**Tagline:** Predict the stopping point. Read the motion graph.

## Learning purpose
Predict reaction travel and total stop, run a slow deterministic replay, compare a synchronized graph, revise, and solve a fresh case.

## Curriculum claims
D1, D2. These are targeted-practice claims, not complete unit coverage.

## Current course placement guardrails
- Chapter 13 — A Need for Safety
- Chapter 14 — The Nature of Speed

## Direct exam-preparation demands
- Use speed × time to calculate reaction distance.
- Keep reaction distance separate from supplied braking distance.
- Calculate total stopping distance and margin to a stationary obstacle.
- Interpret slope and horizontal segments on a distance–time graph.
- Use evidence rather than reflex performance or real-driving claims.

## Canonical workspace
**Road Scenario / Prediction & Replay / Graph & Calculation**

## Mastery evidence
- scientifically valid result;
- relevant model/evidence use;
- explanation or calculation appropriate to the task;
- independent fresh transfer with the worked solution hidden.

## Boundaries
- No speed scoring or repeated-guess mastery.
- No personal/student data.
- No scientific labels or values baked into context art.
- Open reasoning is not certified through keyword matching.
- All authored numbers are illustrative teaching data unless the source record says otherwise.

## Model assumptions learners must see
Stationary obstacle; reaction speed constant; supplied braking distances use a consistent constant-deceleration model of 5 m/s²; not real-road certification.
