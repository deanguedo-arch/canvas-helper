The Crucible – Act One
Arthur Miller

Choose Your Own Path
Select four of the following items to answer completely.

1. What is wrong with Betty? Why does Parris call for Hale? Is Parris simply a worried
parent, or does he have other concerns? Support your answer with examples.

2. What clues suggest that Abigail’s reputation has become questionable?

3. Thomas and  Ann Putnam  seem  to  have  reasons  to  be  bitter  about  the  course  their
lives have taken in Salem. Discuss.

4. Frequently in serious literature one character assumes the role of the voice of reason.
In Act One, the voice of reason is Rebecca Nurse. Examine the first Act for ways in
which Rebecca attempts to apply reason to the situation.

5. Describe  the  character  of  John  Proctor.  Is  he  independent  or  a  follower?  Honest  or
hypocritical?  How  does  he  feel  about  himself,  and  why  might  he  feel  that  way?
Explain with examples.

6. Who is Elizabeth Proctor? What does Abigail think of her? How might this affect the
outcome of the play?

7. What  connotations  might  there  be  in Giles’  questions  to  Reverend  Hale  about  the
meaning  of  his  wife’s  reading  habits?  Are  these  intentional,  or  are  Giles’  questions
innocent? Use quotes from the text to support your answers.
