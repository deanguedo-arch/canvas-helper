## Official curriculum source

Alberta Education. **Science 14 and 24 Program of Studies**. Science 24 Unit A: *Applications of Matter and Chemical Change*, printed/PDF pp. 31–32.  
URL: `https://education.alberta.ca/media/159714/sc1424.pdf`

Checked 6 October 2026.

Relevant official curriculum text includes Unit A key concepts such as evidence of chemical change; combustion and rusting; acid–base reactions; simple composition/decomposition; and chemical reactions in everyday/workplace settings. General Outcome 2 requires students to investigate and classify chemical reactions, with specific outcomes involving evidence of chemical change, reactant/product changes, energy changes, and identifying selected reaction types.

## Scenario provenance

The six cases in this package are authored teaching scenarios, not real measurements, lab procedures or released Alberta exam questions.

Generated images are original concept assets produced for this project handoff. They are contextual art only and are not authoritative scientific records.

## Important limitation

This handoff aligns A1 to relevant Unit A demands, but it does not claim full Unit A coverage or guaranteed exam performance. It is a targeted practice component that still requires teacher/science review and runtime QA before course release.
