AUTHORITATIVE CONTENT RULE:
The PNG mockups are visual references only. Do NOT copy chemistry wording, formulas, values, evidence or answers from pixels. Use SCENARIO_BANK.json and the content documents as the source of truth. In particular, do not define rust as a single exact Fe2O3 formula just because a generated mockup may show that simplification.

IMPLEMENTATION:
- Produce a polished self-contained static web game using semantic HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript.
- No framework, CDN, account, analytics, database, network service or cloud save.
- Keep state in memory for v1.
- Use local SVG icons and the supplied local context art.
- Preserve the approved charcoal + forest green + white Next Step / Science 24 visual identity.
- Desktop should use the Case / Evidence / Reasoning hierarchy; narrow layouts stack in that order.
- Every control must work by keyboard and touch.
- No drag-only mechanic.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

GAME FLOW:
brief → worked example → four practice cases → feedback/retry → independent transfer → review.

PRACTICE MECHANIC:
- base observations visible;
- initial prediction before extra evidence where specified;
- 2 investigation tokens; learner may reveal at most 2 of 4 optional evidence records;
- choose up to 2 strongest pieces of evidence;
- choose conclusion;
- choose reaction type when applicable;
- enter an explanation;
- submit;
- targeted feedback and one-at-a-time hint ladder;
- unlimited retry without score penalty.

OPEN-RESPONSE RULE:
Do not auto-grade explanation quality with keyword matching. Structured choices may be deterministic; the explanation is required and then shown against the visible Claim/Evidence/Reasoning/Limits rubric. Record it as submitted, not automatically correct.

TRANSFER:
T01 Boiling Water has no hints before first submission and no investigation-token mechanic. It must feel closer to a conventional exam question than the earlier cases.

DO NOT ADD:
coins, XP, leaderboard, timer, streak pressure, avatars, unrelated mini-games, unsafe lab directions, real student data, medical advice or real-furnace instructions.

Run every item possible in 06_QA/QA_TEST_PLAN.md. Test correct, misconception, incomplete and valid-alternative responses.
