and snow on the outskirts of Edmonton in January.  A forensic entomologist

    2.     a.   Use your knowledge of insect maturation or development to explain that

  b.   What does the observation of numerous pupal membranes in the chest
region suggest about the cause of death of the victim described above?
Va l u e

2
Any maggots or pupae that have fed upon the body can be tested
for poisoning.
It has been observed that during the autumn and winter in the
Edmonton area, blowfl y eggs do not mature into larvae or pupae.
Because pupal membranes were found in the chest region of the
victim, signifi cant blowfl y development had occurred.  Therefore,
this had to have happened while the body was lying there in the
summer or spring.  (more likely the summer or decomposition
would have progressed even further).
Numerous pupal membranes in the chest region suggest that
the victim likely suffered from some trauma to the chest (such
as knife or bullet wound) because fl owfl ies lay their eggs in open
wounds more so than in body openings.
