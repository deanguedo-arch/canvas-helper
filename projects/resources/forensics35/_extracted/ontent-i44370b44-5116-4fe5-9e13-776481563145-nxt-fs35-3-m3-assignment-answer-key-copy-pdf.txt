Lesson C.  Explosives
9.Describe a fuse, and explain why some explosivedevices require one. (2 marks)
A fuse is a length of cord either filled with combustible material or made from
combustible material. (1 mark)
Some devices require a fuse to produce heat to cause a reaction between the
compounds in the metal casing resulting in an explosion. (1 mark)
10. Identify and describe a major disadvantage of using both the X-ray machine and the metal
detector to detect explosives. (2 marks)
Both methods require visual confirmation by a person if a positive reading is made. (1 mark)
Operators of both these devices rely heavily on visual analysis of objects. Thus, explosives
that are cleverly hidden may be missed. (1 mark)
11. Could the EGIS system replace the use of X-ray machines or metal detectors in airports?
Explain. (3 marks)
No. The EGIS system takes a long period to confirm its findings. This would disrupt airport
scheduling.

Lesson D. Case Study Involving Arson and Explosives
13. Describe how Raymond Lee Oyler was able to set the wildfires without being seriously
burned. (3 marks)
• Oyler used a device that was time-delayed. (1 mark)
• It consisted of five to seven paper or wood matches attached around a Marlboro cigarette
with duct tape or a rubber band. When the cigarette was lit, it burned slowly until it reached
the matches, ignited them, and started a brush fire in about 10 minutes. (2 marks)
14. What have some suggested could have prevented the death of the five firefighters in the
Esperanza Fire? (2 marks)
Firefighters should not be sent to protect vacant houses or isolated individual homes. (2
marks)
15. Was the bomb used to destroy the Alfred P. Murrah Federal Building a high explosive or a
low explosive, or did it have traits of both?  Explain your choice. (3 marks)
• The bomb exhibited traits of both low and high explosives. (1 mark)
• It had traits of a low explosive because it used some easy-to-find  materials, especially
ammonium nitrate (fertilizer) and nitromethane (fuel). (1 mark)
• It had traits of a high explosive because it was a large bomb, produced powerful shock
waves, and used compounds used in mining, especially Tovex and ANFO. (1 mark)
