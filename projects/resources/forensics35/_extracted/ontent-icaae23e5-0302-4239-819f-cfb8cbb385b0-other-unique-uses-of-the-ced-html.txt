Other Unique Uses of the CED
XREP is a wireless projectile that can be fired from a standard 12-gauge shotgun. Each XREP weighs only 2.4 grams, contains a battery, and has a launch velocity of over 90 metres per second.
The XREP projectile causes temporary involuntary muscle incapacitation that is identical to the effect of a handheld CED. However, because it is wireless, it can be used at distances of up to 30 metres. When the XREP hits a suspect, it incapacitates him or her for 20 seconds allowing officers to take the offender into custody. If a suspect tries to remove the XREP projectile, 'reflex engagement electrodes' in the device release numerous electrical pulses that cause pain and involuntary muscle incapacitation.
The XREP was field tested in 2007 and was made available to police departments in 2008.
TRAD modules are stand-alone devices designed to observe, warn, incapacitate, and retain intruders that have entered a secure facility or area (such as military checkpoints, military command centers, aircraft facilities, embassies, utility facilities, or pipelines). A TRAD module is operated remotely. When it detects an intruder, it fires several Taser cartridges that incapacitate the intruder. If the intruder attempts to flee, the Taser cartridges can be made to release an electrical current again.
