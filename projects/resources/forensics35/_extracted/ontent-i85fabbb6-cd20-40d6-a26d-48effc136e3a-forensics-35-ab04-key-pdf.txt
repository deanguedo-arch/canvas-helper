    3.     Using your knowledge of velocity, kinetic energy, and trajectory, explain how
a handgun round becomes less effective than a rifl e bullet over long distances.
    4.     Explain why a police tactical unit always takes at least one assault rifl e or
sniper rifl e when responding to a call involving a suspect armed with a gun.
3
2
A rifl e bullet will leave the rifl e with greater kinetic energy and velocity because a larger
amount of gunpowder is contained in the cartridge.  A handgun round typically suffers
from a signifi cant reduction in velocity and kinetic energy as it travels beyond a certain
distance.  This means a drop in trajectory.  A bullet fi red from a large calibre rifl e retains
a large amount of velocity and kinetic energy even after it has travelled one or two
kilometres.  This enables the bullet to have a fl atter trajectory.  In contrast, the kinetic
energy of a handgun round is expended much more quickly.  Consequently, this round will
lose height and fall to the ground much sooner.
A high-powered rifl e is able to fi re a bullet over long distances with a highdegree of
accuracy and kinetic energy.  This provides offi cers with an opportunity to effectively
neutralize a target with reduced risk to the public as well as other offi cers.  A scope
mounted on the gun allows for more accurate aiming.
