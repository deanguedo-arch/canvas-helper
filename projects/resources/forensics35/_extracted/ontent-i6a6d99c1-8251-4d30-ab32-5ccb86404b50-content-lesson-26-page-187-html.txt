In the
1880s, Alfred Nobel made his fortune by developing dynamite and a smokeless gunpowder called ballistite. When Nobel died in the late 1800s, he left the majority of his estate to establish the Nobel Prizes to be awarded annually to individuals who helped to better mankind. Apparentlly Nobel did this after reading an article in a French newspaper that condemned him for inventing dynamite and gunpowder by stating that he "…became rich by finding ways to kill more people faster than ever before."
