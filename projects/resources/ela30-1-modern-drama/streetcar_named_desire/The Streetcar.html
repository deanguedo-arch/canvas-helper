The Streetcar

This is the picture of an actual streetcar named Desire. The streetcar was in fact used during the period the play was published (1947) . This is the streetcar that Blance used to get to the Elysian Fields. In greek mythology, Elysian Fields is a synonym for paradise. Classical Mythology. the abode of the blessed after death. any similarly conceived abode or state of the dead. any place or state of perfect happiness; paradise.

But on her way there she stopped at Cemeteries.. We wonder if this is a potential clue about the character’s ending.

The stops mentioned in the play such as Belle Reve ( page 9 ) and Elysian Fields ( page 6 ) are also real.
All These details enhance the illusion of the reality.

The streetcar itself is still being used today, in San Francisco.
