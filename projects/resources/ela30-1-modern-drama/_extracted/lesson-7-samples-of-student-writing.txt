Samples of Student Writing Before you begin to write your first Critical/Analytical Response to Literary Texts Assignment, you may find it helpful to review some samples of student's writing. Take some time to look through the samples provided below in order to gain an idea of the expectations. In particular, carefully review the markers' rationale for each paper and consider what makes the difference between a mark of S, PF, E etc. **These are fairly lengthy documents, and they contain samples of both the Personal Response to Texts and the Critical/Analytical Response to Literary Texts. For now, review the samples of the CARs and compare the S paper to the PF paper, and so on. Student writing samples from English Language Arts 30-1 provided by Alberta Education
