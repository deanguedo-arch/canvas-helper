Modern Drama Introduction

In this unit, you will have the opportunity to:

  • appreciate the artistry of a classic play by Tennessee Williams

  • practice developing strong and thoughtful thesis statements

  • get inside the head of one of the main characters and create an original dramatic monologue

  • improve thoughtfulness, effectiveness and correctness of communication through your first Critical/Analytical Response to Texts essay

  • review some Literary Techniques in Drama as you work your way through the play
