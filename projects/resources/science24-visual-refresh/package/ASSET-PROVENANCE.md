## Rules these assets already satisfy
- No AI-generated image depicts a source observation or disguises a correction (all GEN images are
  clean conceptual comparisons, clearly illustrative).
- No complete textbook scan is used as an inline illustration (textbook figures are cropped by Codex
  from the per-page renders listed in CHANGE-PLAN.md).
- The one real-phenomenon image (water treatment plant) is a real photograph, not AI art.
- Captions (one-to-two sentence takeaway) are written by Codex per the lesson — see CHANGE-PLAN.md
  for each figure's teaching job.
