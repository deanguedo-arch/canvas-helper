## Structured ledger transactions (Rev 28 — live money + lifecycle semantics)
**Every monetary transaction is a structured ledger posting.** In `product-records-v7.24.json` each transaction
carries `postings` (an explicit list of {account, debit|credit, amount-expression} legs — no prose postings
survive), `variables` (every amount expression's inputs), a named external `counterparty` (employer, bank,
casino house, tax authority, vendor, park world — never an anonymous sink), `stateChanges`, `invokes`,
`settles`, `originates`, and a `narrative`. **Every monetary transaction sums to zero over every branch** —
`rev28-integrity.py` evaluates the branch equations numerically, including the hostile cases.
**The lifecycle is the money authority's other half:** `transactions.lifecycleResolvers` names 26 promised
transitions — reserve, abort, accrue, resolve, convert, repay, terminate, repair — each with exactly one registered
executable resolver and named predecessor/successor states; `rev28-integrity.py` runs the lifecycle
reachability audit (no transition may be prose-only) and checks each edge's required instance keys against the
resolver's declared `instanceKeys`.
- **Client sessions (`txn.sessionReserve` → `txn.sessionSettlement` / `txn.sessionAbort`):** ONE canonical transaction owns actual
  receipts, actual COGS, the reserve release, and the automatic 10%-of-receipts advance repayment —
  repay = min(advanceLiability, round2(0.10 x receipts)); ordinary auto-resolved Premium sessions post here
  too. `txn.businessReceipts` and its master-tier auto-resolve hook are RETIRED. A Lawn Safe flop ($10
  receipts, $30 COGS) posts −$20, never +$10. The auto-resolve reserve is the SAME max-COGS gate for played
  and automated sessions — no separate 0.85 x average formula exists. **Quitting before settlement is safe:**
  `txn.sessionAbort` reverses the funded max-COGS reserve (reserveHold → businessCash) exactly once per
  sessionId (abortOnce); settlement and abort are mutually exclusive per session — a session either settles
  or aborts, never both, and the reserve is never stranded. The guard is executable, not prose: every
  posting and state change in `txn.sessionSettlement` carries the when-guard `settleOnce == 1`, where
  `settleOnce` is 1 while the reserve is still held and no abort has committed, else 0 — an abort-then-settle
  attempt posts nothing.
- **Big Job (`txn.bigJobReserve` → `txn.bigJobSettlement` / `txn.bigJobAbort`):** the $72 working-capital reserve moves from
  businessCash to reserveHold BEFORE the session commits (`actions.bigJob.prerequisiteTransactionId`); the
  dedicated settlement posts $12 receipts per correct request and $6 rework COGS per incorrect/missed request
  from that reserve (unspent reserve releases), repays min(outstandingAdvance, 10% of receipts) toward the
  restart advance ($144 receipts with $200 outstanding repays $14.40), and routes disputed receivables to

  job posts $48 − $48 = $0. `txn.bigJobSettlement` carries the same executable guard: every posting
  and state change is gated on `settleOnce == 1` (1 while the $72 reserve is held and no big-job abort has
  committed, else 0). The $72 reserve posts only when `businessCash >= 72` — the floor is an executable
  `affordable` guard, not prose.
- **Mortgage origination (`txn.housePurchaseSettlement`):** mints ONE immutable mortgage contractId per purchase,

## UNIVERSAL INVARIANTS (apply to every system)
- **Time model: day-tick life sim inside a month-tick economy.** Each month = 30 days. Each day has two blocks: **DAY** and **EVENING**. There are no within-block clock times anywhere in the design — no 09:00, no 12:00, no 22:00. Availability is per-block: a venue is open for DAY, EVENING, both, or neither, shown as a block badge on the door. Sleeping (evening, at home) advances one day: energy/stress update per the rules below, weather/NPC/chaos state update, fridge consumption tick. Sleeping on day 30 fires **month-end processing at home** following the immutable `monthClose.settlementOrder` in the records: **wages credited FIRST** (the month's wage RECEIVABLE — posted at each shift completion, not cash — transferred to personal cash exactly once, zeroes the receivable; pay stub issued then), THEN tax arrears collection (oldest first — repaid first from the month's income), THEN the single current-period taxation debit, THEN bills, rent, contract auto-payments, insurance premiums (step 6: obligations posted at the tick are auto-deducted here), dividends; then the mandatory planned-vs-actual budget review, grocery adequacy check, and shop resets. **Pay stubs stay monthly** (curriculum requirement). Shop hours, day/night lighting, and weather run on the day clock. **Rent and occupancy charges accrue daily** (monthly rent ÷ 30 per day, days 1–29 rounded half-up with a **day-30 true-up** so a full rental month totals exactly the monthly rent — `housing.rentAccrual` in the records); a mid-month move-out or home purchase uses the **episode true-up** (rentDue = round2(monthlyRent × occupiedDays ÷ 30), the final rented day posts the difference), so the balance owed never depends on when within the month a purchase happens; **a home purchase never erases already-incurred housing charges.** From day 28, home shows a **month-end preview**: guaranteed obligations vs cash-on-hand vs pending — pending is labeled as pending and NEVER summed into one reassuring number. Mandatory month-end steps (budget review) happen AT HOME during month-end processing — never at a shop — so availability can never block required actions. Contract payments stay automatic. **Month close is atomic:** each close gets a unique period ID (`YYYY-MM`) and an immutable input snapshot (earned wages, ownership, obligations, consumption, RNG positions). The full result is calculated once; the financial/world state and the pending-review checkpoint commit together, then the review displays. No mutable action (trading, shopping, playing) is allowed inside a close. A reload resumes the committed result — it never reruns the calculation. **Terminal boundary:** the month-24 close runs BEFORE month 25 opens. Only completed-period earnings and obligations settle (including maturities due within the horizon); no future-period renewal charges or benefits are granted for an unplayable month. Forward commitments appear in an open-obligations schedule (arrears shown as part of carrying balances, never additional).

Any active labour income resets the 3-day recovery streak. Passive income — interest, dividends, already-earned receivables — continues while burned out. The ONLY exit is the combined rule: **recoveryStreak ≥ 3 AND stress < 80 after sleep** (the executable state machine in `actions.laborGate.recovery` in the records: a QUALIFYING day is a rest day with NO labor action executed AND ≥ 2,000 calories consumed that day — a day under 2,000 calories is non-qualifying and resets the streak; streak increment at the sleep boundary, reset to 0 on any non-qualifying day BEFORE the clear check, clear iff recoveryStreak ≥ 3 AND stress < 80 after the sleep stress reduction).

A paid enrollment is never free. Once a month, a **statement-audit moment at the home computer** — **the full sorting game triggers ONLY on a new charge, a price change, a trial conversion, or a suspicious item; a stable month gets one-click "reviewed — no changes"** (still an explicit confirm — the CALM habit without the solved-chore grind). - **StreamFlix $12/mo:** +3 happiness/month; unlocks "movie night" (a free EVENING happiness option, +2 happiness per viewing, at most 2 viewings per week count toward happiness).
- **MealKit $40/mo:** 15,000 cal/month auto-added to the fridge — **NOT sold as cheaper per calorie** (FreshMart rice & beans at $3/9,000 cal win on price, and the game says so): the tradeoff is convenience — **an automatic-stocking service (pre-portioned ingredients, recipes, no shopping trip) presented as an interface convenience**, with explicit copy: "MealKit saves no money per calorie. It saves the hassle." No claim of saved hours or action tokens — time is not a resource the simulation tracks, and MealKit grants no extra actions.
- **FitApp Premium $8/mo:** +1 wellness per **qualifying** workout (meets that workout's qualifying floor — e.g. Beat Rep ≥40% hits); an all-miss workout earns no bonus. Unlocks workout history.
- **CloudSave $3/mo:** keeps >20 emails and backs up computer files (flavor + inbox capacity).
- **Entitlement identity (anti-duplicate rules):** every active subscription is identified by a stable **entitlement ID = productId + billing-period ID**. **Cancellation never erases delivered charges** — a cancelled period keeps its charges and keeps the benefit for the rest of that paid period, then lapses. **Restarting the same product in the SAME billing period reinstates the existing entitlement (no double charge, no duplicate benefits)**; restarting in a new period opens a new entitlement ID. Trial conversion and all subsequent charges reference the entitlement ID, so "cancel-and-resubscribe" can never double-bill or double-grant.

Canceling loses the benefit — the choice is real. **Canceling CloudSave (defined lifecycle):** the account drops to the free tier — the 20 most recent emails stay active; older ones become **archived read-only** (viewable, cannot reply). The behavioral ledger is always local; CloudSave is a backup, never the only copy.
