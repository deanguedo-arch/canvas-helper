## 1. What was released (verified)

On **September 22, 2026**, Alberta Education and Childcare posted draft curricula for **grades 7–9 science**, **Career and Life Management 10 (CALM 10)**, and **Physical Education and Wellness 10 (PEW 10)** to new LearnAlberta for **optional classroom field testing in the 2026–27 school year**. Announced by Minister Demetrios Nicolaides at a news conference in Calgary.

**Rollout timeline (official):**
- 2026–27: optional field testing (feedback stage; teachers apply to join focus groups)
- 2027–28: optional piloting
- **September 2028: mandatory implementation**
- $41 million through Budget 2026 to support implementation in 2026–27
- Drafts developed with input from 7 education partners, 1 First Nation education partner, 2 subject-area specialists, 35 stakeholder organizations, 93 teachers

**Official description of the renewed CALM 10** (government news release, Sept 22, 2026):
> "The renewed CALM 10 curriculum will equip students with knowledge and skills to **explore career opportunities and manage money**. A **stronger focus on career planning, entrepreneurship and work-related learning** will help students move successfully into rewarding careers they actually want to work in. Students will also develop their financial skills by learning about areas like **saving, responsible spending, budgeting, banking and debt management**."

**Structural facts (verified):**
- CALM 10 will **replace CALM 20**, which was last updated in **2002**.
- **Sexual health education moves OUT of CALM** into the new Grade 10 physical education and wellness course (PEW 10).
- Both CALM 10 and PEW 10 remain **mandatory half-year (3-credit)** courses.
- A "Draft Grade 10 curriculum: career and life management (CALM 10) fact sheet" was published alongside the release (URL not yet indexed publicly as of Sept 28, 2026).
- Subject code on new LearnAlberta: **CLM** (confirmed via the official "Senior High School Career and Life Management at a Glance" document). CALM 10 sits under the new core subject area **"Career Education and Financial Literacy"** on the Alberta's Curriculum landing page.

## 2. Draft architecture (new-curriculum format)

Alberta's new curriculum architecture organizes each subject as:

1. **Organizing Ideas** — the top-level strands (the "big ideas" of the subject)
2. **Guiding Questions** — under each organizing idea
3. **Learning Outcomes** — under each guiding question, with supporting Knowledge / Understandings / Skills & Procedures

> ⚠️ The exact organizing-idea titles, guiding questions, and learning-outcome statements for draft CALM 10 have **not yet been extracted** — the program-of-study pages on new LearnAlberta are client-rendered and were not readable with text-extraction tools, and the draft text is not indexed by search engines yet (5 days post-release). ### What the emphases imply for strands (inference, NOT yet verified against the draft text)

Based strictly on the official release language, expect strands covering roughly:
- **Career exploration & planning** — career opportunities, pathways, planning for post-secondary/work transitions
- **Entrepreneurship** — named explicitly as a new/stronger focus
- **Work-related / work-integrated learning** — named explicitly
- **Financial literacy** — saving, responsible spending, budgeting, banking, debt management (all named explicitly)

## 3. Old CALM 20 (2002) — structure for comparison

The 2002 Career and Life Management program of studies (being replaced) is organized into **three General Outcomes**, each with numbered Specific Outcomes:

- **General Outcome 1 — Personal Choices** (P-outcomes, e.g. P13): well-being across emotional, intellectual, social, spiritual and physical dimensions; relationships; **healthy sexuality and responsible sexual behaviour** (e.g., "explain the ongoing responsibility for being sexually healthy"; "describe sexually healthy actions and choices for one's body, including abstinence"; "assess the consequences of being sexually active"); media/peer-pressure/alcohol-and-drug influences on wellness.
- **General Outcome 2 — Resource Choices** (R-outcomes): *"Students will make responsible decisions in the use of finances and other resources that reflect personal values and goals and demonstrate commitment to self and others."* Includes: identifying personal resources (time, energy, knowledge, skills, technology, **finances**); *"examine the fundamentals of getting and using money — basic information on getting an income, deductions, paying taxes, using money for various expenses"*; *"prepare and use a personal budget"*.
- **General Outcome 3 — Career Choices** (C-outcomes): career planning and life-role planning for life after senior high school.

(Quoted outcome statements above are verbatim from the 2002 program of studies.)

## 4. Financial-literacy & career-emphasis differences: old CALM 20 → draft CALM 10

| Area | Old CALM 20 (2002) | Draft CALM 10 (2026) — per official release |
|---|---|---|
| **Financial literacy** | One strand of three (Resource Choices). Fundamentals only: getting income, deductions, paying taxes, expenses, preparing a personal budget. | Explicitly **expanded and named**: saving, responsible spending, budgeting, banking, **debt management**. Framed as a headline purpose of the course ("explore career opportunities **and manage money**"). |
| **Career emphasis** | One strand of three (Career Choices): career/life-role planning. | **Stronger focus**: career planning + **entrepreneurship** (new explicit focus) + **work-related / work-integrated learning** (new explicit focus). Career content is the lead message of the renewal, ahead of finance. |
| **Sexual health** | Inside CALM (Personal Choices strand). | **Removed from CALM** — moves to the new PEW 10 course. |
| **Wellness (mental health, nutrition, substance abuse)** | Inside CALM (Personal Choices strand). | Shifts toward PEW 10 (minister: "mental health, nutrition and knowledge of substance abuse are interconnected with physical well-being"). |
| **Course structure** | General Outcomes → Specific Outcomes (P/R/C). | New architecture: Organizing Ideas → Guiding Questions → Learning Outcomes (exact titles TBD — see below). |
| **Grade placement/name** | CALM 20 (typically Grade 11). | CALM 10 (Grade 10), mandatory half-year, replacing CALM 20. |

**Game-design takeaway:** the draft doubles down on exactly the two pillars Paycheck Panic already simulates — **career progression** (jobs, entrepreneurship, work-integrated learning) and **money management** (budgeting, saving, banking, debt). Sexual-health/wellness content leaving CALM means the game no longer needs to cover it for CALM alignment; wellness mechanics (stress/energy) still fit the "life management" frame but are no longer core curriculum.
