## Written review

**1.** Intrinsic motivation concerns enjoyment or interest in the activity itself. Integrated regulation concerns a purpose that fits broader values and identity. It remains extrinsic. The same person can have both reasons.

**2.** Use the stated barrier. A clear demonstration can support competence; a respectful partner can support relatedness. Explain what to ask or observe after the change rather than promise immediate motivation.

**3.** Effort does not remove constraints, unclear instructions or recovery needs. Describe the changed demand, identify support and adapt the action. A realistic revision is part of planning, not proof of weak character.

**4.** Name the earlier response and the exact change. For example, separate an outcome goal from a process action or replace a global judgment with an observation. Explain why the revision improves the reasoning rather than copy an entire new plan.
