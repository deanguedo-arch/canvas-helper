Fences

Act One, Scene Two
Synopsis
Conflicts begin to build as Troy grumbles about the lottery, lottery winners, Cory’s continued
involvement in football, and his seeming inability to find the time to build a fence around the
yard. Complications in Troy’s character are embodied in the arrival of Gabriel, Troy’s delusional

brother who was injured in World War I.

Vocabulary and References

Archangel Gabriel - angel responsible for sounding the trumpet that will open the
gate of Heaven

Numbers - slang for lottery numbers

St. Peter — biblical keeper of Heaven's gate and the Book of Life
