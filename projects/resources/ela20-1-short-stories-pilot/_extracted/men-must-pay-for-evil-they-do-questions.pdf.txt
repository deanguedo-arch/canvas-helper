Men Must Pay for Evil They Do
Tom Barrett

First Impressions
1. What can you tell about Tom Barrett’s views on capital punishment from the title of
his article?

Responding Critically
2. Identify the main points Barrett presents for reinstituting capital punishment.

3. Quote one sentence in which Barrett uses highly emotional words. What is his
purpose for using those words?

Responding Creatively
4. Assume this article has appeared in your local paper. Write a letter to the editor
supporting or condemning Barrett’s views.
