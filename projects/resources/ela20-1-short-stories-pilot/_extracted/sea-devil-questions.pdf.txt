The Sea Devil
Arthur Gordon

Answer all questions with complete sentences.

Responding Personally
1. If this story were made into a movie which scene do you think would be most
exciting? What would you have the camera focus on? Explain.

Responding Critically
2. Why does the man like to fish at night? How does his love for fishing at night lead to
his struggle with the ray? What foreshadowing is there to indicate the man will catch
the ray?

3. List the ways the man attempts to free himself from the ray. What characteristic
enables the man to survive?

4. What does the man learn from his experience with the ray?

5. What does the story suggest about the relationship between human beings and nature?
Which is the superior force? Discuss with references to the plane and the causeway.

Responding Creatively
6. As a local news reporter, you are assigned to interview the man about his brush with
death. What questions would you ask him? What answers would you expect to hear?
Script the conversation using the distributed format sheet.
