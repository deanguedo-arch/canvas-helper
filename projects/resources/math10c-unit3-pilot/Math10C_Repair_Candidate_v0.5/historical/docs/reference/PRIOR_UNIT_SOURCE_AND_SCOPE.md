## What the larger unit does and does not prove

The bounded unit checker extends to multiple single-letter variables, exact integer polynomial arithmetic and powers through six. That is a **new unit-specific contract**, needed for variable GCF, non-monic factoring and polynomial products; it is not an unnoticed change to the original monic-only fixture file. Roots are taught and numerically answered; the tool does not parse arbitrary radical notation, fractions, equations, logarithms or intervals.

The trigonometry contrast from the old cross-domain engine pilot is not part of this coherent Chapter 3 unit. M11–M13 remain future trigonometry obligations. No Math 20/30 courses, universal symbolic tutor, handwritten-work grader or formal exam bank is represented as included.
