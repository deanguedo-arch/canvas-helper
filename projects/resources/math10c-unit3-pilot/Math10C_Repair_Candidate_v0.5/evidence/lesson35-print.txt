Factor x² + bx + c

WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE

Use both conditions—not just one
For a trinomial whose leading coefficient is 1, expansion gives a useful pattern.
(x + p)(x + q) = x2 + (p + q)x + pq
To factor x² + bx + c, look for integers p and q that multiply to c and add to b. “Monic” means
the leading coefficient is 1. This sum/product rule depends on that leading 1; a different
leading coefficient needs the method in 3.6.
Write the pair you are trying, then check both conditions. You are not guessing a full answer
blindly—you are narrowing a small list of factor pairs.

x2 + 11x + 24 = (x + 3)(x + 8)
The middle products are 8x and 3x; they combine to 11x.
