# Insulin and Glucagon Feedback — Answer Key

## High blood glucose / insulin pathway
- A — High blood glucose
- B — Pancreas
- C — Beta cells (islets of Langerhans)
- D — Insulin
- E — Muscle cells
- F — Adipose tissue (fat cells)
- G — Liver

## Low blood glucose / glucagon pathway
- H — Low blood glucose
- I — Pancreas
- J — Alpha cells (islets of Langerhans)
- K — Glucagon
- L — Liver

## Shared outcome
- M — Normal blood glucose level / homeostasis
