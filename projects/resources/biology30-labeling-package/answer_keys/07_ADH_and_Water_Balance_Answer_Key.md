# ADH and Water Balance — Answer Key

## Lettered labels
- A — Increased blood solute concentration / dehydration (high blood osmolarity)
- B — Osmoreceptors in the hypothalamus
- C — Hypothalamus
- D — Posterior pituitary
- E — ADH (antidiuretic hormone)
- F — Kidney
- G — Collecting duct / nephron tubule
- H — Increased water reabsorption
- I — Small volume of concentrated urine
- J — Homeostasis restored / blood water balance restored

## Suggested sequence for the unlabeled top blank boxes
1. Osmoreceptors detect increased osmolarity.
2. Posterior pituitary releases ADH.
3. ADH acts on the kidney tubules/collecting ducts.
4. More water is reabsorbed back into the blood.
