# Hypothalamus and Pituitary Pathways — Answer Key

## Main structures
- A — Hypothalamus
- B — Releasing hormones (hypothalamic releasing hormones)
- C — Hypophyseal portal system
- D — Anterior pituitary
- E — Posterior pituitary
- F — Neurosecretory cells / hypothalamic neurons

## Anterior pituitary hormones
- G — TSH (thyroid-stimulating hormone)
- H — ACTH (adrenocorticotropic hormone)
- I — GH / hGH (growth hormone)
- J — Prolactin (PRL)
- K — FSH (follicle-stimulating hormone)
- L — LH (luteinizing hormone)

## Posterior pituitary hormones
- M — ADH (antidiuretic hormone)
- N — Oxytocin

## Target organs / tissues
- O — Thyroid gland
- P — Adrenal gland / adrenal cortex
- Q — Bones and muscles
- R — Mammary glands
- S — Ovaries and testes
- T — Ovaries and testes
- U — Kidneys
- V — Uterus
- W — Mammary glands
