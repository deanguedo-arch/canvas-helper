Colour & Emotion Painting

For your studio project you will create one painting that is approximately 6"x6" communicates a chosen emotion through colour. The subject matter can be simple or even abstract. The most important goal is that the colour choices clearly express a mood or emotional atmosphere.

Your painting must use a limited palette of two to four colours. These colours should repeat throughout the artwork so that the emotional tone remains consistent.

Avoid outlining everything with heavy black lines. Instead, allow colour relationships to create structure and emphasis.

Focus primarily on the emotional effect of colour rather than on detailed realism.
