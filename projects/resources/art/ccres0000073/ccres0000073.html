Sketchbook Rubric

This rubric is used for ongoing sketchbook checks and will be completed after each lesson submission. It focuses on thinking, exploration, and reflection, not finished artwork.

You will be required to complete a self check for each of your submissions as well as you teacher will submit a mark for your submission. 

Level 5 — Exceptional

  • Sketchbook shows extensive exploration and risk-taking
  • Multiple ideas tried and revised
  • Strong use of symbols, notes, and visual thinking
  • Clear connections to artists, themes, or personal ideas
  • Reflections are insightful and honest

This sketchbook shows how an independent artist thinks.

Level 4 — Strong

  • Consistent and thoughtful sketchbook use
  • More than one idea explored for most tasks
  • Evidence of experimentation and decision-making
  • Reflections explain why choices were made

This sketchbook shows purposeful artistic thinking.

Level 3 — Acceptable (Art 30 Standard)

  • Required pages are complete
  • Ideas are explored, but often safely
  • Some experimentation is visible
  • Reflections describe process and learning

This sketchbook meets Art 30 expectations.

Level 2 — Developing

  • Sketchbook work is inconsistent or rushed
  • Few ideas explored
  • Limited experimentation or risk-taking
  • Reflections are brief, vague, or incomplete

This sketchbook shows partial engagement.

Level 1 — Beginning

  • Little sketchbook evidence
  • Pages are mostly empty or copied
  • No meaningful exploration
  • Reflections are missing

This sketchbook does not yet show artistic thinking.

Important Reminder

Your sketchbook is not graded on neatness or talent.

It is graded on:

  • Thinking
  • Trying
  • Reflecting
  • Growing

Messy pages often score higher than empty ones.
