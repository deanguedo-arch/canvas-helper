Completed Drawing

After completing the practice exercises, you will create a minimum size of 6x6 finished observational drawing of a simple still-life object, in your sketchbook.

Choose an object that has a clear shape and visible light and shadow. Begin by drawing the outline carefully and checking the proportions.

Once the outline is correct, begin adding shading to show the range of light and dark values. Try to include at least three to five different values so the object appears more three-dimensional.

Avoid outlining everything in heavy dark lines. Instead, allow the shading to describe the form of the object.

Focus more on observing the object than on drawing quickly. Artists often spend more time looking than drawing.
