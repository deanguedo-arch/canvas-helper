Final Project for Art 30

 

Create an In-Depth Study & Personal Style through a Personal Portfolio containing 6-8 finished works and a written critical defence.

 

Requirements:

In - Depth Study

Your portfolio cannot be random, unrelated images. At least 4 of your 6–8 images must form a cohesive series or "in-depth study" centered around a single theme, concept, or technique.

Dimensional Diversity

Your portfolio must include both two-dimensional (e.g., drawing, painting) and three-dimensional (e.g., sculpture, assemblage) works.

Encounters / Research

At least one work in your portfolio must be a direct visual response to a specific historical artist, movement, or period you have researched throughout this course.

Personal / Contemporary 

At least one work must address a personal experience or a significant issue in contemporary culture.

Critical Defense

A written statement where you analyze your portfolio pieces using the vocabulary and techniques of art criticism to analyze and evaluate your own works in relation to the works of professional artists.



Your choices 

Theme

You decide the subject matter. It can be a social issue, a fantasy concept, a formal study of light, a psychological exploration, etc.


Guideline: It must be substantial enough to sustain 4 separate works.

Media

While you must do some 2D and some 3D, the specific materials are up to you (e.g., acrylic vs. watercolor; clay vs. wire).

Professional Artist Comparison 

You choose which artist or movement you want to research for your "Encounters" requirement. It should be someone whose work resonates with your personal style. Can be from this course or one that you are interested in.  If you are choosing your own please let the teacher know before you start your project.
