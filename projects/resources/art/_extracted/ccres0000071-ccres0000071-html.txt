Sketchbook
Getting ready for your final project.  This is time for you to practice your critique technique.
1. Select Artwork for Critique
Choose one of your own artworks or studies.
Include:
A small sketch or photo reference
Notes on intent and process
2. Formal Critique Notes
Using art vocabulary, analyze:
Composition and focal point
Use of elements and principles
Technique and materials
Communication of meaning
Use bullet points or diagrams.
3. Professional Comparison
Choose one artist from this lesson.
Create notes comparing:
Visual strategies
Conceptual intent
Effectiveness of communication
Avoid judgement — focus on analysis.
4. Comparative Sketch or Diagram
Create a small sketch or diagram showing:
A strategy used by the artist
How you might adapt it in your work
Label clearly.
5. Written Reflection
Respond using critique vocabulary:
What is working well in your art?
What needs development?
How did the artist comparison help you?
What is your next step as an artist?
