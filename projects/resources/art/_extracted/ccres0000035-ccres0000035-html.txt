Sketchbook Tasks
Page 1 — Colour Mood Experiments
Begin by dividing one page of your sketchbook into four equal sections. In each section you will explore a different colour combination.
Fill each section with simple shapes, patterns, or brushstrokes using a specific group of colours. As you experiment, think about the emotional feeling each colour combination suggests.
Label each section with the emotion you believe the colours communicate. For example, one section might feel calm, while another might feel energetic or tense.
At the bottom of the page write one sentence explaining which colour combination felt most powerful and why.
Page 2 — Limited Palette Planning
Choose one emotion you would like your final painting to communicate. Write this emotion at the top of your sketchbook page.
Next choose two to four colours that you believe represent this emotion. On the page create small colour swatches showing how each colour appears in darker and lighter versions.
You may create lighter variations by adding white or water and darker variations by layering colour more heavily.
Below the swatches write a short explanation describing why you selected those particular colours.
Page 3 — Painting Plan
On half a page create a simple sketch of the composition for your final painting. The subject of the painting can be abstract shapes, a landscape, or a simple object.
Label the areas where the main colour will dominate and indicate where stronger contrast will appear. This planning step helps artists think about colour placement before beginning the final artwork.
Page 4 — Reflection
After completing the painting, write a short reflection describing how colour influenced the feeling of your artwork. Consider which colour choices worked well and whether any colour combinations surprised you.
Finally, reflect on what you might change if you created the painting again.
