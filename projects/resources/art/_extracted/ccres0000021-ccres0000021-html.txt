What Strong Sketchbook Work Looks Like
✔ Many lines, not one perfect outline✔ Evidence of trial and error
✔ Notes beside drawings✔ Willingness to abandon accuracy
✔ Clear experimentation
✘ One careful drawing only
✘ Erasing all marks
✘ Avoiding difficult tools
Common Mistakes (and How to Avoid Them)
Mistake: Trying to control every lineFix: Let the tool lead the mark
Mistake: Judging drawings too quicklyFix: Finish the page before deciding
Mistake: Avoiding expressive marksFix: Discomfort usually means growth
