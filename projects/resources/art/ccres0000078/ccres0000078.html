Take this time to work on on your final project.  Here are some steps to help you 

1. Portfolio Selection

List and/or sketch:

  • All major artworks created this course
  • Supporting studies or series

Choose 6–8 works that best represent:

  • Your ideas
  • Your skills
  • Your growth

Explain why each piece was selected.

 

2 — Portfolio Organization

Plan the order of your portfolio:

  • What comes first and why?
  • How do pieces relate to each other?
  • Where does your strongest work belong?

Use arrows, notes, or diagrams.

3. Artist Statement Drafting

Draft your artist statement addressing:

  • What themes or ideas drive your work
  • How materials, colour, and composition support meaning
  • How your work has evolved during Art 30

Aim for one well-developed paragraph (150–300 words).

4. Revision & Refinement

Revise your artist statement by:

  • Removing unnecessary detail
  • Strengthening art vocabulary
  • Clarifying intent

Read it aloud — clarity matters.

5 — Final Reflection

Respond using reflection language:

  • How has your artistic voice developed?
  • What risks did you take?
  • What are you most proud of?
  • What would you explore next if given more time?
