# Aboriginal Studies 30 — full-course teacher-voice review

Status: **ready for teacher sampling in the local preview; not packaged or released**

## What changed

- All 50 lesson routes now use the same warm, direct teaching presentation as the approved pilot.
- Directions, examples, practice prompts, and feedback name the speaker, document, or reading instead of asking students to remember labels such as “Source F.”
- Consecutive passages from the same reading are presented together. The current student-facing pass also removes page-number and provenance-style headings, removes repeated source-detail disclosures, and consolidates textbook links. Each passage keeps its exact quotation, creator/speaker, Nation when recorded, date, locator, attribution, and context record.
- Mechanical phrases such as “repair the misconception,” “fence the claim,” and numbered activity headings are presented in ordinary classroom language.
- Every lesson includes a clear reading purpose tied to its essential question. Existing task requirements remain unchanged.
- Practice & Review applies the same reading names to all 400 items while keeping the stored item IDs, content versions, answer keys, response keys, and option identities unchanged.

The three pilot lessons retain their bespoke rewrites and activities. The remaining 47 lessons use a shared, source-aware presentation layer over their existing full teaching content, so saved work and course records remain compatible.

For the latest reading-title evidence, use `../student-reading-pass-2026-09-24/REVIEW.md`.
