Status: **local teacher-review candidate; not approved, packaged or released**

## What was integrated

The supplied package contains ten individual generated illustrations and a placement guide. All ten were visually inspected. The excluded collage was not present and was not added.

The package numbers were mapped to the current course by title because the internal lesson IDs are not in display order:

| Package label | Current lesson | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L46 — Colonial wounds | Theme 4 Lesson 3: Child Removal in Australia and Canada | road hero; northern-shore supporting illustration |
| L47 — Resources and conflict | Theme 4 Lesson 4: Resource Development, Rights and Responsibility | lake/industry hero; recovering-wetland supporting illustration |
| L48 — The UN, pressure not law | Theme 4 Lesson 5: Indigenous Peoples and the United Nations | meeting-table hero; abstract-globe supporting illustration |
| L49 — Education and voice | Theme 4 Lesson 6: Education, Voice and Self-Determination | classroom hero; open-book supporting illustration |
| L50 — Youth and response | Theme 4 Lesson 7: Halfbreed: Choose, Support and Revise a Response | prairie-path hero; seedling supporting illustration |

Each image is labelled as an illustration in its alt text. The evidence-limit caption remains in canonical data and is available to assistive technology, but it is visually hidden so repeated production notes do not interrupt the learner page. The illustrations remain supplementary and must not be treated as historical photographs, maps, United Nations records, memoir locations or evidence about a named community.

The PNGs were converted to responsive WebP assets at quality 84. Header images are 1400 px wide; supporting images are 900 px wide. The ten course assets total about 1.2 MB instead of the package’s roughly 25 MB of PNGs.
