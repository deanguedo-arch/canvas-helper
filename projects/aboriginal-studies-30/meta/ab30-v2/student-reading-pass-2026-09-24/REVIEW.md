# AB30 full-course student reading-title review

Status: **local teacher-review candidate; not packaged or released**

## What changed

Lesson 17 was the example that exposed a course-wide problem. This pass reviewed the rendered reading headings in all four themes: 50 lessons and 275 reading groups.

- Generic `Reading: ...` labels are removed. Headings now state the idea, person, document or question students are reading about.
- Citation language such as `as reproduced in the textbook`, source types and page locators no longer appears in headings.
- Weak editorial shorthand was replaced with direct topic language. Examples include **Comprehensive and specific claims processes**, **The Nunavut Agreement and the creation of Nunavut**, **How stereotypes become discrimination**, and **Canada’s residential school system**.
- Named voices remain visible as **In their own words: ...**. Named documents use a short **Read: ...** heading.
- Repeated **About this reading** disclosures remain removed. Publication and page details stay in the compact byline and textbook link beneath the reading.
- Lesson 17 keeps its ten purpose-written headings; the same student-facing standard now applies across the other 49 lessons.

Canonical quotations, creator, speaker, Nation/community, date, locator, attribution, source type, activity identity, response key and saved work records remain intact.
