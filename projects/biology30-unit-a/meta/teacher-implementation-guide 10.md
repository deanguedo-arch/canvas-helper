## Delivery

The course is independent-first and asynchronous. Required learning totals 1,505 minutes. Optional extensions total 295 minutes and never block completion. Learners complete lesson exits, seven artifact checkpoints, and one submission of the 24-item formative final practice.

## Investigations

Lessons 7–10 use safe, low-material observations with complete supplied-data alternatives. Do not treat course activities as clinical tests. Do not require escalating audio, intense exercise, uncomfortable temperature exposure, or personal health disclosure.

## Feedback and collaboration

Enable a discussion for the sensory investigation design and hormone-technology brief when useful. Every checkpoint also includes a teacher or self-review route; peer availability must not block progress.

## Assessment boundary

Practice is non-graded and provides explanatory feedback. Artifact drafts are prepared for a companion Investigation Portfolio assignment. The secure Unit A test remains a separate native Brightspace activity.

## Completion and support

The SCORM completion rule, after promotion and export, requires all 17 lesson exits, a saved draft for all seven artifacts, and one final-practice submission. Success status remains unknown. If LMS save fails, direct the learner to copy or print current work before leaving.
