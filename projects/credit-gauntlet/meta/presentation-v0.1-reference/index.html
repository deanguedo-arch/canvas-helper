How this works

The model's boundaries

All amounts are CAD. Annual nominal rates are divided by twelve. Monthly interest and scheduled payments round HALF_UP to cents. There are no fees, penalties, insurance costs, new purchases, or later rate events.

The variable contract adjusts its payment to retain the original repayment date. The $200 reserve is not spent. Predictions and reflection are formative.
