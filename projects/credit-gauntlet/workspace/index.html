How to play

  1. Pick a term and compare both loan forecasts.
  2. Choose a loan, predict the payment split, then reveal it.
  3. Step through twelve payments and inspect the rate event.
  4. Keep all minimums covered and the $200 reserve separate.
  5. Choose avalanche or snowball. Play or step the repayment simulation.
  6. Compare the results, explain your choice and finish.

Optional choices and predictions give feedback, not a grade. Use Menu anytime; progress saves automatically.

The model's boundaries

All amounts are CAD. Annual nominal rates are divided by twelve. Monthly interest and scheduled payments round HALF_UP to cents. There are no fees, penalties, insurance costs, new purchases, or later rate events.

The variable contract adjusts its payment to retain the original repayment date. The $200 reserve is not spent. Predictions and reflection are formative.
