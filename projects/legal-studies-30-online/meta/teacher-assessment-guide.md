# Legal Studies 30 — Online: teacher assessment guide

Status: blocked review candidate, not a registration or learner-release approval.

## Assessment approach

Assess each CTS code separately against the current official outcomes once verified. The four-category reference rubric supplies achievement language, not a numeric conversion or category weighting. Do not use saved state, reading visits or self-check correctness as a course grade. Brightspace records the official mark and feedback. A local bundle title is not an official CTS registration.

For each submission: check authenticity and distinct learning; inspect the original product plus short response record; identify outcome-level evidence; use the reference levels and task descriptors below; give one specific strength and one actionable revision; record unresolved outcomes. An incomplete Portfolio does not prove absent external work, and a filled Portfolio does not prove mastery.

## Reference achievement language

### Knowledge & Understanding

- **Exceeding:** Demonstrates deep, insightful understanding of all concepts and terminology; consistently applies knowledge with outstanding accuracy and depth.
- **Meeting:** Clearly understands core concepts and processes; accurately applies knowledge with only minor or infrequent errors.
- **Approaching:** Shows a basic understanding of key concepts and processes; applies knowledge with some inaccuracies or gaps.
- **Far from approaching:** Shows minimal understanding of concepts; application of knowledge is frequently inaccurate or absent.

### Skills & Application

- **Exceeding:** Executes all required technical skills with exceptional precision and independence; consistently adapts skills to complex tasks and unique challenges.
- **Meeting:** Executes most technical skills effectively and accurately; applies skills consistently to complete tasks and solve routine problems.
- **Approaching:** Executes some required skills; applies skills to complete simple tasks but often needs guidance for complex problems.
- **Far from approaching:** Executes few required skills; application of skills is inconsistent and requires significant support to complete basic tasks.

### Communication & Professionalism

- **Exceeding:** Communicates clearly, engagingly, and effectively across all required formats; consistently displays outstanding professionalism, organization, and responsibility.
- **Meeting:** Communicates effectively and clearly in most situations; generally displays strong professionalism and responsibility.
- **Approaching:** Communicates ideas with some clarity; professionalism and responsibility are inconsistent and variable.
- **Far from approaching:** Communication is frequently unclear or incomplete; displays limited professionalism or responsibility.

### Creativity & Initiative

- **Exceeding:** Consistently demonstrates significant innovation, independent problem-solving, and takes initiative to extend learning far beyond expectations.
- **Meeting:** Demonstrates strong creativity and initiative; solves problems with minor support and extends learning on occasion.
- **Approaching:** Demonstrates some creativity and initiative but relies on support to solve problems and meet expected outcomes.
- **Far from approaching:** Demonstrates little creativity or initiative; rarely attempts problem-solving independently and requires direction.

## Delivery and moderation

Plan approximately 25 hours of access and purposeful work per CTS code: 6 instruction/research, 4 guided practice, 9 original practical work, 4 revision, 2 evaluation. These are planning allocations, not a timer or a certified sufficiency finding. Teacher review must confirm depth and all-outcome coverage; add teaching where reconciliation identifies gaps. No required purchase, contact, travel or partner. Allow accessible media with equivalent evidence, but do not replace an actual required performance with a written claim.

Projects require teacher-verified successful completion of eligible contributing CTS courses and an approved proposal before assessed project work. Reading and saving do not establish eligibility. Every project has a different product and process record; do not award multiple credits for the same learning. The browser leaves practice open and does not perform registration enforcement.

### Calibration example

The club's $200 refund demand is a position. Its need for usable, affordable space is an interest that a replacement-room offer might address.
