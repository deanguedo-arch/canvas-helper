How is economic globalization at work in your life?

Global economic connections play a big role in our daily lives. How many of the products you use are made or sold outside of Canada? Does your family income have any connection with the global economy? Goods and services created today are easily shipped from one country to another, and this gives many people and companies an opportunity to prosper.

Many of the items we buy every day are the products of transnational corporations. A transnational corporation is a corporation that operates in two or more countries. Most large transnationals operate in several countries and the largest of these have more money than many entire countries. For example, Wal-Mart is based in the United States, but it has stores all around the world. Many of the products sold in Wal-Mart are made in factories in China, where pay is much lower than in North America.

How does globalization affect political aspects of your life?

Globalization also has a big role to play in political aspects of your life. When events take place far away, they may have an impact on your life. For example, if Canada decides to go to war, this affects how Canadian soldiers and their families live. Their contributions may make Canada and the world a better, safer place. However, when tax dollars go to support that war, it means there is less money for other government programs. If the Canadian government chooses to take measures to protect the environment from climate change, there are costs associated with these actions. Of course, there are also costs associated with not acting to prevent climate change as well. The federal government may send aid to countries in need if there is a disaster. It may try to convince other countries to treat their citizens more equally. When global events occur, your local politicians may be forced to act. For example, your mayor and town or city council may have to make decisions about the environment. Your provincial government may find that it has to make deals with transnational oil companies to encourage economic growth and create jobs in Alberta. All of these decisions have an impact on you.

Tiananmen Square, G8 protests in Quebec City, 2004 tsunami (pictured), Afghanistan, Caledonia, Ontario and Hurricane Katrina are examples of issues that illustrate political globalization.

How does globalization affect social aspects of your life?

Social Globalization refers to global access of popular culture. Avril Lavigne recorded the song "Girlfriend", released in 2007 in French, Portuguese, German, Italian, Spanish, Japanese, and Mandarin.

Globalization has an impact on social aspects of your life. By looking at the historical background to globalization, you learned that your ancestors were connected with people from other parts of the world. Today you are even more connected. For instance, you can connect with people all around the world through the Internet, whether it’s through social software, gaming, or e-mail. You may have friends and family all over the world.

The nature of work also affects social aspects of your life. Many globalizing forces have an impact on who you are and how you interact with others. If people all over the world start wearing the same clothing, drinking the same kind of pop, and eating the same brand of food in the same style of restaurant, how would this affect how they live? How would their lifestyle affect who they are? You may not notice the impact of big companies such as Coke, Disney, and McDonald’s because they represent the mainstream culture of which you are a part. Think about their impact a little more. In your grandparents’ day, women usually worked in the home. Now most women work out of the home, partly due to global social changes. This affects how families live. In your parents’ day, chances are that families all ate together, cooking and eating food that grew nearby. Now that there are global shipping networks and cheap fast-food outlets, many families order takeout or go to fast-food restaurants, spending less time together. Globalization may be changing your lifestyle and the lifestyles of people all over the world in ways that you have not considered.

Environmental Globalization involves the ecological issues that concern the world. Individuals are responding to their immediate environment while keeping the consequences on a global scale in mind.
