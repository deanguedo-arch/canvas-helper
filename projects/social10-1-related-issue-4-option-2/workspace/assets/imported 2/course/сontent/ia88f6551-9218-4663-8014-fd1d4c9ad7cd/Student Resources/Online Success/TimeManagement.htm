Time Management

Do you want to be more effective at managing your time? Try the time management strategies and resources on this page.

10 Steps to help manage your time

Prioritize the Work

  • Focus on the tasks directly in front of you rather than worry about future due dates.
  • Make this course a priority this term.
  • Learn how to say "no" to friends or family or requests for extra hours at work when you have work to do in your course.
  • Consider limiting other demands on your time, just for this term.

Track Your Progress

  • Use a Checklist and reward yoruself by checking off assignments and tests completed.
  • Make a more specific daily or weekly Checklist (not just for assignments but also for completing various parts of the learning - eg. read one lesson per day; completed a learning activty; took notes on terms and concepts; posted to discussion forum; etc.)

Stay Organized

  • A tidy workspace can increase productivity and keep your mind focused (see Mind Map below)
  • Chunk down complex learning and tasks and take on each chunk one at a time.

Seek Help

  • You are not alone in this course.
  • Ask your teacher for help in your private discussion area.
  • Other students may also be able to help in the Student Help forum.
  • Student Services will be happy to provide additional time management strategies.

Take the Course with You

  • You can access the online learning anywhere you can connect to the internet.
  • You can access computers/internet at public libraries and at school.
  • Bring your laptop with you and work on the course in small chunks throughout the day.
  • Avoid mobile devices, like phones and iPad for accessing the course online. Some features of the course will not function properly in mobile devices.

Identify Your Time of Peak Productivity

  • What time of the day do you work best and most alert? Do the learning, assignments and tests during those periods

Sleep

  • Research has shown that a full night's sleep increases productivity and mental acuity.

Re-Evaluate

  • After trying different time management techniques, evaluate what works best for you or explore different approaches.
  • The Student Services department at your school can also help you with time management strategies.
