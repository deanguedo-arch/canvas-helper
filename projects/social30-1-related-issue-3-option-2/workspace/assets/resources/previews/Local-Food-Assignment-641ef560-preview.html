Local Food Assignment


Keep a list of all the food and drink you consume over the course of two days. Wherever you can, note the source of the products you consume. Hint: Packaged foods usually have the country of origin listed on the label or a contact number that you may call to find out more about the product you are consuming. Don't be scared to call because, as a consumer, you have the right to know about the food you eat.

Find a map of your region on the Internet to determine the area within a 160-kilometre radius of your home. (You may be able to find an online utility that will do this for you, and search for the term “100-mile diet.”)

Once you have your map, review your list and cross off any items that came from beyond the 160-km limit.
