Diploma Exam Prep Source Analysis Hints

Source Analysis

Value: 20%

Suggested time: 60 minutes

Task: Examine each source. Write a response in paragraph form in which you must:

  • interpret each source, explain the ideological perspective(s) presented in each source, and discuss the links between the principles of liberalism

AND

  • identify and explain one or more of the relationships that exist among the three sources.

Process:

  • Read all three sources in order to get an underlying theme of either political or economic.
  • Writing on the source booklet, break each source down independently by doing the following:
    • Highlight or underline key words that may support the ideology or need clarification or explanation.
    • Check credits/dates/titles/names for clues to the source and its background.
    • Record the ideological perspective of the source. Clarify whose perspective—the cartoonists, the authors?
    • Consider: What is this source about? What is it telling you? What other ideas might be associated with the beliefs or perspective in the source?
    • Record theory or individuals associated with that perspective.
    • Record how does this source and its ideas relate to liberalism. Be sure to be specific what aspects of liberalism—ie: economics: classical liberalism or modern liberalism—politics: liberal democracies and illiberalism. This could also include rejections of liberalism such as authoritarianism.
    • Consider any possible theory or Social Studies content that helps explain the source but be careful not to get bogged down in extraneous detail.
    • If one of the sources is a cartoon or photo, interpret the message and perspective of the source, do not simply describe the characters shown.
    • Be sure to: interpret, explain AND link to the concept of liberalism.
  • Once you have completed the above planning on the source booklet, record the information in paragraph form.
  • Once a paragraph is written for each of all three sources in this manner, write a paragraph showing relationships among the sources. (Compare and contrast all three sources.)
    • Are there commonalities between two or more of the sources?
    • What are the key differences?
    • How are all three related economically or politically?
    • How do they connect to the overall idea of liberalism?

Reminders:

  • The source analysis is NOT a position paper. You are not required to take a position on the perspectives presented.
  • If using direct words from a source, place in quotes in your writing. Keep this cribbing minimal. Do not make this the bulk of your paragraph—only use if to reinforce your explanation and interpretation.
  • It is very important to use Social Studies vocabulary but be sure to use it accurately. For example, illiberal is a term that can only be used when discussing political responses and rejections of liberal democracy.
  • This assignment is worth only 20% of the final diploma grade. Though taking time for planning is key, pacing is extremely important to allow appropriate time for Writing Assignment II.
