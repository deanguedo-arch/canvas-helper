## Critical Essay Studio ownership

- Canonical source: `workspace/index.html`, `workspace/styles.css`, `workspace/tokens.css`, `workspace/components.css`, `workspace/app/**`, `workspace/content/**`, and `workspace/schemas/**`.
- Browser bundle: `workspace/writing-studio.bundle.js`, regenerated by `npm run build:writing-studio`; never edit the bundle directly.
- The supplied handoff ZIP is the product reference. Its light Plan and Settings images guide presentation; its screenshots are not evidence of Brightspace saving.
- `workspace/schemas/deployment-config.json` remains `SYNTHETIC_TEST_ONLY`. A tenant-approved scope and live reopen tests are required before any production SCORM package.
- Product scope: a reusable Critical Essay Studio for Alberta English 30-1. Students bring their own assignment and studied literary text. Every stage is optional; drafting inside the tool is optional. Personal Response expansion is deferred.
- Primary learner navigation: Home, My Work, Feedback, Resources, Help, Settings. Project tabs remain Plan, Evidence, Draft, Revision, Notes. Assignment details and earlier project routes are preserved. Earlier teaching and saved practice remain accessible through Resources.
- `content/critical-essay.json` owns eight critical-essay sections, the complete original story and annotated essay, a second passage, alternative approaches, and five self-review categories. `app/critical.js` renders this teaching using the existing notebook coordinator. `meta/critical-essay-curriculum-map.json` owns outcome/source mappings. `content/lessons.json` retains 18 earlier explanations/models/thinking checks/guided practices. `content/analysis-lessons.json` owns nine concepts, two original practice passages and 18 worked analyses. `app/teaching.js` owns six-step personal and analytical response practice and help strategies.
- All models are original teaching examples; no fixed published work is selected for a learner. Transfer prompts accept the learner's own text/task. Personal connections can use knowledge, observation, or imagination without private disclosure.
- Keep teaching models labelled separately from learner-authored practice. Never automatically insert a model into an assignment draft.
- Preserve earlier project IDs, notes, drafts and backups. Learning practice is stored in the existing notes schema in a marked learning notebook; no state migration or automatic deletion is authorized.
- The old 12 guide cards and legacy nine-term/five-reading/90-example Explorer are retained as reference; old guide routes redirect to the full new lessons.
- JSON-rendered teaching content is canonical developer-editable source and runtime-owned Annotation only in Studio. Do not claim direct teacher editability for these sections without a supported adapter and reversible proof.
