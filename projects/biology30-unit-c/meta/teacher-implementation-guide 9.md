# Biology 30 — Unit C: Cell Division, Genetics and Molecular Biology — Teacher Implementation Guide

Candidate build: 13605571cc46b402b35ce5abc8af646f78cdfd5d2da899c275ca4e29cb21acd2

The course is independent-first and asynchronous. Required learning totals 2400 minutes; optional learning totals 480 minutes and never blocks completion. Learners complete 24 lesson exits, 12 artifact checkpoints, and one submission of the 40-item formative final practice.

All investigations provide safe local models or supplied-data paths. Classroom activities must not be treated as clinical, genetic, or ecological diagnosis. Practice remains non-graded; the secure Unit C test remains a separate native Brightspace activity.
