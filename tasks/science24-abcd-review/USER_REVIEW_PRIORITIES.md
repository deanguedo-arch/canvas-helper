# Teacher's stated review priorities

This summarizes the teacher's feedback during A–D construction. It is a **review target list**, not proof that a defect remains in the frozen package. Inspect the current candidates and mark each concern as resolved, partly resolved, still present or unverified.

- Match Biology 30 Pilot 3's clear navigation, typography, spacing, cards, focus, dialogs and saved-work communication while keeping Science 24's own content.
- Maintain the deeper lesson pattern: source-specific page guide and vocabulary, how/why explanations, two reasoned examples, five varied practice tasks, transfer and later retrieval, and a two-selection/two-written required check.
- Keep textbook reading links simple with **specific printed pages**. Provide a sizeable, movable original-page viewer with complete question context. Put optional workbook source work in Textbook Practice, not as long lesson-end blocks. Do not crowd the Textbook Library with a separate workbook self-check card.
- Make optional video cards resemble the stronger Unit A treatment: credible publisher, lesson purpose, viewing focus, written explanation and usable fallback without playback.
- Treat figures as instructional devices. The teacher rejected boring, redundant or poorly cropped illustrations, oversized/undersized images, a duplicated coal sequence, tiny labels and unbalanced columns. For each current figure ask what students must notice or do, how much visual space it deserves, whether surrounding text should wrap or stack, and whether desktop/phone views remain legible.
- Compare A, B, C and D directly. Earlier B and C drafts drifted from the A standard; D was requested as a one-pass improvement. Do not assume any unit is automatically the best. Derive the shared standard from the strongest current example for each component, then identify gaps unit by unit.
- Preserve original Brightspace learner-visible material and exact 2023 workbook keys as evidence; keep hidden test banks out of learner pages. Historical or questionable science and safety claims need explicit current-source correction, not silent rewriting of the original.
- Preserve stable saved-work meaning, old-draft histories, visible save failures and recovery. Optional media/practice must remain outside required completion. Keep teacher review, Studio, export and actual Brightspace certification distinct.
