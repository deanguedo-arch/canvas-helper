Lesson 1:
What is Art?

Art is not just the final brushstroke on a canvas. It is the curiosity that precedes it, the observation of light hitting a surface, and the translation of emotion into form. In this introductory lesson, we strip away the technicalities to ask: Why do we create?

We'll explore the philosophical roots of visual expression from Paleolithic cave paintings to contemporary digital installations, identifying the thread of human connection that remains unchanged.
